    Ejector and Grinder Pump Jobs We Handle in Elyria, OH

    Grinder Pump Installation in Elyria

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. They cost more. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Elyria

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Elyria

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. Audible alarms on their own power or Wi-Fi alarms that text you give warning while the pit is still filling, not after waste reaches the floor. Older cities with combined sewers can back up during big storms, so a backwater valve can safeguard a finished basement. Backup power can help.
