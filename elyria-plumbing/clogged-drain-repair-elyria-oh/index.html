    Kitchen Sink Clog Repair in Elyria

    Kitchen sinks clog from grease that cools and hardens on the pipe wall. Then food scraps and soap pile on.     Hydro-Jetting Services in Elyria, OH

    A jetter pushes water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and pulls itself down the pipe. The spray cleans the pipe all the way around. Cables punch a hole through a clog, but a jetter washes away the grease, scale and soft roots that cause the next clog to form. It works best on lines that clog again and again.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Clogs in Elyria

    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in Elyria

    Clearing one sink, tub or shower drain usually costs $125 to $300. Tougher clogs run more. A clog that needs a larger machine or access through a roof vent is typically $250 to $450, and jetting a heavily scaled or greasy line usually costs between $350 and $800. The price is set up front.

    Hydro jetting sends water at very high pressure through a nozzle that scrubs the pipe wall. It cleans, not just opens. We use it on lines with heavy grease, scale or roots that a cable cannot fully remove. We check pipe condition first.

    Much of Ohio has hard water, which leaves scale in water heaters, clogs aerators and shortens the life of appliances. Scale narrows the pipe and gives grease and hair something to cling to. Rusty pipe adds to it. Older homes may have cast iron that rusts and roughens inside, so each bit of debris catches on the wall and the clog builds faster than it would in smooth plastic pipe.

    Most single fixture clogs take 30 to 90 minutes. Some take longer. Lines with heavy buildup, poor access or a clog that has been treated with chemicals take more time because we have to work carefully. We tell you if it will run long.

    Enzyme cleaners can help. They are gentle. Used regularly, they slow the buildup of grease and soap. They will not clear a full clog, though.

    Water backing up into a different fixture. That is the big one. Gurgling, sewage odors, water around a floor drain and frequent clogs in several drains all point to trouble in the main line rather than a single trap. Those need prompt attention.

    Most sink and tub clogs take one to two hours. Often less than that. Main line clogs that need a camera inspection and jetting can take three or four hours or longer, depending on access to the cleanout and how much buildup is in the line. We give you a time estimate before we start.
