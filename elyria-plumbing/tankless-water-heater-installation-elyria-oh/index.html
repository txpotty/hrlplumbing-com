    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Elyria, OH

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install both condensing and non-condensing Rinnai models, and the right one depends on where the vent can go and how much you want to spend up front. The non-condensing ones need stainless vent pipe. Condensing models use PVC vent and a condensate drain.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Elyria, OH

    Businesses in Elyria, OH with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. If one unit goes down, the others keep running. We size the rack by peak demand. Expect a bigger gas line and a mechanical permit on commercial jobs.

    Rinnai, Navien and Noritz Installation in Elyria, OH

    We install Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem in Elyria, OH homes all year long. Rinnai brings range and parts support, Navien offers built-in recirc, Noritz makes compact condensing units for tight spots, and Rheem rounds out the list. Warranty terms vary. We walk you through them.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Elyria

    Propane homes in Elyria can run tankless units too, using a model built or converted for propane. Since propane runs at different pressure from natural gas, the regulator, line sizing and gas valve must all match, and the supplier may need to swap the tank regulator. A small tank can run short.

    Cost-Effective Tankless Conversions in Elyria, OH

    A tankless conversion in Elyria, OH costs more than a tank swap, mostly because of the gas line upgrade, the new venting and the unit price itself. The savings come back over time. With no standby loss and a lifespan that can reach 20 years, many homeowners see the difference on the gas bill within the first few winters. Your quote shows the math.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Elyria, OH

    Switching from a tank is more than a swap. A typical 40-gallon gas tank uses around 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the gas line from the meter almost always gets upsized. Venting changes too. We plan both on the quote.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Elyria, OH

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Homeowners around Elyria often install a softener to protect water heaters and reduce spotting on fixtures. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. Flush it yearly. Service valves make it easy.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Elyria, OH

    Replacing an old tankless unit tends to go faster than a conversion. The gas line is already sized and the vent path already exists, so in many homes it is a matter of matching the connections, checking the vent for damage and hanging the new unit. Vent sizes can change by brand. We confirm fit first.
