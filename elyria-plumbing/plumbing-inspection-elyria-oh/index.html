    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Elyria, OH

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve.     Water Heater Inspections in Elyria

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Elyria

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Elyria, OH

    A long heating season keeps water heaters and boilers working hard, so yearly maintenance helps avoid midwinter breakdowns. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. Outdoor faucets, exposed pipe, irrigation backflow devices and the heater drain and relief valve all get checked. We look at pipe insulation. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Elyria, OH

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location, and you keep a copy of the recording for your records or for a contractor.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Elyria

    Homeowners around Elyria often install a softener to protect water heaters and cut down on spotting on fixtures. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.
