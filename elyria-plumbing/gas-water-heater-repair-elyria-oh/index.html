    Propane Water Heater Repair in Elyria

    Propane and natural gas heaters look the same but burn at different pressures. Propane units need a matching orifice and a control set near 11 inches of water column. A manometer reading tells us the supply pressure. A bad conversion gets corrected with the right parts.
