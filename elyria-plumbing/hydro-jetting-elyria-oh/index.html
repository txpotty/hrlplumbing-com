    Jetting costs more on the first visit. The results hold up better. Several cable visits a year can easily cost more than one jetting job that keeps the line clear for a year or more, though a simple soft clog may only need a cable.

    Hydro Jetting vs. Snaking Cost Comparison in Elyria, OH

    Snaking costs less up front. Jetting usually holds up better. A cable punches through the blockage and leaves residue on the walls, which brings the clog back, while jetting cleans the whole pipe and cuts down the number of service calls over time.

    Recurring clogs follow a pattern. Plan ahead and they stop. Jetting once or twice a year keeps mineral deposits, grease and sludge from building up again, which avoids emergency blockages and extends the life of the plumbing system on properties with a history of trouble.
