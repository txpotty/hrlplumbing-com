    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Elyria

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. Leaking joints get taken apart, cleaned, resealed with gas-rated compound and tightened, and damaged fittings or valves get replaced. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Elyria

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Tree roots and shifting clay often crack older clay or cast iron sewer laterals, leading to slow drains and backups. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Elyria, OH

Damaged runs get spliced with the manufacturer's fittings, and we confirm the bond on the way out.
