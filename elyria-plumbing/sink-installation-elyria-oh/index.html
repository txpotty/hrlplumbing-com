    Bathroom Sink Installation in Elyria, OH

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. The faucet holes have to match the faucet you picked, whether that is a single hole, a 4 inch centerset or an 8 inch widespread, and the overflow has to line up with the pop-up drain. We check the spec sheet first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Elyria, OH

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. Backsplash comes last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Commercial Restroom Sink Installation in Elyria

    Public restroom lavatories take a beating. Wall-hung or countertop lavatories go on carriers with sensor faucets and mixing valves where tempered water is required. We can fit tamper-proof parts. Spec sheets go to your general contractor.

    Restaurant and Office Sink Installation in Elyria

    Breakroom and prep sinks get used hard. Stainless bowls with commercial faucets go in strong enough for a full bowl and a stack of dishes. Downtime gets planned around your hours. Night installs are possible.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Elyria

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. Silicone holds in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries a full bowl. The bowl stays braced until our return. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Elyria

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. A crooked reveal never goes away. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Elyria, OH

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Mounting the faucet and the pop-up on a sink that is still on sawhorses takes a fraction of the time and gets a tighter seal than working upside down inside the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Elyria, OH

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.
