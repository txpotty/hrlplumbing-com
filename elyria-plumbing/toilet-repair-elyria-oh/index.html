    What We Fix on Toilets in Elyria, OH

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Elyria

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. Hardware stores rarely stock those parts. We carry Flushmate cartridges and dual-flush seals for the common TOTO and American Standard models, and we check house pressure, since these units need steady line pressure to flush properly. Low line pressure gives you a weak, half-hearted flush.

    Flush Mechanism Repair in Elyria, OH

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties. We bring the water up to the line stamped on the overflow tube and adjust the chain to about half an inch of slack. Handles break too. A loose or cracked handle is a five-minute swap.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Elyria, OH

    A rocking bowl breaks the wax seal a little more every time someone sits down, and eventually the leak shows up as a stain or a soft spot. On slab floors the flange is set in concrete and can crack if the closet bolts are overtightened. We check it. A cracked flange gets a stainless repair ring or a new flange before the toilet goes back down.

    Toilet Leaking Through the Ceiling in Elyria, OH

    Water coming through the ceiling under an upstairs bathroom usually traces back to a failed wax ring or a cracked supply nut, and the drywall below holds the evidence. Leave that toilet alone until we look. We pull the bowl, check the flange and reset it on a new seal, then we show you how far the water traveled so you can decide about the ceiling.

    Toilet Flush Repair in Elyria, OH

    Two or three tries to get a full flush points to a flapper closing too early, a chain hooked in the wrong hole, scale in the rim jets, or a cracked overflow tube letting water bypass the bowl. Each one has a tell. We watch a full cycle with the lid off and replace the flush valve if the overflow tube is cracked, since it cannot be patched.

    Older Toilet Brands We Service in Elyria, OH

    Older toilets from Eljer, Briggs, Crane and Universal Rundle still turn up in homes built before the 1990s, and their flush valves often take parts nobody stocks anymore. We carry adapters for some of them. For the rest, we will let you know whether parts can still be found or if replacement is the honest answer.

    After-Hours Toilet Repair in Elyria, OH

The house main shutoff is your backup if the angle stop will not budge, and we can replace the stop with a quarter-turn valve once the water is off.
