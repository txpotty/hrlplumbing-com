    Noisy Disposal Repair in Elyria

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Elyria

    When a disposal fails, water can back into the sink and the dishwasher. With water rising or on the floor, turn off the disposal and dishwasher and call, because running them only adds water to the cabinet. We respond fast.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Elyria, OH

    Good grinding but slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We pull the trap, clear the tube and the arm with the right size cable, and check that the arm has at least a quarter inch of fall per foot toward the wall.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Elyria

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Then the object comes out with pliers. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Elyria, OH

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Most repairs use parts from the truck. Motors on these units are not sold separately.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Elyria, OH

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Three of the four are fixable. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Elyria

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Elyria, OH

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. That seal cannot be replaced.
