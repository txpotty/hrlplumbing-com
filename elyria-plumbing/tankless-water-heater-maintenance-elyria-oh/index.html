    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Elyria

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing.     Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Elyria

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. The trap gets cleaned and the media checked. We replace spent media. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Elyria

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. On a condensing unit the PVC or polypropylene vent should pitch back toward the heater. We check every joint and test for carbon monoxide near the unit. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Same-Day Tankless Help in Elyria, OH

    A blinking error code and a cold shower usually mean the heater protected itself from something. Usually it is a blocked exhaust or heavy scale. We bring common parts and a descaling kit on emergency calls, so most heaters are firing again before we pack up the truck.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Elyria, OH

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Elyria

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is your heat exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. A 45 to 60 minute circulation with descaling solution dissolves it.
