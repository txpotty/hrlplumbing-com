    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Elyria, OH

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized for the inflow, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. A missing check valve lets the discharge line drain back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Elyria, OH

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. A long heating season keeps water heaters and boilers working hard, so annual maintenance helps avoid midwinter breakdowns. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Drainage System Integration in Elyria, OH

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Exterior footing drains, interior drain tile and surface French drains all do the same job at different depths.     Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Elyria

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests.     Interior systems manage water after it has come through the wall, using drain tile under the slab edge and a sump pump.     Done right with good materials, a waterproofing system lasts 20 to 50 years. The range is wide since soil movement, groundwater levels and how well it was installed all play a part, and a crack that opens later can let water around even a good membrane. Drain tile can silt up over time.
