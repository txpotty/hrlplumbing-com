    Main Water Line Repair in Elyria

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. When it fails you tend to see wet ground, a spinning meter and weak pressure at every tap. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. If it still moves, the leak is buried. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, so the crew opens one small pit rather than trenching the whole run.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Elyria

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Elyria

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. Then the new pipe gets pulled through underground. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, and most jobs wrap up in a single day.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Elyria

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, either along the old route or through fresh soil next to it. Most replacements finish in a day. You also get the manufacturer warranty on the new pipe.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Elyria

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Ohio has a lot of older housing, and many homes still have galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains that are wearing out. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. You get a price on every option.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Elyria, OH

    Besides repairs, HRL Plumbing handles service line upgrades when a commercial tenant needs more flow, for example when a restaurant adds fixtures or a gym adds showers. Bigger meters need bigger pipe. We size it to the fixture load.     PEX Water Line Repair in Elyria, OH

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and pipe left in the sun before it was buried can get brittle. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.
