    Water Softener Maintenance Services We Do in Elyria, OH

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Elyria

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. Between those, the salt level, brine well and float all get a look. It takes about an hour on most homes.

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Elyria, OH

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We advance it manually through each cycle and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. A worn piston is a common find.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Elyria

    Resin does the actual softening. Each bead trades sodium for calcium and magnesium until it runs out of capacity. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to tell whether the bed is still at full strength.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Elyria, OH

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks around the brine well and overflow fitting and refill with fresh salt.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Elyria

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. If it hits a hard layer and then falls through, there is a bridge. We break the crust, vacuum out the salt mush below it, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Repair or Replace a Softener in Elyria, OH

    New softener systems often run $1,200 to $3,500 installed, while a resin rebed usually lands between $300 and $700. Sound tanks and valves are worth keeping. We will tell you plainly to replace it when the valve is obsolete and parts are gone, or when the tank itself is cracked.

    Full-System Softener Inspections in Elyria

    An inspection covers more than the two tanks. We trace the inlet and outlet plumbing, the bypass, the drain line and the brine overflow. Green crust on copper fittings, a slow leak at the bypass or a drain line jammed down into a standpipe all go on the written report.
