    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Toledo

    Our trucks carry ground microphones and correlators, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Each tool has a job. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, and tracer gas finds leaks that make no sound at all on plastic pipe.

    Leak Testing for Multifamily and Offices in Toledo

    Multifamily buildings and office parks have long supply systems that let leaks hide for months. We isolate each unit or floor, test it and trace leaks with the same acoustic and thermal tools we use in single-family houses. Every unit gets its own report. Early detection means fewer tenant complaints and fewer insurance claims building-wide.

    Leaks in Walls and Ceilings in Toledo, OH

    A brown ring on the ceiling under a bathroom usually points to a shower valve, a drain joint or a toilet wax ring. Our job is figuring out which. Thermal cameras show the cool wet area, a moisture meter confirms it and a borescope through a small hole lets us see the actual pipe without opening the whole ceiling.

    Commercial Building Leaks in Toledo, OH

    Restaurants, offices and apartment complexes in Toledo pay for every hour a leak keeps running. Sprinkler lines, cooling tower makeup water and long copper mains are common sources, and a leak in any of them can run under a floor or above a drop ceiling for weeks. Each zone gets isolated and tested, then mark leaks for repair. Most tenants never see us.

    Fixture and Appliance Leaks in Toledo, OH

    A silent toilet leak can lose hundreds of gallons a day. Worn faucet cartridges, softener valves and water heater connectors add more. We dye test tanks, check flappers and fill valves, and inspect supply lines and water heater fittings, with most of those fixed in under an hour from truck stock. Cheap fixes, real savings.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Toledo, OH

    A yearly leak check catches small problems early. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves. Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors get the most out of this, because those parts often fail together. Short visit, big savings.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leaks in Toledo

    Bubbling paint, soft drywall and a musty smell are the signs we hear about most. All of them mean water. Thermal cameras find the wet area, and a moisture meter confirms it. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line, and once the source is pinned down we open one small access hole rather than following the stain around the room.
