    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Toledo, OH

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Toledo, OH

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue.     Hot Water Pressure Problems in Toledo, OH

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead.     Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Toledo

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. Fresh thread tape fixes it. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Toledo

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. A vinegar soak can work. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Toledo

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. A new cartridge fixes it. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Toledo, OH

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. Cleaning often works. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Toledo

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. Over time the cartridge or spool sticks, and you get a shower that will not stay at one temperature or a handle that turns past where it should before the water warms up. We replace the cartridge. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.
