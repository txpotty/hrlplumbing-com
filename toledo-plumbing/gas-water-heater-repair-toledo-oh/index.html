    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Toledo, OH

    Honeywell and White-Rodgers valves report faults with a blinking LED, and the flash count matches a chart on the label. We read the code, then test the thermopile and sensors before blaming the valve. Valves do fail. When the control itself is the problem, we replace it with an exact-match valve or a listed universal model, set the temperature and leak test every joint on the gas train before relighting. We leave the old valve with you if you ask.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Toledo

    Rumbling or popping from the bottom of the tank is water boiling under a layer of sediment. Hard water speeds that up, and so does a tank that has never been flushed. We drain the tank through the bottom valve, stir up the settled scale with short bursts of cold water and keep going until it runs clear, which on a neglected tank can take a while. Whistling points somewhere else. That usually means a relief valve or a partly closed supply valve.

    Burner Tray and Venting Repair in Toledo

    Rust scale on the burner tray is a sign the flue baffle or the tank top is shedding. The burner comes out, the ports get cleared and the flame arrestor gets a look. Then the vent gets checked from the draft hood to the roof, because a flue that is loose, sagging or capped by a bird nest can push carbon monoxide back into your Toledo home. A smoke pencil shows the draft.

    Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Toledo

    A pilot that lights, holds for ten seconds and then goes out is almost always a thermocouple or a weak magnet in the gas valve. No flame at all usually means a plugged pilot orifice or a bad piezo. Burner trouble looks different. The pilot stays lit but the main flame never comes on, which points to the thermostat side of the valve, a tripped thermal cutoff or a flame arrestor so clogged with lint that the burner starves for air. Each part gets tested in that sequence.

    Repair or Replace: Straight Answers in Toledo, OH

    Tank Leaks and Anode Rods in Toledo

    Corrosion starts at the anode rod and works its way into the steel once the rod is spent. Private wells serve many rural homes, and iron, sulfur and hardness are common issues that call for filtration or softening. We pull the anode, check how much magnesium or aluminum is left and replace it if the core wire is showing. A fresh anode, a drained tank and a relief valve that seats properly can add years to a heater in a Toledo home that has not started leaking from the shell. Water from the shell seam is another story. That tank needs replacing.

    Repairing Older Gas Water Heaters in Toledo

    Older tanks with standing pilots and no vapor sensor are still out there in Toledo, mostly in basements and utility closets. Parts for them are simpler, and a universal thermocouple fits most.     Propane Water Heater Repair in Toledo

    Propane and natural gas heaters look the same but burn at different pressures. Propane units need a matching orifice and a control set near 11 inches of water column. Natural gas runs closer to 7 inches, so a heater converted badly, or a regulator that drifted, gives you soot, a yellow flame and a pilot that keeps going out for no obvious reason. A manometer reading tells us the supply pressure. Wrong parts get swapped for the right ones.
