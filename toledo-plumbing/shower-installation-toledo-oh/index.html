    Curbless Shower Installation in Toledo

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. A linear drain often works best. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Shower Pan Installation in Toledo

    Shower pans fail quietly. Pinholes and loose drain clamps let water into the subfloor slowly, which is why tiled pans sit flooded for a full day before tile. We level prefab pans carefully. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Toledo

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Acrylic and steel tubs get mortar or foam underneath, while cast iron rests on a level ledger, which keeps the caulk line intact. The valve goes in next. Spout and head go at the heights you choose, and the overflow gasket gets a water test.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Toledo

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. The circuit is an electrician's job. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

    Barrier-Free Walk-In Showers in Toledo, OH

    Barrier-free entries need careful drain placement. We work with your tile setter on the waterproofing membrane and the slope, since water has to reach the drain before it can roll out the open side of the shower.     Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Toledo

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. We check the heater. A thermostatic valve manages each outlet.

    Digital Shower Systems in Toledo, OH

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. These valves usually sit in a nearby closet or vanity and need low-voltage wiring to the controller and an outlet close by. The outlet is an electrician's job. Our part is the plumbing.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Toledo, OH

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. A standard pressure-balance valve can feed one head, but rain heads, body jets and handhelds need a thermostatic valve with separate volume controls and supply lines sized up to 3/4 inch. We plan the layout first. Then we check whether the water heater can keep up.
