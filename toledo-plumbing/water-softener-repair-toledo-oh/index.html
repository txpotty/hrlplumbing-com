    Resin Bed Replacement in Toledo

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. HRL Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. The softener works like new.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Toledo, OH

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Keep the salt about half full.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Toledo, OH

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. HRL Plumbing repairs these units in Toledo, OH. The media wears out in a few years.
