    Waterless Urinal Installation in Toledo

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Toledo, OH

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. A low-flow bowl and matching valve on the existing carrier cut water use sharply. Some utilities offer rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Toledo

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. The flush handle has to be easy to reach. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Home Gym and Garage Urinals in Toledo, OH

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. The drain ties to the nearest stack, a vent or AAV goes in where allowed, and a compact bowl fits the space. Freeze protection matters in garages. Ask us about the layout.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Toledo, OH

    New urinals usually need a permit. Adding a fixture means new drain, vent and supply work that most cities inspect at rough-in and again at final, and commercial jobs often need plans that show fixture counts and accessible layouts. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Toledo, OH

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. Reusing a carrier and lines is cheap, while new carriers and pipe runs through finished walls cost more. Touchless valves cost more than manual. We itemize each piece.
