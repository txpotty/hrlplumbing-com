    Common Faucet Repairs in Toledo, OH

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Toledo, OH

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Both are quick fixes. Cartridges get matched for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Toledo

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Toledo

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. A new faucet can usually go in on the same visit, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Leaky Faucet Repair in Toledo

    Most drips come from the part that seals the water inside the faucet, which is a rubber washer on older two-handle faucets and a cartridge or ceramic disc on newer ones. First we identify which type of valve you have. It matters. We carry the common washers, seats and cartridges, so the fix usually happens on the first trip.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Toledo, OH

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house. We carry commercial cartridges. We set metering faucets to run long enough for a full rinse without wasting water, and we can work after hours.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Toledo

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. We replace what failed and check the rest. Compression, cartridge, ball and disc faucets all fail differently, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Toledo

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Toledo

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. It happens faster with hard water. The valve body gets cleaned while the old part is out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Local Faucet Repair in Toledo, OH

    Local plumbers know the faucets that show up most in Toledo homes, from builder-grade single-handle kitchen faucets to older two-handle bath faucets with compression stems. That shapes what we carry. Very few repairs need a special order.

    Close by matters most when a faucet will not shut off, since water keeps running until someone closes the stops under the sink. Active leaks go first. Dispatch keeps someone nearby in most of Toledo.
