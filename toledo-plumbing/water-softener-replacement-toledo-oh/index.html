    Softener Systems We Install in Toledo, OH

    Softener Loop Installation in Toledo, OH

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Toledo

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. We replace the media or the whole system, depending on age. Installed to spec, the warranty stays valid.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Toledo

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Softener Replacement on a Budget in Toledo, OH

    Capacity and valve brand set most of the price. A basic 32,000-grain metered unit costs far less than a twin-tank Kinetico, and for a small household on moderately hard water it may be all you need. We show both options side by side.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Toledo, OH

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. We typically leave the hose bibs on hard water. Why soften water for the lawn?

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Toledo, OH

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. A TAC conditioner is the honest alternative there, and we tell people plainly that it prevents scale but will not give them the slick feel of soft water. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Toledo, OH

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Toledo, OH

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Ohio homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.
