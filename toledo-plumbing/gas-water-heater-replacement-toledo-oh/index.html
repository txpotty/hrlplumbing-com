    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Toledo

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Ohio homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Current tanks are often taller and wider. We measure the closet before ordering. Plan on about two to three hours.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Service in Toledo, OH

    Higher BTU inputs on commercial units mean bigger gas lines and larger vent sizes, plus a permit and inspection in most jurisdictions. That is all part of the job. We keep your downtime short.

    No Hot Water Calls in Toledo

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. We check those first. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Toledo

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Ohio plumbers install them for that reason. Parts are easy to get. We follow their venting and clearance specs to the letter, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Toledo

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. With a check or regulator on the main, expanding hot water has nowhere to go and the pressure spikes each heating cycle. Many codes require a thermal expansion tank there. We precharge it to your house pressure.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Toledo, OH

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Code is stricter here. Sizing comes from peak demand and recovery rate.

    Emergency Gas Water Heater Replacement in Toledo, OH

    Water on the floor around the tank usually means the inner tank has rusted through, and that is not a repair. Shut the cold supply valve on top and turn the gas control to off. After that, give us a call. We carry 40 and 50 gallon tanks and can usually have hot water back the same day.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Toledo, OH

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.
