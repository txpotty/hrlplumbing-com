    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Toledo, OH

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It is often the cheapest fix. A small opening gets cut, the pipe is exposed and repaired, the line is tested, and the hole is backfilled in layers and patched level. Your flooring contractor can finish the surface afterward.

    Leak Detection Tools We Use in Toledo, OH

    Infrared cameras, acoustic listening gear and tracer gas cover most leaks we chase in Toledo, OH. Tracer gas helps on tiny leaks. A hydrogen and nitrogen blend goes into the empty pipe, and a sniffer picks it up where it rises through the slab, even when the leak is silent.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Toledo, OH

    Crawl spaces hide leaks from supply lines, drains and AC condensate lines. Sagging, wet insulation is a common clue. Standing water under a house rots sills and joists and grows mold on the subfloor, so we repair the source and look at the vapor barrier while we are down there.
