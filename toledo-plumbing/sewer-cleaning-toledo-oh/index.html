    Main Line and Sewer Cleaning in Toledo, OH

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Toledo

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Commercial Sewer Cleaning in Toledo, OH

    Commercial lines take a beating. Restaurants fight grease every day. In offices and shops, paper and odd objects flushed in restrooms are the usual cause of a stopped lateral, and our heavy jetters handle 6 inch commercial pipe without much trouble.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Toledo

    Household sewers clog slowly. Grease, hair and wipes build up over months. Each flush adds a little more to the pile at a rough joint or a low spot, until the 4 inch line is down to a trickle and the lowest drain in the house starts to back up, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Toledo, OH

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. One pipe, many fixtures. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.

    Root Masses in Toledo Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Roots find the joints. Our root cutting heads attach to the sectional cable and saw through the mass in passes, and a camera run afterward shows how much growth is left and where the roots are coming in.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Toledo

    No cleanout means pulling a toilet. It makes every call slower. We can install a two-way cleanout near the foundation so the line can be cleaned in either direction, which makes every future cleaning and camera inspection faster and cheaper, and the work is done to local code with any required permit.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Toledo, OH

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Toledo

    Trees send roots toward any moisture they can find. A sewer joint is a steady source. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out, then follow with a jetter if fine root hairs are left behind, and a camera shows where roots are entering so you can choose between lining and repair.
