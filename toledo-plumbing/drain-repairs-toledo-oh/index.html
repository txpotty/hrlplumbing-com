    Storm Drain Repair in Toledo, OH

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Sump pumps work hard during wet seasons, and a battery backup keeps them running through storm-related power outages. Debris and silt fill them up. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and replace damaged corrugated or PVC pipe so water flows to the outlet instead of pooling against the house. A clean outlet matters too.

    Laundry and Floor Drain Repair in Toledo

    A washer dumps a lot of water fast. Undersized drains spill over. We correct standpipe height, replace cracked laundry box drains and add venting where it is missing, and for garage and utility floor drains we restore the trap seal so sewer odor stays in the pipe. We fix primers that keep those seals full.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Toledo, OH

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. Fine soil plugs it over time. Tree roots and shifting clay often crack older clay or cast iron sewer laterals, leading to slow drains and backups. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, so we camera the tile, find the failed section and replace it with perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric and clean gravel. The outlet gets checked as well.

    Drain Vent Repair in Toledo, OH

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. Air cannot get in behind the water. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, which is why we inspect the roof opening and the vent piping inside the walls. Clearing it is often quick.

    Drain Repair Cost Comparison in Toledo, OH

    A trap or slip-joint repair is one of the cheapest plumbing jobs. Often under an hour. Replacing a section of branch drain in an open wall or crawlspace costs more but is still a modest repair, while opening finished walls or replacing a full cast iron stack is the most involved option on the list. You get every option with a number.

    Restaurant and Office Drain Repair in Toledo

    Restaurants, salons, offices and medical suites all need working drains during business hours. Grease is the big one.     Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Toledo, OH

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. Full stack replacement gets a separate quote.
