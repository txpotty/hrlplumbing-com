    Type L vs. Type K Copper in Parma, OH

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is lighter and mostly goes indoors. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against corrosive soil and stray rocks. Same fittings, different pipe.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Parma

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? A repair coupling is often the right call. Three leaks on a galvanized line from the 1950s is different, because the pipe is thin everywhere and the next leak is already forming, so the full line costs less than a string of repairs. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Parma, OH

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Disconnecting garden hoses and shutting off outdoor spigots before winter avoids many burst pipe calls. A frozen PEX line often survives where copper would crack. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and we keep it out of direct sun at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Water Line Materials We Install in Parma, OH

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each has a place. All of it is listed for drinking water, carrying the NSF 61 listing.
