    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Parma

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used. We retest hardness and reprogram the valve, or upgrade the head to a metered control that only regenerates when the resin is actually close to exhausted. Salt bills drop.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Parma

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. HRL Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Parma, OH

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. We break up and remove the bridge and clean the tank. Cracked brine tanks on older units get replaced. Fill the tank halfway.

    Hard Water Symptoms and Diagnostics in Parma

    Scale on faucets means trouble. HRL Plumbing checks hardness at the tap, inspects every component and gives you a repair plan for your Parma home. We test hardness before and after the repair, so you see the grains per gallon fall.

    Control Valve Repair in Parma

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. HRL Plumbing technicians rebuild these valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Mechanical or electronic, we fix the timing. Common parts are on the truck, so most valve repairs are finished the same day without a second trip to the supply house.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Parma, OH

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. HRL Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. We lay out both options.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Parma, OH

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Parma, OH

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. O-rings and fittings get replaced and pressure tested. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.
