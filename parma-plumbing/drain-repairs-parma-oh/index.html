    Most trap and fixture drain repairs take one to two hours. Wall work takes longer. Replacing drain pipe inside a wall or under a floor usually runs half a day or more with access setting the pace. The estimate includes the timeline.

    Unclogging a drain does not need one. Larger work often does. Replacing a stack, relocating a drain or replacing a section of the building drain in Parma may require a plumbing permit, depending on local rules and how much of the system is being changed. The permit is on us if needed.

    Repeat clogs come from something in the pipe catching debris. Usually it is buildup. Poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a cracked fitting with a rough edge catches debris over and over, even after the line is cleared. A camera shows which one you have.

    Age and corrosion lead the list. Soil movement is close behind. Tree roots, temperature swings, heavy loads over buried pipe, plus bad installation such as skipped primer or unsupported runs, can all break a drain early. Most failures have more than one cause.

    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. The whole inside gets cleaned. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, but a jet nozzle strips buildup off the entire pipe wall. Fragile pipe gets checked before jetting.
