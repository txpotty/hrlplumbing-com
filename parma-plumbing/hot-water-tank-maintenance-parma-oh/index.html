    Anode Rod Replacement in Parma, OH

    Water chemistry changes from one street to the next, and so does anode life. Most rods last three to five years. A softened water supply can chew through a magnesium rod in two. A new rod costs a fraction of a tank and often adds years.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Parma

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. Hard water makes it more urgent. We drain, partly refill and drain again until the hose runs clear, sometimes four or five rounds. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Parma, OH

    Garage and attic tanks live through bigger temperature swings than closet units, which wears out plastic drain valves and wiring insulation faster. A pan under an attic tank is not optional. We confirm the pan drain line is open and runs to a safe spot. Gas tanks in garages need their ignition source at least 18 inches off the floor unless they are FVIR models.

    Electric Element Check in Parma

    Electric tanks have two elements, usually 4,500 watts at 240 volts, and the lower one does most of the work and takes most of the scale. We test each one with a meter. A failed lower element shows up as hot water that runs out halfway through a shower. We replace it with the matching wattage and voltage.

    Relief Valve Testing in Parma

    Temperature and pressure relief valves on residential tanks are typically rated to open at 150 psi or 210 degrees, and they can stick shut after years of mineral buildup. Our tech lifts the lever, lets water flow, then watches it close. A valve that drips afterward gets replaced. We also confirm the discharge pipe runs full size to within six inches of the floor or to an approved drain, which is what plumbing codes ask for.

    Anode Upgrades for Odor in Parma, OH

    A rotten egg smell from the hot side only is almost always sulfate-reducing bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. Swapping the rod usually fixes it. We replace it with an aluminum-zinc rod or a powered anode, then chlorinate the tank to kill the bacteria living in it. Powered anodes never wear out and draw very little electricity.

    Electric Thermostat Service in Parma

    Each element on an electric tank has its own thermostat, and the upper one carries a reset button for the high-limit switch. A tripped reset is a symptom. We trace the cause, often a shorted element or a stuck thermostat, before resetting it. Power stays off at the breaker the whole time.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Parma

    Street pressure over 80 psi is hard on every fixture in the house, and plumbing codes generally call for a pressure reducing valve above that number. A gauge on a hose bib tells us the static pressure. Then we lift the relief valve lever. If the valve opens and reseats cleanly and the expansion tank holds its charge, the tank is safe from pressure spikes.
