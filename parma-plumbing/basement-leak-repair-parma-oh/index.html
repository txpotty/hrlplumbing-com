    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in Parma

    Price depends on the source. A supply line or fitting repair usually runs a few hundred dollars.     Simple pipe repairs and crack injection usually do not need a permit. Bigger jobs often do. Replacing a drain stack, installing a new sump with a discharge line, adding a backwater valve, or excavating near the foundation may require a permit, and we pull it when it is needed.

    Gutters and downspouts do most of the work. Clear them and extend the outlets away from the walls. Inside, pour a bucket of water into the sump pit every spring to make sure the pump kicks on, check the water heater and washer hoses each year, and replace rubber washer hoses with braided steel ones every five years or so.

    Tightening a loose fitting or replacing a washer hose is a reasonable DIY task. Cast iron stack cracks are another matter. Slab leaks and sump wiring need a pro, since a mistake can flood the floor or create a shock risk.

    Heavy storms dump a lot of water quickly, and the soil around the foundation cannot absorb it fast enough. Heavy spring rain is a major plumbing stress in Ohio, raising water tables and pushing water into basements. Saturated soil pushes water against the walls and up under the slab through the cove joint, so the leak shows only when the ground is soaked. It disappears when the ground dries.

    For sump pumps we mostly install Zoeller and Liberty, with Wayne for some battery backups. Cast iron housings outlast plastic ones. For crack injection we use two-part polyurethane or epoxy kits from established commercial suppliers, and for interior drains we use perforated pipe with a dimpled wall membrane from the same manufacturer so the pieces fit together.

    Workmanship on pipe repairs is warranted for the term on your invoice. New pumps carry their own coverage. For seepage work, a return leak at the same spot during the warranty gets fixed at no labor charge.

    Leak repair stops a specific source, like a cracked pipe or a leaking wall crack. Waterproofing covers the whole wall.
