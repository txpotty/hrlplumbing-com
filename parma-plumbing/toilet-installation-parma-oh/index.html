    Floor-Mount and Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Parma, OH

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Parma, OH

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. Floors stay clear underneath. Cleaning gets easier, and the bowl height can be set anywhere within the carrier range, useful for a tall family. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Parma

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Even tightening, alternating sides, matters more than force, since uneven pressure either cracks the china or leaves one bolt weeping. It should not wobble.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Parma, OH

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. Suddenly the old flange is below the floor. Either an extender ring brings the flange up or a new flange goes in, and relocating a toilet on a slab means saw-cutting concrete to move the drain. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Parma

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. After scraping the old wax off, we check the flange for cracks, set new stainless bolts, and lower the new toilet straight down onto a fresh seal so it compresses evenly. The old one leaves with us.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Parma

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. A frame set wrong puts the bowl too low or too high, and once tile is up there is no adjusting it. Measure twice here.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Parma

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. Before suggesting one, we put a gauge on a hose bib, and we mention the noise, because the flush carries through the whole house. They rarely clog.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Parma, OH

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    Customer-Supplied Toilet Installation in Parma, OH

    Bought the toilet yourself? First, look at the rough-in printed on the carton. Before the old toilet comes out, we inspect the new one for shipping cracks, because cracked china goes back to the store, not onto your flange. We bring the seal and stainless bolts.

    Adding or moving a toilet almost always needs one. Swapping in the same place usually does not. An inspector checks the new drain and vent while things are still open, and we book that visit around your remodel schedule.

    We guarantee the workmanship on every toilet we set. A leak from our work gets fixed free. The seal and supply joint are on us, and factory defects go to the manufacturer, with our help on the claim. Hang on to your receipt.

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the toilet drain. Most homes measure 12 inches. You will also see 10 and 14 inch rough-ins, and buying the wrong one means the toilet will not fit or sits well off the wall. Measure from the wall itself, not the baseboard.

    It is a toilet with the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly chair height, compared with 15 inches or so on a standard bowl. Knees and hips usually like it.
