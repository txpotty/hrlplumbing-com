    Well Pump & Pressure Tank Replacement in Dayton

    Rural homes around Dayton that run on a well go dry the moment the pump stops.     Sump Pump and Well Pump Replacement in Dayton, OH

    A dead sump pump during a storm floods a basement fast, and a dead well pump means no water at all. In Dayton, OH, sump pumps get sized for inflow with a check valve and an optional battery backup, while well pumps get matched to depth, flow and switch settings. We run each one through cycles.

    Pipe Replacement & Whole-Home Repiping in Dayton, OH

    Homes in Dayton, OH with galvanized steel or polybutylene usually show it the same ways: low pressure, rusty water and leaks that keep coming back. Repiping swaps every supply line from the main to each fixture for PEX or copper, usually in two to four days with water restored most nights. Walls get patched after.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Dayton, OH

    Switching to tankless during a replacement is common in Dayton, OH, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually needs to be upsized, and the vent changes from metal B-vent to stainless or PVC. Both are on the quote.

    Gas Line Replacement in Dayton

    Old black iron or corroded gas lines in Dayton homes can leak at threaded joints, and a gas leak is not something to wait on. If you smell gas, leave the house, then call the gas utility from outside, and once the line is safe, we replace the damaged section or the whole run, pressure test it and relight every appliance. Testing comes before relight.

    Repair vs Replacement Cost Comparison in Dayton, OH

    A common rule of thumb says replace once a repair passes half the cost of new, or when the part is near the end of its life.     Pipe Replacement and Repiping in Dayton

    Corroded, leaking or galvanized pipe in Dayton can stain water and cause costly damage.
