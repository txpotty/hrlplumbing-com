    Water Softener Installation in Dayton, OH

    Homeowners around Dayton often install a softener to protect water heaters and reduce spotting on fixtures. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. We test hardness in grains per gallon at the kitchen tap, then size a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner to your household use. The bypass valve matters. With it, the softener can be serviced while the house still has water.

    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Installs in Dayton

    Bradford White and Rheem both make good tanks, and the brand matters less than the fit. Demand, available space and the cost of gas versus electric at your address decide the model we recommend. Then we check the Ohio code items. Closed systems, the kind with a check valve or PRV at the meter, usually need an expansion tank, which we install alongside the heater.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Dayton, OH

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. The fix is a sealed basin with an ejector pump that lifts waste to the main. Pump and basin are sized to the fixtures, the basin is vented to code, and the discharge gets a check valve and a shutoff so the pump can come out for service. Brands we trust here are Liberty and Zoeller.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Dayton

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. A PEX repipe ends that cycle. We run either a manifold with home runs to each fixture, which balances pressure, or a trunk-and-branch layout when the walls make home runs impractical. Access holes are kept small and patched, or left square and clean for your drywall contractor.

    Gas Line Installation for New Appliances in Dayton, OH

    Water heaters, ranges, dryers, fireplaces and pool heaters all pull from the same gas service. We add up the BTU demand of everything on the line, measure the run, and size each branch so the furthest appliance still gets full pressure. New lines get pressure tested, always. A load that outgrows the meter needs an upgrade, which we coordinate with the gas company.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Dayton, OH

    Navien and Rinnai tankless units hang on a wall and free up the floor space a tank used to take. Gas models usually need a bigger gas line and new stainless or PVC venting. Electric models need amperage. We size by gallons per minute and the temperature rise from your incoming water. Cold winters and hot, humid summers give Ohio plumbing a wide range of seasonal stress from one year to the next. Every unit gets isolation valves so it can be flushed.

    Homeowners around Dayton often install a softener to protect water heaters and reduce spotting on fixtures.
