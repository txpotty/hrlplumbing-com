    Which Sump Pump Suits Your Dayton, OH Home

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Dayton, OH

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. Narrow pits, under about 12 inches across, are where they make the most sense.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Dayton, OH

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Pump not keeping up? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Dayton, OH

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We match the old pump or step it up. Grinder and ejector pumps are a separate job.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Dayton

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. We pull the old unit, scoop the gravel and silt out of the basin, and set the new pump on a flat brick so it does not suck up debris. Then a new check valve goes on. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Dayton, OH

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We swap in a corrosion-resistant pump and clear out leaves and silt. We route the outlet away from the house. A bucket test every spring catches problems early.

    Commercial Sump Pump Service in Dayton

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. Both pumps and the alternator get swapped together. Work can be scheduled early or after closing so nobody is stepping around an open basin.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Dayton, OH

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They suit pits too tight for two separate pumps. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Dayton

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. That is not a sump pump job. The ejector and check valve get replaced, then we reseal the lid and check the vent connection. A bad seal shows up as sewer odor.

    Sump Pump Replacement Near You in Dayton, OH

    Search for a sump pump plumber near you in Dayton, OH, and you will find plenty of names, but fewer that stock the pump on the truck. Our vans usually have 1/3 and 1/2 HP cast iron pumps on board. In most cases that makes it a same-day job.

    Groundwater levels shift with the seasons, and in a wet spring a pit can take on water every few minutes even with no rain in the forecast. We size the pump for that worst week. Average days do not flood basements.
