    Water Heater and Softener Leaks in Dayton

    A softener stuck in regeneration sends water down the drain nonstop. That shows up on the bill. Water heaters leak at fittings, drain valves and relief valves, while a dripping relief valve usually points to high pressure. We check both units on every visit.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Dayton

    Drip and sprinkler lines cover the yard underground, and they hide a lot of water loss. Stuck valves are common. We isolate the irrigation system from the house, run each zone and trace leaking lines, since a cracked lateral or broken emitter can lose thousands of gallons unnoticed.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leaks in Dayton, OH

    Stained drywall, peeling paint and a musty smell usually mean water inside the wall. Shower valves cause many of these.
