    Below-Grade Pipe Repairs in Dayton, OH

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Dayton

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It fails without warning. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, with failures most common at fittings and tight bends. Patching it just moves the next leak. We use PEX or copper.

    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Dayton

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene splits instead. If your buried service is either one, patches are usually temporary, so we replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. You will notice the pressure.

    Main Water Line Repair in Dayton

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house under constant pressure. Small leaks grow. A spinning meter dial with every fixture off, falling pressure or a wet spot that never dries all point to a main line leak, and we locate it, expose the pipe and splice in a new section with approved fittings. Old galvanized usually gets replaced.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Dayton, OH

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. The new line takes its place. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. Collapsed sections are fine. Lining cannot do that.

    Storm Drain and Lateral Repair in Dayton, OH

    Storm laterals carry roof and yard water away from the foundation. Broken ones leave puddles. We jet the line, camera it for crushed or separated sections and replace the damaged corrugated or PVC pipe so water reaches the outlet instead of soaking the ground around your footings. The outlet gets cleared too.

    Sewer Line Underground Repair in Dayton, OH

    Roots and settling soil cause most buried sewer failures. Age does the rest. We offer open trench repair, pipe bursting and cured-in-place lining, and the camera footage decides which one fits the depth, the damage and what sits on top of the line. All work meets Ohio plumbing code.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Dayton

    Trenchless repair replaces or relines buried pipe without opening its full length. One or two holes, not a trench. We can pull new PEX or HDPE through the old pipe path or line a sewer lateral with resin, which leaves driveways, patios and mature landscaping in place. Some lines need a dig. The route and condition decide it.

    Foundation Leak Detection and Repair in Dayton, OH

    A leak under a slab often shows up as a warm spot on the floor. Sometimes you hear water running. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests and locate the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging, then repair it through a small slab cut or reroute the line overhead so the floor stays intact. Both options get a price.
