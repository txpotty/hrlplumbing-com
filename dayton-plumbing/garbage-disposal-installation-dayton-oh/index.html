    Disposal Installation in Dayton, OH: How We Do It

    Disposals in Remodels and New Builds in Dayton

    On new builds and remodels in Dayton, we coordinate with the general contractor so the disposal, the sink and the dishwasher are planned together.     Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Dayton

    The stopper lid acts as the switch on a batch-feed unit. We install the unit, then test the lid in every position to make sure the motor only runs when the lid is fully seated and stops the moment it is lifted out. That is the whole point. They are popular with families who have small children and people who prefer no open hopper in Dayton homes.

    Standard Disposal Installation in Dayton

    Most households in Dayton do well with a continuous-feed unit between 1/3 and 1/2 horsepower. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Then we wire it and test it. A smooth spin and a clean drain tell us it is right.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Dayton, OH

    An air button on the sink deck or counter turns the disposal on. Pressing the button sends a pulse of air through a small tube to a control box under the sink, so there is no electricity at the button and no need to drill for a wall switch. Islands use them often.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Dayton

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. They need no wall switch, so they are a good choice for kitchens where adding a switch would mean cutting into finished drywall or tile backsplash. Mounting and drains are standard.

    Disposal Installation in Rentals in Dayton

    Landlords want disposals that survive tenants. We install sturdy models with simple mounts and good warranties, and we can handle several units in one visit when you are turning over more than one apartment. Quick installs cut vacancy days. Tenants get a short care sheet.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Dayton

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. We also check the circuit. A larger motor pulls more current when it starts.

    InSinkErator Disposal Installation in Dayton, OH

    InSinkErator uses the Quick Lock mount on most current models, with a ring we snug down using the wrench in the box. Badger units are the simple single-stage line, while Evolution models add grind stages and insulation that make them noticeably quieter in an open kitchen. Cords are often sold separately. We bring the right cord kit for the model.
