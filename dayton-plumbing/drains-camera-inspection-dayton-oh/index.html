    Locating Lost Items with Camera in Dayton, OH

    Rings, earrings and small toys go down drains every day. Most stop in the trap. When they get past it, a small camera can find the item in the branch line and show us whether we can fish it out from the nearest opening or need to open a fitting. Much cheaper than guessing.

    Residential Drain Camera Inspection in Dayton

    Kitchen, bath and laundry lines inside the house get a smaller camera head. Branch lines are tight. We can follow a 1 1/2 inch line through its bends, see where it ties into the main and check whether it has the right slope or a dip where water and grease sit. It explains a lot of slow drains.

    Identifying Specific Pipe Damage in Dayton

    Each kind of damage looks different on camera. Cracks show as dark lines. Offsets show as a lip where one pipe section has dropped below the next, sags show standing water and corroded iron looks rough and flaky. Knowing which one you have sets the repair.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Dayton, OH

    The main sewer lateral carries everything from your house to the city sewer. It is out of sight for decades. Our camera travels the full length, showing root intrusion, cracked joints, offset pipe and bellies along with the footage counter so each defect has an exact distance. The locator then marks each one on the ground.

    Camera Inspection Cost vs. Exploratory Digging in Dayton, OH

    A camera inspection usually costs a few hundred dollars. Exploratory holes cost far more. Without a camera, a crew may open two or three holes trying to find a break that the camera and locator would have pinned to one spot within a foot. With footage and a mark on the ground, the excavation stays small. Less lawn gets torn up.
