    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Gurgling is another. Water coming up through a drain when you flush, a sewage smell in the yard or basement and slow drains throughout the house all point to a sewer line that needs cleaning.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Dayton, OH

    A waterproof camera shows the pipe condition. Cracks and breaks show up. We can spot cracks, breaks, corrosion and root intrusion along with the exact location of any blockage, which lets us choose the right cleaning or repair without guesswork.
