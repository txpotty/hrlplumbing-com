    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Dayton

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in.     Commercial Toilet Replacement in Dayton, OH

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank.     What a Standard Swap Includes in Dayton

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then we level it. We caulk the base except for a small gap at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Dayton, OH

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Take the measurement from the wall to the closet bolts, not from the baseboard trim. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. Offset flanges can fix a small mismatch.

    Toilet Brands We Replace in Dayton

    Brand matters less than model, because every major maker sells a great toilet and a mediocre one at nearly the same price. Our picks come down to flush valve size and the MaP score. Parts availability counts too. Proprietary flappers and fill valves mean that ten years from now, a simple fix becomes a special order.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Dayton

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Dayton, OH

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. Clearances get checked before we order anything. Sensor flush valves are an easy add during the swap, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.
