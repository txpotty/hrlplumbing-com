    PEX Water Line Installation in Euclid, OH

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. During deep cold snaps, homeowners around Euclid can let faucets drip and open sink cabinets to keep warm air on the pipes. A frozen PEX line often survives where copper would crack. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and keep it covered from sunlight at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Euclid, OH

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. Plenty of copper mains have lasted 60 years. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pack sand around it so rocks do not touch the pipe. Where it goes through a concrete wall, it gets a plastic sleeve and a seal to keep water and bugs out.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Euclid

    Your service line runs from the meter or curb stop to the main shutoff inside the house, usually as 3/4-inch or 1-inch pipe. Once it has leaked in two different places, patching stops making sense. We dig at both ends, pull or cut out the old pipe, and run new line at the depth local code calls for, then tie into the meter and the house valve. Most homes are done in a day.

    Burst Main Line Response in Euclid, OH

    A burst main is a fast-moving problem. We get the water off, dig to the break, and decide on site whether a clamp or a short section of new pipe will hold until a full replacement can be scheduled, or whether the pipe crumbles when we touch it. You will not be without water overnight if we can help it.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Euclid

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? A repair coupling is often the right call. Three leaks on a galvanized line from the 1950s is different, because the pipe is thin everywhere and the next leak is already forming, so the full line costs less than a string of repairs. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Euclid, OH

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorine in the water breaks it down from the inside. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some insurers will not write a policy on a house that still has it. Worth replacing.
