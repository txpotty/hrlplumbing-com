    Polybutylene Leak Repair in Euclid

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. A repair buys time.     Why Polybutylene Fails in Euclid

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Private wells serve many rural homes, and iron, sulfur and hardness are common issues that call for filtration or softening. Hot water speeds it up. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

Heat tape and insulation on exposed runs get inspected and replaced where needed, so the new supply lines are protected under the home.
