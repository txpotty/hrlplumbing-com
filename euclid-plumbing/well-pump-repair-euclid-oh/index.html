    Well Pump Problems We Fix in Euclid, OH

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Euclid

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. The usual signs are no water at all, weak pressure or a hum with no flow. Testing starts at the surface. When a pull is needed, a hoist brings the pipe up safely so we can replace the motor, the impellers or the check valve.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Euclid, OH

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. The control box capacitor and relay are next on the list. We lock out the power before testing anything. All wiring and splices meet Ohio electrical code before the pump restarts.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Euclid, OH

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. Our technicians know them well, from the QD control boxes to the SubDrive constant pressure units. We carry the common Franklin parts. A motor that tests bad on a megohm meter usually gets replaced rather than rewound, since a new motor costs less than shipping and rebuilding the old one.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Euclid

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Euclid

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up instead of pulling it, and they run quiet for years, but motors, impellers and wire splices still fail eventually. After pulling the pump, we test each part and replace only the failed one. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Euclid, OH well.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Euclid, OH

    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. A submersible pull and replacement runs about $1,800 to $5,000 and up, based on depth and horsepower. An older pump that already needs pulling is often worth replacing on the same trip. We lay out both options.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Euclid, OH

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. When they fault, the drive shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot the drive, the pressure sensor and the wiring, then repair or replace the failed part and verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.
