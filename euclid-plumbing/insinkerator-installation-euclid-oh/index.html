    Hard Water and Disposals in Euclid

    Much of Ohio has hard water, which leaves scale in water heaters, clogs aerators and shortens the life of appliances. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. A scaled unit gets loud. It also drains slower, because mineral crust narrows the tailpiece and the trap, so we look inside the old drain parts before we connect anything new to them. If they are crusted badly, we swap them while we are there.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Euclid

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. We wire each one to match.

    Badger Disposal Install in Euclid

    Most replacement disposals in area kitchens are a Badger. A 1/3 to 3/4 HP Badger handles normal scraps and fits almost any sink base cabinet. The unit hangs on its quick-lock mount, the discharge tube goes on, a cord kit gets added if needed and we finish with a cold-water test grind. About an hour, usually.

    Evolution Series Installation in Euclid

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Cabinet clearance matters. Before install day we measure the drop from sink to trap arm so the unit fits. Tight cabinets suit the Compact, while the Excel wants the most space.

    Garbage Disposal Replacement in Euclid

    If your current disposal hums, leaks from the bottom or trips its reset button every week, replacement usually beats repair. A leak from the housing means the internal seal is gone.     Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Euclid

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. The hose clamps onto the inlet and runs through an air gap or a high loop, which keeps dirty sink water from siphoning back into the tub. We test both appliances at once.

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Euclid

Then the mount ring goes on. The unit hangs on the ring, locks with a quarter turn, and the discharge tube meets the trap on a new slip-joint washer. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.
