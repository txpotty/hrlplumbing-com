    Shower Diverter Repair in Dublin, OH

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. It is a short job.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Dublin

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. When that happens, water goes under the shower instead of down the pipe, and the first sign is often a soft spot in the floor next to the shower or a stain in the ceiling below. The drain gets cleared and tested. Loose drains get resealed, and cracked ones get replaced.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Dublin

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year.
