    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Dublin

    Expect $300 to $800 in Dublin. Severity changes the price. Pipe length, diameter and whether a camera inspection is needed all affect the total, and long commercial lines or extensive roots land at the higher end, with a written estimate before work.

    It is safe for most pipes in fair condition when the pressure is set correctly. Broken or collapsed pipe never gets jetted.

    Buildup responds best. Grease, scale and sludge. Soap scum, hair mats and fine roots that have worked into the sewer also come out under high pressure, since the jet scours the entire pipe surface instead of just one path.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole wall with water.

    We use professional cart and trailer jetters along with rotating and root-cutting nozzles from established manufacturers. Every job is recorded on camera.

    Typically 1 to 3 hours. Inspection time is included. The first camera pass, the jetting itself and the final inspection all fit in that window for most homes, while commercial jobs or a badly blocked main may need more time.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection shows damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.

    Sound PVC, cast iron and concrete handle it fine. Damaged pipe does not. We always check pipe condition on camera first, so we never jet a line that cannot take the pressure.
