    How We Replace Polybutylene in Dublin, OH

    Why Polybutylene Fails in Dublin

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Much of Ohio has hard water, which leaves scale in water heaters, clogs aerators and cuts the life of appliances. Heat makes it worse. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Dublin

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Check the heater first. You may also see it under sinks, near the main shutoff or coming through the garage wall, and we trace runs through the attic to confirm how much of the house it serves.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Dublin, OH

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or the belly underneath. We replace those lines with PEX sized for the home and secure them properly to the frame. We check underneath. Frozen pipes are a regular winter concern in Ohio, especially in crawlspaces, exterior walls and unheated garages. Heat tape and insulation on exposed runs get inspected and replaced where needed, so the new supply lines are protected under the home.

    Polybutylene Checks for Home Sales in Dublin, OH

    Buying or selling a house built between about 1978 and 1995 in Dublin, OH? Check for poly. We look at the pipe at the water heater, under sinks, at the main and in the attic, read any stamps and give you a written finding and a replacement estimate. It helps in negotiations.

    Affordable Polybutylene Solutions in Dublin, OH

    Poly replacement is a big expense, so we give you options instead of a single take-it-or-leave-it number. PEX-A is the budget pick.     Copper Replacement Option in Dublin

    Type L copper appeals to some homeowners for its long history and rigid feel. Joints are soldered or pressed. Since copper is pricier and slower to install, we walk through cost, water conditions and your plans for the house before you decide.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Dublin, OH

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance, and most homes are completed in two to four working days with water back on each night.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Dublin, OH

    Some homeowners in Dublin, OH want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. Our copper replacements use thicker Type L pipe with lead-free soldered or pressed joints, not the thinner Type M. Expect a higher price and a slightly longer job with copper, in exchange for good heat and UV tolerance and a long history.
