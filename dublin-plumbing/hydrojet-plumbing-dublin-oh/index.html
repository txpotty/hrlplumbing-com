    Why Choose HRL Plumbing for Hydrojet Plumbing in Dublin, OH?

    Hydrojetting cleans more thoroughly than any cable can. Cabling leaves the pipe walls coated. Jetting removes grease, scale, sludge and soft roots from the full circumference of the pipe, restoring close to its original diameter, which is why a jetted line stays clear so much longer than a snaked one.

    • Pipe-Safe, With the right settings, jetting is safe for most sound pipe.
    • Camera Checked, We camera the line first so fragile pipe is never jetted blind.
    • Wall-to-Wall Cleaning, Jetting scours the entire inside of the pipe, taking out the grease and scale behind repeat clogs.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Dublin, OH

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Sump pumps work hard during wet seasons, and a battery backup helps keep them running through storm-related power outages. We jet yard lines from the outlet back and clean the basins before the wet season. We also check the pop-up emitters.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Dublin, OH

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Dublin

    Properties with chronic drain issues do better on a jetting schedule. Commercial kitchens often jet quarterly. Homes with root-prone sewers usually jet every year or two, and we keep a camera record from each visit so you can see whether the roots are coming back slower, faster or the same.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Dublin

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. Footage shows the material and joint condition, which sets our nozzle and pressure.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Dublin

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. A spinning nozzle removes the grease all around the pipe so it flushes away cleanly.

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in Dublin, OH

    A full sewer backup cannot wait for a weekday slot. First, we open the line. Usually a cable gets the water moving, and then we jet the line to remove what caused the backup, whether that is a grease plug, a root mass or a buildup of wipes caught on a rough joint.

    Root Removal Jetting in Dublin

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. Root-cutting nozzles clear them. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Dublin, OH

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Working locally means we can plan a job before we get there. Old clay calls for a root head. Restaurant lines get grease nozzles and short passes, and cast iron in older homes gets lower pressure and a scale nozzle, so the right setup is ready instead of improvised in your driveway.
