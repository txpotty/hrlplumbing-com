    Proper Sizing and Venting in Dublin, OH

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We figure your household peak demand from bathrooms and people, then make sure the vent diameter, rise and total length match the manufacturer table and local code. Undersized vents spill CO. We test it every time.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Dublin

    Bradford White sells only through plumbers, and we install a lot of them. We install their Defender models to the manual, keeping the bottom air intake clear and the clearances right. They are built heavy. Code-approved installs every time.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Dublin, OH

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. They need an outlet. We terminate the vent to spec.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Dublin, OH

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Condensing units vent through PVC and need a condensate line, so placement and install work differ from a standard tank. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.
