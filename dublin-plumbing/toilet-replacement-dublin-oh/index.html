    What a Standard Swap Includes in Dublin

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then the leveling. We caulk the base except for a small gap at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Dublin

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. First we find the model number inside the tank. A cracked bowl always means a full replacement, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Dublin

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. We check it every time. For a cracked flange, a stainless repair ring is often enough, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Dublin

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. The carrier itself sometimes needs new bolts or a coupling. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Dublin

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. No plumbing changes are needed. We often pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.
