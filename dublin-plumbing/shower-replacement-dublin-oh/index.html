    Aging in place often starts with getting rid of the tub. The tub comes out, the drain is adjusted and the valve goes in at the right height with grab bar blocking and a handheld. No tall curb. It makes a real difference.

    Leaking Shower Pan Replacement in Dublin, OH

    Leaking pans soak the subfloor and the ceiling below. Whether it is a prefab base with a crack or a tiled pan with a failed liner, we tear it out, replace any rotted subfloor and install a new pan that gets flood-tested for a full day. We replace the drain as well. That stops repeat leaks.

    Shower Unit Replacement in Dublin

    Cracked fiberglass units leak at the base first. Cracks tend to start where the base was not supported, and each shower flexes it until water slips through to the subfloor. We pull the old unit. Then we check the subfloor and set the new base on a full mortar bed.

    Walk-In Shower Conversion in Dublin, OH

    Walk-in conversions are one of the most requested bathroom jobs we do. Accessibility standards are followed on request.

    Replacing Showers Damaged by Hard Water in Dublin, OH

Softened water protects the new shower. We can recommend one.
