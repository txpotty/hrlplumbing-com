    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Dublin

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. Pairs well with a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Dublin

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. Bring your own unit. We set it up for you.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Dublin, OH

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HRL Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Softener Loop Installation in Dublin

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We add the loop. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Dublin, OH

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first.     Fleck Water Softener Installation in Dublin, OH

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Dublin, OH

    Building a home in Dublin, OH? Adding a loop during framing is easy and cheap, since the plumber can route the main through the right spot before drywall goes up. We work with builders. We keep outdoor taps off the softener.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Dublin

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. The savings add up. Several models are available.
