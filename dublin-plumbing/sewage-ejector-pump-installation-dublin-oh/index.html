    What Goes Into an Ejector Install in Dublin, OH

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Dublin, OH

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Redundancy matters here.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Dublin

    Residential ejector systems in Dublin serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. The setup is a sealed basin, a pump, a float switch, a check valve and a vent. The parts are simple. Most problems come from the install, not the hardware, so we take our time on the vent and the lid seal.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Dublin

    Liberty ejector pumps are a common pick on our jobs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. Their packaged systems come with the basin, lid, pump and check valve matched together, which cuts down on field fitting and gives the inspector a clean, listed assembly to look at. We still add a shutoff.

    Ejector Pump Replacement in Dublin

    Ejector pumps usually last 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Signs of trouble include a pump that runs constantly, a grinding or humming noise, an alarm that keeps tripping and sewage odor near the pit, and any of those means it is time to call before the basement floor tells you. Old switches fail first.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Dublin, OH

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Stuck floats are common.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Dublin, OH
