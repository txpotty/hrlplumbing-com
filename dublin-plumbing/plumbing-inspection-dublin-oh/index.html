    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Dublin

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked.     Water Heater Safety Inspection in Dublin

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter.
