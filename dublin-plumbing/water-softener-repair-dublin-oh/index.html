    Resin Testing and Replacement in Dublin

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Dublin

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Dublin, OH

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. HRL Plumbing gives honest advice based on both. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. We lay out both options.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Dublin

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. We test each part. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Dublin, OH

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Dublin, OH

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. HRL Plumbing repairs these units in Dublin, OH. Media lasts a few years.
