    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Joints are the weak spot. Roots seek out moisture and nutrients through tiny gaps, and we use root cutters and jetting to clear them from the line, then explain options to keep them from coming back.

    Preventative Sewer Line Maintenance in Dublin

    Scheduled cleaning beats emergency calls. Grease builds again. A regular cleaning schedule keeps roots and grease in check, especially on older lines near big trees.

    Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Compared to Replacement in Dublin, OH

    Cleaning costs a few hundred dollars. Replacement costs thousands. If the pipe is structurally sound, regular cleaning is often the better value, but if the camera shows a collapse or a long belly, cleaning only buys time, and you see both prices.
