    New urinals usually need a permit. Adding a fixture means new drain, vent and supply work that most cities inspect at rough-in and again at final, and commercial jobs often need plans that show fixture counts and accessible layouts. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Dublin

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. The flush handle has to be easy to reach. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Dublin

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. Carrier bolts get inspected first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Dublin, OH

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. Reusing a carrier and lines is cheap, while new carriers and pipe runs through finished walls cost more. Sensor valves add a few hundred. We itemize each piece.
