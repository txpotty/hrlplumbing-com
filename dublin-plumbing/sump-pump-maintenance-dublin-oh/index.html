    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Dublin, OH

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm. We clean their screens, test the floats and follow the discharge until it reaches daylight or an approved drain, because a line that dead-ends under the house just moves the water around. Disconnecting garden hoses and shutting off outdoor spigots before winter prevents many burst pipe calls.

    Float Switch Checks in Dublin, OH

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Dublin

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Submersible Sump Pump Maintenance in Dublin

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. Our plumber pulls the unit out so we can see the cord and the shaft seal. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Dublin, OH

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Any brand can rattle its discharge fittings loose over time. Easy fix.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Dublin, OH

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. That is the moment a backup pump earns its keep. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Dublin, OH

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. We check the battery with a load tester, not just a voltmeter. Next, we cut power to the main pump and watch the backup clear the basin by itself. Alarms get tested too.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Dublin, OH

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. Those units need more frequent checks than a house pump. Our crew tests the controls, the alarm panel and each pump in a duplex station, and records amp draw every visit so a slow decline gets caught. Downtime costs money.

    Our Dublin plumbers have serviced Zoeller, Liberty, Wayne and Little Giant pumps in all kinds of pits, from old fieldstone basements to new poured walls. The price is set before we pick up a tool.
