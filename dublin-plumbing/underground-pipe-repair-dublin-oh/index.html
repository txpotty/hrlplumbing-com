    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Dublin

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It splits suddenly. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, and buried runs tend to split at fittings or where the pipe was bent tight during installation. Patching it just moves the next leak. We use PEX or copper.

    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Dublin

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene splits instead. If your buried service is either one, patches are usually temporary, which is why we recommend a full swap to PEX or copper threaded along the existing route. Pressure comes back right away.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Dublin, OH

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. The new line takes its place. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. Collapsed sections are fine. Lining cannot do that.

    Water Main Underground Repair in Dublin

    Your water main is the pipe between the meter and the house. The homeowner side is yours. When it breaks, we locate the leak without digging, expose the pipe and cut out the bad section and join in new pipe with buried-rated fittings. Rusted steel lines rarely patch well.

    Pool and Outbuilding Supply Line Repair in Dublin, OH

    Pool auto-fills, guest houses, outdoor kitchens and detached garages often have their own buried water lines. These runs are long.
