    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in Dublin

    Most wet basement repairs fall between $500 and $5,000, and the spread comes from what is causing the water. Regrading or extending downspouts is at the low end. Interior drains cost more since the slab gets cut, and exterior work costs most because of the digging. We give a written price first.

    Heavy storms dump a lot of water quickly, and the soil around the foundation cannot absorb it fast enough. Heavy spring rain is a major plumbing stress in Ohio, raising water tables and pushing water into basements. Once the ground is soaked, water pushes in at the cove joint. Dry months hide it.

    Check the base of the walls after a heavy rain. Damp bands low on the wall and water at the cove joint are typical. With everything turned off, a moving water meter means a plumbing leak. Note where the water shows.

    Gutters and downspouts do most of the work. Clear them and extend the outlets away from the walls. Inside, pour a bucket of water into the sump pit every spring to make sure the pump kicks on, check the water heater and washer hoses each year, and replace rubber washer hoses with braided steel ones every five years or so.

    Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne cover most of our installs because they are durable and parts are easy to get. Primary pumps get cast iron housings. For homes that lose power in storms, we add a battery backup pump or a water-powered backup where city water pressure is strong enough, and we put a high-water alarm on the pit either way.

    Leak repair stops a specific source, like a cracked pipe or a leaking wall crack. Waterproofing is broader. It handles groundwater all around the foundation and makes sense when water comes in at many points.

    For sump pumps we mostly install Zoeller and Liberty, with Wayne for some battery backups. We prefer cast iron pump housings. Injection resins and drain membranes come from commercial suppliers, matched so the parts fit.

    Crack repairs use two-part polyurethane or epoxy injection resin, depending on whether the crack is actively wet. Wet cracks get polyurethane. Our interior drains use rigid perforated pipe, which keeps its slope and can be jetted later.
