    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Dublin

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Dublin, OH

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. Pressing the button sends a pulse of air through a small tube to a control box under the sink, so there is no electricity at the button and no need to drill for a wall switch. They are common on islands.

    Disposal Rough-In for New Construction in Dublin

    Rough-in decides where the outlet and drain end up before the cabinets go in. For a kitchen with a disposal, we usually set the drain stub lower than a plain sink would need, so the trap can sit below the disposal outlet with room for proper fall.     InSinkErator Model Selection in Dublin, OH

    Choosing between Badger and Evolution is where we start based on how you cook, how much noise bothers you and how much cabinet room you have.     Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Dublin

    On a batch-feed unit, the lid is the switch.     Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Dublin, OH

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other.
