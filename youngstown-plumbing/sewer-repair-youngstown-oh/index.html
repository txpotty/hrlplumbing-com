    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Youngstown

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection & Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. So does an extra-green strip of lawn. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and mark the location so the repair stays small. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, so nothing in the yard gets disturbed. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Youngstown

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. The full length gets recorded. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, which keeps the dig to one small pit. Older homes in Youngstown often hide two or three problems in one line, and the footage shows every one.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Youngstown

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then run water through it and check it on camera before backfill. Most of these are one-day jobs.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Youngstown

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Youngstown

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The blockage sits downstream of every fixture. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, since a grease clog and a root mass need very different fixes. Older Ohio homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. You get the footage either way.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, depending on how many joints are affected and how far apart they sit. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Youngstown, OH

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. Every drain backs up. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and the camera tells us exactly where the pipe gave way and how deep it sits. The broken length comes out and new pipe goes in. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.
