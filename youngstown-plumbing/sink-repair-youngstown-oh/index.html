    What We Fix Under the Sink in Youngstown, OH

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Youngstown

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. We carry Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler cartridges. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Youngstown

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Youngstown

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount sinks hang on clips glued under the counter, and pedestal sinks hang on a bracket that can pull loose from drywall. We reset the clips and reseal the rim. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. While you wait, shut off the valves under the sink. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Youngstown

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. During deep cold snaps, homeowners around Youngstown can let faucets drip and open sink cabinets to keep heated air on the pipes. Sun breaks down plastic traps and supply lines left outside, and yard dirt and grass clippings clog the drain faster than kitchen scraps ever do. Hose bib leaks are part of the same call. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Youngstown

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Next, put towels on the floor. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Youngstown

    Restaurants and salons cannot wait a week for a sink. Three-compartment sinks and hand sinks have to work for the health inspector, so we schedule around your hours and bring commercial-grade faucets and basket strainers. Most commercial clogs are grease. We also check the grease interceptor connection when the drain backs up.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Youngstown

    Check the slip nuts first. Plastic slip nuts crack when they are overtightened with pliers, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until water seeps out at every joint along the trap. We replace the trap as one kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.
