    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Youngstown

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Youngstown, OH

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. Finished basements are the usual reason. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Youngstown

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. The outlet sits well clear of the foundation and the property line.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Youngstown

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Youngstown, OH

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. Backup and combo units are available too. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Youngstown, OH

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Youngstown

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Landlords like them. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Youngstown, OH

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Access is often tight. We route the discharge out through the rim joist and away from the foundation.
