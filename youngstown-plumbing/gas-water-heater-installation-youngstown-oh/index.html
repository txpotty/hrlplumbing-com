    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Youngstown, OH

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. That means the heater can go in a spot with no chimney or vertical flue, and the vent can run sideways out an exterior wall as long as it stays within the length limit. A 120-volt outlet is required. We terminate the vent to spec.

    Standard Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Youngstown

    Our standard gas tank install starts with removing the old heater. The old tank gets drained and removed, then the new one goes in with new connectors, a drip leg and a sloped vent. Then we test it. They are dependable and affordable.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Youngstown

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. We connect a new gas flex line with a sediment trap, set the vent connector with a quarter inch per foot of rise and pipe the relief valve to an approved spot. Strapping where required. Your household sets the size.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Youngstown

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They cost the least, but the vent has to be the right size, slope upward and keep its clearances. Old vents get checked. We replace bad sections.
