    Drain Clogs We Clear Every Week in Youngstown, OH

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Youngstown, OH

    Vanity drains clog at the pop-up stopper first. The pivot rod catches hair. We pull and clean the stopper, clean the trap, cable the drain arm and swap out worn parts while we are there. Mineral crust can narrow the tailpiece too.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Youngstown, OH

    A jetter pushes water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and pulls itself down the pipe. The spray cleans the pipe all the way around. Cables punch a hole through a clog, but a jetter washes away the grease, scale and soft roots that cause the next clog to form. It works best on lines that clog again and again.

    Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Youngstown, OH

    A washing machine dumps up to 20 gallons in a couple of minutes. Fibers and lint go with the water. Over months that lint packs the standpipe and the branch line behind it, which is why a clogged laundry drain usually overflows at the standpipe in the middle of a cycle. A lint filter on the hose helps. Some laundry lines share the kitchen branch, so we check both.

    Drain Cleaning & Maintenance in Youngstown, OH

    Regular cleaning keeps a drain from building up to the point of a full clog. Kitchen lines benefit the most. Private wells serve many rural homes, and iron, sulfur and hardness are frequent issues that call for filtration or softening. We set the schedule by how fast each line builds up, which might be once a year for a quiet household and every few months for a busy one, and we note on each visit how much came out.

    Toilet Clog Repair in Youngstown

    A toilet clog usually sits in the trapway, the S-shaped channel inside the bowl. Plungers often miss it. We use a closet auger with a rubber sleeve, which reaches through the trapway and pulls out the blockage without scratching the porcelain. Toys and wipes come out this way. If the auger finds nothing, the clog is past the toilet and we check the main.

    Tub and Shower Drain Clogs in Youngstown

    Slow tubs are almost always hair and soap scum. Every so often it is a lost bottle cap. We remove the strainer or overflow plate, clear it with a small drum auger and then work on the trap if needed. We take care not to scratch acrylic, fiberglass or porcelain.

    Main Sewer Line Clog Clearing in Youngstown, OH

    Several drains backing up at once usually means the clog is in the main. It needs a bigger machine. We find the cleanout, open it carefully because the line may be holding sewage under pressure, and run a sectional cable with a root cutter if roots are suspected. A camera afterward shows what caused it.

    Outdoor Drain & Storm Drain Clearing in Youngstown, OH

    Patio drains, driveway trench drains and downspout lines fill with leaves, grit and mud. Sump pumps work hard during wet seasons, and a battery backup helps keep them running through storm-related power outages. Then water heads for the foundation. We clear the grate and the pipe behind it with a cable or jetter, then flush to the outlet and make sure the discharge point is open. Clear outlets matter.
