    Valve, Head and Drain Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Youngstown

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. A vinegar soak can work. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Youngstown

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. Each part gets tested. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. We start with the head. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Youngstown, OH

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. Each side gets tested. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Youngstown

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. Replacing the cartridge solves it. If you cannot get the water hot enough, the anti-scald limit stop may just need adjusting.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Minerals bind soap scum and hair into a tough mat that clings to the pipe walls and gets thicker with every shower. An auger cuts through it. Heavy buildup further down gets cleared with a water jetter instead of harsh chemicals.
