    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Youngstown, OH

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. Pool heaters are the big load, often 250,000 to 400,000 BTU, so a line that was fine for a grill can starve the heater and the house appliances at the same time. We size outdoor lines for the full load. Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Youngstown, OH

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners.     Propane Gas Line Service in Youngstown, OH

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.
