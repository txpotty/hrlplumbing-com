    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. Most go unnoticed for months. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then cut out the damaged section and replace it with matching PVC, ABS or cast iron. Repairs meet local plumbing code.

    Drain Repair Cost Comparison in Youngstown, OH

    A trap or slip-joint repair is one of the cheapest plumbing jobs. Often under an hour. Replacing a section of branch drain in an open wall or crawlspace costs more but is still a modest repair, and cutting into finished drywall or swapping an entire stack sits at the top of the range. You get every option with a number.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. Fine soil plugs it over time. Tree roots and shifting clay often break into older clay or cast iron sewer laterals, leading to slow drains and backups. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, so we camera the tile, find the failed section and replace it with perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric and clean gravel. We confirm it drains to daylight or a sump.

    Clogged Drain Repair in Youngstown

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Repeat clogs are a warning. We clear the line with a drum auger or a jetter, then camera the pipe when the blockage is deep or keeps coming back, which shows whether you have buildup, a sag or a broken fitting. That decides the repair.

    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Poor original work causes some. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then replace that section with new pipe joined by shielded couplings that fit the old and new outside diameters. The repair gets a flow test.

    Storm Drain Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Heavy spring rain is a major plumbing stress in Ohio, raising water tables and pushing water into basements. Leaves and silt clog them. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and replace damaged corrugated or PVC pipe so water flows to the outlet instead of pooling against the house. We check the discharge end every time.

    Recurring Clog Repair in Youngstown

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Cleaning will not stop it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and each one shows up clearly on a camera or when we pull the trap. The fix goes where the clog starts.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. Full stack replacement gets a separate quote.
