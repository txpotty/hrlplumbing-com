    Grease Trap Cleaning in Youngstown

    A grease trap that smells sour is usually past due for cleaning. We remove the lid, scrape the baffles, pull the floating grease and settled solids, and check the gasket so the lid seals again instead of venting into the kitchen. Each cleaning goes in the log. That record is what the pretreatment inspector asks to see first.

    Kitchen Sink Installs in Youngstown

    Undermount sinks hang from clips and epoxy under the stone, so the first thing we check is how the bowl is held up. Drop-in sinks get a bead of silicone under the rim, new strainer baskets and a fresh 1-1/2 inch trap set to meet the drain in the wall without any strain on the joints. Old supply lines never get reused. Both bowls get filled and drained while we watch every joint.

    Disposal Repair and Replacement in Youngstown, OH

    A disposal that hums but will not spin usually has something wedged in it. We cut the power, work the flywheel loose with a 1/4 inch hex key from underneath, pull out the bottle cap or bone, and press the red reset button before testing it again. Leaking units are another matter. A drip from the bottom of the housing means the internal seal failed, and that unit has to be replaced.

    Burst Supply Lines in Youngstown, OH

    Supply lines with plastic coupling nuts split more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings. A failed line under the sink can put several gallons a minute into the cabinet and the floor below before anyone notices it. Both supply lines get replaced. If one line cracked from age, the other one is about the same age.

    Recurring Kitchen Clogs in Youngstown, OH

    Grease, rice, pasta and coffee grounds stack up in the trap and the drain arm over months, not days. We clear the line with a cable sized for kitchen pipe and can camera it when the same clog returns. The pipe is sometimes the cause. A low spot in the line or a drain arm without enough fall will collect grease however often it is cleared.

    Grease Trap Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Rusted inlet tees, cracked lids and missing baffles all let grease pass straight through a trap. We inspect each part during cleaning and replace what has failed, usually on the same visit. A trap missing its baffle is just a pipe. On big interceptors we schedule the pump-out first and then check the walls, outlet and sampling port.

    Disposal Jams and Hums in Youngstown, OH

    A hum without grinding tells us the motor has power and the plate will not turn. That is often ten minutes. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before running water through it. No sound at all points us to the switch, the outlet or the breaker.

    New Disposal Installation in Youngstown

    Adding a disposal where there never was one means a switched outlet or circuit in the cabinet, and that part is electrical work. We handle the plumbing and can line up the electrician. The trap often has to drop a couple of inches because the disposal outlet sits lower than the sink tailpiece did, and the wall stub has to allow it.
