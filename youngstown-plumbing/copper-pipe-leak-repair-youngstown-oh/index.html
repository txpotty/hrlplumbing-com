    Pinhole Leak Repair in Youngstown

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. You see a drip or a stain. They are small and easy to miss behind drywall, which is why we use thermal imaging to find them before we open anything. We replace the pitted length with Type L. Nearby pipe gets checked.

    Split Copper Line Repair in Youngstown, OH

    A copper line can split from a freeze, a settling foundation or a hit from a nail. Splits leak hard. Our plumber isolates the water source, cuts out the split and solders in new pipe, while checking nearby pipe for damage from the same cause. We protect the pipe afterward.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Youngstown

    Commercial buildings often run larger diameter copper for domestic water. Water-off time hurts. We repair copper supply lines in offices, restaurants and apartment buildings, using press tools on large lines to shorten the shutdown and skip the torch. We schedule around business hours.

    Corrosion Leak Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Water can be hard on copper over time. Private wells serve many rural homes, and iron, sulfur and hardness are common issues that call for filtration or softening. Thinning walls lead to leaks. We look at corrosion patterns on the pipe we remove, because the location and shape of the pits tell us whether water chemistry, velocity or electrical current is doing the damage. Then we fix that too.

    Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in Youngstown

    Angle stops, shutoff valves and copper fittings under sinks and behind toilets can weep or seize. Minerals jam them. Hard water buildup inside a valve can keep it from closing when you need it most, so we replace failing valves and fittings with quarter-turn ball valves that shut off fully and are easy to turn. Every valve gets checked.

    Under Slab Copper Leak Repair in Youngstown, OH

    Copper lines under a slab can wear through where they rub concrete or gravel. A hot spot underfoot is common. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests and locate the leak with acoustic and thermal equipment, then choose between a small slab cut at the leak and rerouting the line through the walls or attic. We quote each way.

    Thin-Wall Copper Pinhole Repair in Youngstown

We locate the leak precisely and either solder in a new section or use a press fitting near framing, replacing the damaged length with Type L copper so the repair has a thicker wall than the pipe it replaced. We check the rest of the run.
