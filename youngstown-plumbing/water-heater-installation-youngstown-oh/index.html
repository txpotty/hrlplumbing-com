    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Youngstown

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. We test draft before leaving. Gas suits busy households.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Youngstown

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. Sizing is based on the first-hour rating on the yellow label, which tells you how much hot water the tank can deliver during a busy hour. Gas or electric both work. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Youngstown

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. Our commercial work covers high-BTU tanks and multi-unit tankless racks, along with the larger gas lines, venting and code inspections that come with them. We schedule around your hours. Installs can happen after close.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Youngstown, OH

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most.     Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Youngstown, OH

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Some qualify for tax credits. We help you decide if one fits.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Youngstown

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Plumbing work in Ohio follows a statewide plumbing code based on the International Plumbing Code, with residential work covered by the state residential code. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Youngstown, OH

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. They do not need a vent. You get more spots to put it.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Youngstown

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Venting is what keeps CO out. All work follows local code.
