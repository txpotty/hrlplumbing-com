    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Middletown

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's built for sewer use. Its liners cure into a structural pipe that holds even if the old host pipe keeps falling apart around it. They handle harsh soil and flow.

    Pipe Bursting in Middletown, OH

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Middletown, OH

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path.
