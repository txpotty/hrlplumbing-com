    Gas Line Leak Locating in Middletown

    You cannot locate a gas leak by smell alone. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, and check every joint with soap and a detector. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe often marks a leak that has been there a while. We coordinate with the gas utility.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Middletown, OH

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either can lose thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. Check the yard first. Our tech tests the irrigation system separately from the house, runs each zone and traces buried lines, since sprinkler leaks are the first thing most water departments suggest checking.

    Gas Leak Detection in Middletown

    A faint sulfur smell near the range or furnace needs a professional check. Get out first if it is strong. Our licensed plumbers use a combustible gas detector and soap solution on fittings, appliance connectors and the meter riser, then pressure test the line to find a leak too small to smell, and we repair it to the fuel gas code before turning service back on.

    Slab Leak Detection in Middletown

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. A warm tile is the classic sign. We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then listen through the slab with a ground microphone and confirm with a thermal camera on hot water lines.

    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Middletown

    Soggy grass near the front walk or a sinking spot over the line can mean the service pipe is leaking. Old galvanized and copper lines fail most. We map the pipe with a line tracer and listen for the leak with a ground microphone, so excavation stays to one small hole instead of a trench across the yard. Then we patch or replace.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Middletown, OH

    Checking for leaks once a year catches the small stuff. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves. Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors benefit most from a yearly check, since those parts tend to fail all at once. Short visit, big savings.

    Emergency Leak Detection in Middletown, OH

    Water coming through a ceiling at night does not wait for an appointment. Shut the main. We respond to Middletown, OH emergencies with full detection gear, find which line let go, and isolate it so you can get water back on the rest of the house while the permanent repair gets scheduled.

    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Middletown

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Tree roots and shifting clay often break into older clay or cast iron sewer laterals, leading to slow drains and backups. Isolating the line comes first. Then acoustic listening and thermal imaging find the leak so nobody has to break the floor open to search, and you get a marked spot plus repair options before any concrete gets cut.
