    Drop-In Sink Installation in Middletown, OH

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Clips pull it tight to the counter. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Commercial Restroom Sink Installation in Middletown

    Public restroom lavatories take a beating. We install wall-hung and countertop units on carriers, add sensor or metering faucets, and set thermostatic mixing valves where code requires tempered water at public hand sinks. We can fit tamper-proof parts. Spec sheets go to your general contractor.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Middletown, OH

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. A three-bay sink drains indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water near each prep area. Plan review comments come first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Middletown

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Old basket strainers fuse to the bowl, the disposal has to come off its mounting ring, and the dishwasher drain hose needs to be rerouted to the new disposal or tailpiece with a high loop. Then the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Middletown, OH

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Middletown

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. A wall bracket lagged into blocking carries the weight, and if the wall has none, we open it and add a board. Drywall anchors do not cut it. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Middletown, OH

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. For an accessible bathroom we set the rim no higher than 34 inches, leave knee clearance underneath, and wrap or shield the hot water and drain pipes so a seated user cannot touch them. An offset tailpiece frees up knee room. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Middletown
