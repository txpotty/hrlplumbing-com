    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Middletown

    Standard plumbing inspections typically run $150 to $300. Adding a sewer camera inspection usually brings the total to $250 to $500.

    Most homes do well with an inspection every one to two years. Buying a home is another time. If you are purchasing a property, live in an older house or have had recent leaks or backups, a yearly check gives you a better chance of catching the next problem early.

    For an older home, it usually pays for itself. Old pipe hides problems. Homes with galvanized supply pipe, clay or cast iron sewers or original water heaters often have problems that are invisible from the outside, and an inspection lets you plan repairs instead of reacting to a failure.

    We inspect all the common brands and materials. That includes Rheem, AO Smith and Bradford White heaters. We work with PEX, copper, CPVC, PVC, cast iron, galvanized and poly, and we know the usual weak points for each and for common fixture brands.

    Most residential inspections in Middletown take one to two hours. Older or bigger homes take longer. The exact time depends on how big the house is, how old the plumbing is and whether we find something that needs extra tracing, such as a hidden leak or a sewer defect.

    Every one to two years works for most homes. Some houses need it yearly. Houses with original pipe, big trees over the sewer or past leaks do better with a yearly check that catches problems while they are small.

    It is smart before buying any home, especially one over 25 years old or with large trees. Backups and gurgling drains also call for one.

    No permit is needed for an inspection. Repairs may need one. If the inspection leads to repairs or replacements that need a permit, we will say so up front and handle it if we do the job.
