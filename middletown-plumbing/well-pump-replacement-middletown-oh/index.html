    What We Fix on Well Systems in Middletown, OH

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Middletown

    Sand kills submersibles early. A well that pumps sand will wear impellers down in a few years, so we check for grit in the old pump and talk about setting the new one higher off the bottom or adding a sand separator. Pump setting depth matters.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Middletown

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. If the pump clicks on every time someone runs a faucet for ten seconds, the bladder in the tank has probably failed and the pump is short cycling itself to death. A tire gauge on the air valve tells us a lot. The air charge should read 2 psi under the switch cut-in.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Middletown, OH

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. Air sneaks in through a pinhole in the suction line, the pump loses prime and the seal burns up running dry. We pressure test the line. If the well is too deep for a jet pump, we will say so and quote a submersible.

    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Middletown

    Pump and tank work as a pair. A tank that is too small or has lost its air charge makes the pump start dozens of extra times a day, and every start heats the motor. We check it on every pump job.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Middletown

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. Depending on depth and pump weight, it gets replaced with schedule 120 PVC or poly. Wire gets the same look. Nicked jackets and old tape splices go.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Middletown, OH

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. On a three-wire submersible, the start capacitor or the relay in that box fails far more often than the motor down the well does. We test them first. Swapping a capacitor costs a fraction of pulling a pump.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in Middletown, OH

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A few panels feed a DC pump through a controller while the sun is up. The storage tank does the job a battery would. We size panels to your daily gallons.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Middletown, OH

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. A pressure switch with pitted contacts or a plugged sensing tube can stop the pump even though nothing is wrong down the well. The switch itself is a cheap part. You get to see the old part.
