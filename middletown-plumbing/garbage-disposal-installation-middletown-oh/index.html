    Disposal Rough-In for New Construction in Middletown

    At rough-in we set the drain height and the outlet location before the cabinets go in. For a kitchen with a disposal, we usually set the drain stub lower than a plain sink would need, so the trap can sit below the disposal outlet with room for proper fall. A switched outlet goes in at this stage. We mark its spot so it clears the trap.

    Commercial Disposal Installation in Middletown

    Commercial disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and typically come with a control panel and solenoid valve and need their own circuit. We set the unit on the mounting collar, run the drain to the grease trap where the local rules require it, and wire the controls so the water runs whenever the motor does. Health codes vary. We check the requirement before we order anything.

    Disposal Upgrades in Middletown, OH

    Stepping up from an old 1/3 HP unit to a quieter model is a common request. We disconnect and remove the old disposal, check the trap and drain arm for corrosion, and install the new unit with a new mount, so the upgrade does not inherit the old unit's worn parts. Old drain arms often need replacing. Galvanized arms rust from the inside.

    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Middletown

    Every new disposal has a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. Forgetting the plug floods the dishwasher. An air gap or high loop goes in too, which keeps sink water from running back into the dishwasher.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Middletown

    For kitchens that have never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply. We install the mount, hang the disposal and connect the discharge tube to a new trap, lowering the drain at the wall if the old stub sits too high for the disposal outlet. Power comes from a licensed electrician when needed.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Middletown

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit is bigger and heavier than a starter model, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. We also check the circuit. Bigger motors draw more at startup.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in Middletown, OH

    Old disposals usually come off with a twist of the mounting ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Seals harden with age. We haul the old unit away.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Middletown, OH

    A batch-feed disposal will not start until the lid is in, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. They are slower to use. Families with small kids often like the trade.
