    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Middletown, OH

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. Every one is listed for potable water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Middletown, OH

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.
