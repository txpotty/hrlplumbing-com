    Why Polybutylene Fails in Middletown

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Homeowners around Middletown often install a softener to protect water heaters and reduce spotting on fixtures. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.
