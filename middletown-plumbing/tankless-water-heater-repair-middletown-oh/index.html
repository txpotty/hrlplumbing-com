    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Middletown, OH

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, so a code 11 on one unit means something else on another. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, but the tests behind them differ. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Middletown

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just at the moment it lights. We put a manometer on the inlet test port and run every hot tap in the house, since pressure that sags under load leads to flame loss and slugs of cold water. Then we check the exhaust. Codes come off when the numbers are right.

    Igniter and Flame Rod Repair in Middletown, OH

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. An oxidized flame rod gives a weak microamp reading and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, look for a cracked igniter or a lazy spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Repair in Middletown

    Restaurants and laundromats in Middletown often run two to six tankless units on a common manifold. One unit down means the others work harder and scale faster, so we check the cascade controller, the isolation valves on each heater and the water quality coming in. A down kitchen loses money. We schedule around service hours when we can.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Middletown

    Recirculation cuts the wait for hot water at a far bathroom from a minute or more to a few seconds. When the pump fails or the check valve sticks, you get long waits again or lukewarm water in the cold line. We test the pump, check for a crossover valve under the far sink, and set the schedule so it runs around your mornings and evenings. Two settings, mostly.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Middletown, OH

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. Clogged traps shut the unit down. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Rinnai Error Code Repair in Middletown

    Rinnai owners usually call about a code 10, which points to an air supply or exhaust blockage. We check the vent terminal for bird nests and dryer lint, then pull the front cover to inspect the intake screen and the combustion fan. Vent runs longer than the manual allows can trigger it too. We measure.

    Navien Condensate & Fan Repair in Middletown

    Navien's E016 is a heat exchanger overheat, and scale or a backed-up condensate line is usually behind it. We also read fan speed from the service menu and compare it to spec, since a fan that cannot reach target RPM will cause flame and exhaust faults on the same unit. Clean trap, clean screen, retest.
