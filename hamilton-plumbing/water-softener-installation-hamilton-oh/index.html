    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Hamilton

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. We size before we sell. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Hamilton, OH

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. When a homeowner has already bought one, we handle the plumbing, the bypass and the startup flush the manual requires. Skipping the flush causes trouble. Every step gets done.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Hamilton

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Hamilton, OH

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Hamilton

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. We install several models.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Hamilton, OH

    Building a home in Hamilton, OH? Adding a loop during framing is easy and cheap, since the plumber can route the main through the right spot before drywall goes up. We work with builders. Hose bibs can stay on hard water.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Hamilton

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. Bring your own unit. We set it up for you.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Hamilton, OH

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HRL Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.
