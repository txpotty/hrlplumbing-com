    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Hamilton

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole.     Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Hamilton

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example.
