    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Hamilton

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas tank usually costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless gas units often cost more because of the venting and gas line work involved.

    Power vent units use a fan to push exhaust outside. Direct vent pulls outside air through a sealed pipe, and both place more freely than a chimney-vented tank.

    We advise against doing it yourself. Gas, water and venting all have to be right, and an error in any one of them can cause a gas leak, carbon monoxide in the house or a flooded garage. Licensed plumbers handle it safely.

    Flush the tank once a year and check the anode rod every few years. Sediment over the burner makes the tank run hot and noisy, and a spent anode lets the steel rust from the inside. We offer yearly maintenance.

    No hot water, rusty water, rumbling, leaks at the base or an age past 10 to 12 years are the common signs. One of them alone is reason to plan a replacement.

    Most gas tanks carry a manufacturer warranty of 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts, with longer coverage on premium models that use thicker steel and better anodes. HRL Plumbing also warranties our labor. You get both in writing.

    Once a year is the right interval for a gas heater. A yearly service flushes sediment, checks the anode and burner and tests the relief valve, which catches problems early.

    Gas usually recovers faster and can cost less to run where gas rates are reasonable. Electric skips the vent and the gas line.
