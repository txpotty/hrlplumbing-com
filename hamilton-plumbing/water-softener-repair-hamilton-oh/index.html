    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Hamilton, OH

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. A small drip still wastes water and damages flooring. Once we find the source, from the bypass to the drain connection, the failed seal or fitting gets replaced. We test it under pressure.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Hamilton, OH

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Hamilton, OH

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. We break up and remove the bridge and clean the tank. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Keep the salt about half full.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Hamilton

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Control Valve Rebuild in Hamilton

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Over the years, seals, spacers and pistons wear down. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. The valve gets rebuilt with the kit made for it.
