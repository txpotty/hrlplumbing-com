    Residential Plumbing Service in Hamilton, OH: What We Fix

    Toilet Repair and Installation in Hamilton

    A running toilet can waste a couple hundred gallons a day. Most fixes are a flapper, a fill valve or a new wax ring, and when the bowl is cracked or the toilet is older than the last bathroom remodel, we set a new water-saving model and level it on the flange. Rocking toilets get shimmed and caulked.

    Faucet, Toilet and Shower Valve Repair in Hamilton, OH

    Hard water chews through faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Shower valves get rebuilt, cartridges replaced and tub spout leaks sealed, and if a fixture is too corroded to save, we install the replacement and haul the old one away so it is not sitting in your garage. A slow drip still adds up on the bill.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Hamilton, OH

    Water stains on a ceiling rarely sit right under the leak. Tree roots and shifting clay often break into older clay or cast iron sewer laterals, leading to slow drains and backups. We trace leaks with acoustic sensors, thermal imaging and isolation pressure tests, which lets us open one small hole in the right spot instead of cutting drywall all over the house. Repairs follow the same day when possible.

    Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in Hamilton, OH

    Water pressure over 80 psi wears out hoses and valves early. We test static pressure with a gauge on a hose bib, and if it reads high we install or replace a pressure reducing valve and set it in the 50 to 70 psi range most fixtures are rated for. We also replace main shutoffs that will not turn.

    Water Heater Service and Installation in Hamilton, OH

    Tank or tankless, the failures repeat. A tank heater starts leaking at the bottom or rumbling from sediment, and tankless units throw error codes when scale builds up in the heat exchanger or the venting is wrong. We flush, descale and repair both types.

    Drain Cleaning and Main Line Backups in Hamilton

    Slow drains in more than one fixture point to the main line. One slow sink by itself is usually just the trap or a short branch line, but when the tub gurgles as the washer drains, the blockage is further out and needs a machine cable or a jetter run from the cleanout. Roots are common in older clay lines.

    Pipe Repair and Repiping for Older Homes in Hamilton, OH

    Galvanized steel and polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. Newer homes around Hamilton typically use copper or PEX supply lines with PVC drains, which are easier to maintain. Repipes are done in PEX or Type L copper, running new lines through the attic and walls with small access holes, and the water stays on at night for most of the job. A typical repipe takes two to four days.

    Water Softener and Filter Service in Hamilton

    Much of Ohio has hard water, which leaves scale in water heaters, clogs aerators and shortens the life of appliances. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. We put in and maintain whole-house softeners and carbon filters, including Kinetico and Pelican units, sized from a hardness test we run at your kitchen sink, not a guess. We program regeneration to your household's use.
