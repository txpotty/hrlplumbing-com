    Float Switch Inspection in Hamilton, OH

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Next we adjust the on and off levels so each run is longer and the starts are fewer.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Hamilton

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Hamilton, OH

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. Our plumber load tests the battery instead of just reading its voltage. Next, we cut power to the main pump and watch the backup clear the basin by itself. Alarms get tested too.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Hamilton, OH

    Float trouble causes more no-start calls than anything else. A tethered float can tangle on the pump body or catch on the side of the basin. Vertical floats stick when mineral crust builds up on the rod. For narrow basins where a float keeps catching, an electronic switch with no moving float is a good swap that ends the problem.
