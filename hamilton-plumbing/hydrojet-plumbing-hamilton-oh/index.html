    Why Choose HRL Plumbing for Hydrojet Plumbing in Hamilton, OH?

    Hydrojetting cleans more thoroughly than any cable can. A cable leaves most of the buildup behind. Jetting removes grease, scale, sludge and soft roots from the full circumference of the pipe, restoring close to its original diameter, which is why a jetted line stays clear so much longer than a snaked one.

    • Deep Clean, Jetting cleans the entire pipe wall, unlike a snake that only opens a hole.
    • Verified Results, Before and after camera video shows exactly how much flow we restored.
    • Safe for Pipes, Done by a trained tech, jetting is safe for most sound pipe.

    Citywide Coverage

    Homes with septic systems outside Hamilton get jetting too. Septic lines get careful passes. Jetting the line between the house and the tank clears grease and sludge without harming the tank's bacteria, since we use only water, and the camera can show whether the inlet baffle is intact.

    Experienced Team

    Years of jetting experience go into every job. Knowing when to stop matters. If a line will not clear, we camera it rather than forcing the nozzle through.

    Root Removal Jetting in Hamilton

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. A root nozzle cuts them out.     Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Hamilton, OH

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Chemical cleaners can damage pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Hamilton, OH

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. The jet is the bigger bill. Because jetting removes the buildup that causes repeat clogs, many homeowners spend less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking visits for the same line.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Hamilton

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. A cable cuts a channel through it. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Hamilton, OH

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.
