    Sump Pits, Pumps and Backups in Hamilton, OH

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Hamilton, OH

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Sump pumps work hard during wet seasons, and a battery backup keeps them running through storm-related power outages. We add a second pump in the same pit with its own float, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. Your primary pump still handles the everyday load.

    Sump Pit Installation in Hamilton, OH

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. Too small a pit means the pump short cycles in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Fewer starts mean a longer life.

    Same-Day Sump Pump Installation in Hamilton

    Same-day emergency installs cover pumps that quit in the middle of a storm. We remove standing water from the pit first, then set a new pump on a new check valve, plug it into a tested GFCI outlet and watch it run through several cycles before leaving your Hamilton basement. Then we talk about a backup.

    Sump Pump Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Hamilton, OH

    High water alarms cost little and buy you time. The basic version sounds off when water climbs above the float, while a Wi-Fi monitor sends a text if the level rises or the power drops, which homeowners who travel tend to like. We test it in front of you.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Hamilton

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. A battery backup keeps a second pump running without house power, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We load test the battery.

    Residential Basement Sump Pump Installation in Hamilton

    A typical house pit holds an 18 by 22 inch basin with a 1/3 horsepower pump, and that covers most homes. We size the pump to your inflow and lift, not to the sale sticker. In Hamilton, we set the basin at the low corner, bed it in washed gravel and run 1-1/2 inch PVC out through the rim joist. The discharge ends well away from the foundation.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Systems in Hamilton, OH

    Some lots sit in a high water table and fill a standard pit every few minutes during spring. Those basements need a 1/2 or 3/4 horsepower pump in a larger basin, all sized after we time how fast your pit refills. We use a stopwatch.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Hamilton

    Zoeller's M53 Mighty-Mate is the cast iron pump we install more than any other. It runs at 3/10 horsepower on a vertical float switch, and it moves about 2,580 gallons an hour at 5 feet of lift, enough for a lot of Hamilton basements. Replacement switches are on every supply shelf.
