    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Hamilton, OH

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We swap cartridges or stems and seats, greasing the O-rings so handles turn easily. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Hamilton

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Hamilton, OH

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.

    Tub Valves in Older Houses in Hamilton, OH

    Cast iron tubs and plaster walls change the plan. We open plaster slowly or from a closet behind it, and the cast iron tub stays in place. Patching plaster is slow. Access panels avoid it.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Hamilton, OH

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. They adjust flow when a toilet flushes so the mix holds, and thermostatic valves hold an exact temperature. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Hamilton

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Hamilton

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister each use their own cartridges and trim threads, and a new trim kit from the same maker as the valve usually bolts right on without touching the plumbing. Mixing brands rarely works. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Hamilton, OH

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. Unlike a pressure-balance valve, which only reacts to pressure changes, a thermostatic valve senses the actual water temperature and adjusts the mix, which matters for kids and older adults who cannot react fast to a hot spike. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.
