    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Lakewood, OH

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Lakewood, OH

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. Pop-up linkage parts sit in a damp cabinet all day, and the pivot nut is the leak we find most often on bathroom sinks. Pedestal and wall-hung sinks get the same checks. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Lakewood

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount sinks hang on clips glued under the counter, and pedestal sinks hang on a bracket that can pull loose from drywall. We reset the clips and reseal the rim. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Lakewood

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. When the epoxy under the counter fails, the bowl drops a quarter inch, the caulk line splits, and water from the counter runs straight down into the cabinet. New clips go in and the bowl gets pulled tight. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Lakewood, OH

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. While you wait, shut off the valves under the sink. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Lakewood, OH

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. A clogged aerator screen causes most low flow at a single faucet, and if cleaning it does not help we check the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Low pressure everywhere points to a different problem. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Lakewood

    Restaurants and salons cannot wait a week for a sink. We also check the grease interceptor connection when the drain backs up.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Lakewood, OH

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. In kitchens it is cold grease and food, and in bathrooms it is hair knotted on the stopper and glued with soap scum. We clear it with a hand auger. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.
