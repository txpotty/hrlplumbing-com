    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Lakewood

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Lakewood, OH

    Ruud and Rheem come out of the same company and share many parts. We install both, and because parts overlap, a thermostat or element is easy to find when repairing a newer tank beats replacing it. Repair stays an option.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Lakewood, OH

    A standard tank replacement is usually the most affordable option in Lakewood, OH. Tankless and hybrid heat pump models cost more to buy and install but can cut operating costs over the life of the unit, and tankless units often last 15 to 20 years compared to roughly 8 to 12 for a typical tank. We price all three.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Lakewood

    Businesses in Lakewood run their water heaters harder than homes do, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons, laundromats and offices each have a different demand curve, and we size the new unit by peak gallons per hour and the temperature health code requires, which can mean a larger tank, a booster or a rack of tankless units. Downtime costs money.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Lakewood, OH

    A leaking tank can empty itself onto the floor faster than most people expect. Close the cold supply on top, turn off the gas or breaker, open a hot faucet to relieve pressure, and we will aim to have a plumber and tank there the same day. Do not wait it out.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Lakewood, OH

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Lakewood

    Electric water heaters in Lakewood usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Lakewood

    Newer homes around Lakewood typically use copper or PEX supply lines with PVC drains, which are easier to maintain. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. We bring the help.
