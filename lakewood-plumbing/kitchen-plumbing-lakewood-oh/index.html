    Disposal Jams and Hums in Lakewood, OH

    A hum without grinding tells us the motor has power and the plate will not turn. Usually a ten minute fix. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before running water through it.     Kitchen Drain Clog Removal in Lakewood, OH

    Water rising in the second bowl when the first drains tells us the clog is past the trap, in the branch line behind the cabinet.     Dishwasher and Ice Maker Lines in Lakewood

    A dishwasher needs a hot supply with its own shutoff, a drain to the disposal or a wye tailpiece, and an air gap on the sink deck or a high loop fastened under the counter, depending on local code.     Disposal Repair and Replacement in Lakewood, OH

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is almost always jammed. Leaking units are another matter. Water from the bottom of the housing means the seals are gone and the unit needs replacing.
