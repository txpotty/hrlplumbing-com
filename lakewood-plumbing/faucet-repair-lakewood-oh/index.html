    Common Faucet Repairs in Lakewood, OH

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Lakewood

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. Hard water speeds it up. The valve body gets cleaned while the old part is out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Lakewood, OH

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Lakewood

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. The hose usually splits near the quick-connect fitting. We replace hoses, spray heads and weights with brand parts, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Lakewood

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. We replace what failed and check the rest. Compression, cartridge, ball and disc faucets all fail differently, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in Lakewood

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Close the angle stops under the sink. Should those stops be stuck, the house main shutoff is the backup. We come fast for these.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Lakewood

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. The seals are cheap. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Lakewood

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Washers harden and seats get pitted. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Lakewood, OH

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Many brands sell the handle on its own. The replacement handle gets matched to both the stem and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.
