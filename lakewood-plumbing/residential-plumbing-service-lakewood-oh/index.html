    Toilet Repair and Installation in Lakewood

    Slab Leak Location and Repair in Lakewood

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Heavy clay soils are common in Ohio, and they hold water and shift with the seasons, stressing foundations and sewer lines. We locate them with acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. Then you pick the fix. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute through the attic.

    Faucet, Toilet and Shower Valve Repair in Lakewood, OH

    Hard water chews through faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves.     Sump Pumps and Basement Drainage in Lakewood

    A sump pump only gets tested when it rains hard. We install and replace cast iron submersible pumps, check float switches and replace worn check valves, and we can add a battery backup so the pit keeps draining when the power goes out in a storm. Pour a bucket in to test it.

    Smell gas? Leave the house and call the gas utility first, then call us. We run new black iron or CSST lines for ranges, dryers and grills, then pressure-test the line and check every joint with a leak detector before the appliance gets lit. All gas work is permitted where required.
