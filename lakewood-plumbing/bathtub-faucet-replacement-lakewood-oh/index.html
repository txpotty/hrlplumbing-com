    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Lakewood, OH

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Lakewood, OH

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. Unlike a pressure-balance valve, which only reacts to pressure changes, a thermostatic valve senses the actual water temperature and adjusts the mix, which matters for kids and older adults who cannot react fast to a hot spike. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Lakewood

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. The connection gets checked first. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Lakewood, OH

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.

    Corroded Tub Faucet Replacement in Lakewood, OH

    Much of Ohio has hard water, which leaves scale in water heaters, clogs aerators and cuts the life of appliances. Corrosion shows up as green crust at the handle or a stem you cannot turn by hand. At that stage the trim threads are often too weak and the valve body is pitted where the cartridge seals. Replacement is the better call.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Lakewood

    Repeated repairs add up. If a faucet has had three cartridges in five years, the valve body is likely worn where the cartridge seals, and replacing the whole valve once costs less than another round of service calls. Your service history helps us decide. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.
