    Type L Copper Repiping in Lakewood

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. Our copper repipes use Type L with proper hangers and isolators so the pipe does not wear against framing. Lead-free solder only. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Lakewood, OH

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Weak flow and rusty water are common. Old galvanized gets removed or abandoned and replaced with PEX or copper, and most homes see better pressure and clearer water right away.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Lakewood, OH

    Homeowners around Lakewood often install a softener to protect water heaters and cut down on spotting on fixtures. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Adding the loop now costs less than later.

    Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Lakewood, OH

The first step is temporary repairs so you have water, and then we book the repipe as quickly as the permit and schedule allow. We move fast.     Commercial Repipe Services in Lakewood

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Lakewood, OH

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. The pipe gets brittle from the inside, and fittings crack, so a home that has had one polybutylene leak is usually at risk everywhere else in the system. Full replacement is the fix. Insurers often ask about it at renewal.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Lakewood, OH

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.
