    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Lakewood

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Lakewood, OH

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Lakewood

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. We still answer. An after-hours call gets a tech with a cable machine and a camera, which is enough to restore flow in most cases and to find out whether the trouble is a blockage or a structural break. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. We avoid digging blind at night near gas and electric lines.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Lakewood

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Lakewood

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The clog is past the branches. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, since a grease clog and a root mass need very different fixes. Older Ohio homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. You get the footage either way.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Lakewood, OH

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Spot repairs will not keep up. We price open trench replacement, pipe bursting and full-length lining side by side for your Ohio property, showing what each method costs and what it does to the yard.     Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Lakewood
