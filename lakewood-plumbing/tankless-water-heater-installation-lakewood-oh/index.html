    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Lakewood, OH

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Lakewood, OH homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage.     Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Lakewood

    Sizing a home unit depends on two figures: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. A shower head flows about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen faucet a bit less, so a house that runs two showers and a sink together needs roughly 5 to 6 gallons at the rise your inlet water calls for. That narrows the model list fast.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Lakewood

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Lakewood

    Propane homes in Lakewood can run tankless units too, using a model built or converted for propane. Propane runs at a higher pressure than natural gas, so the regulator, gas line sizing and the unit's gas valve all have to match, and the propane supplier may need to change the regulator at the tank. Tank size matters too.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Lakewood

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump. That pump can push hot water toward a far bathroom on a schedule, which shortens the wait at the tap without adding a separate pump under the sink. Venting is usually two-inch or three-inch PVC.
