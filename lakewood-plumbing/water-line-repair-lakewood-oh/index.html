    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Lakewood

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. Most codes cap it at 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, then swap a failed regulator for a new Watts or Zurn Wilkins valve when it reads too high. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. That way you can actually shut the water off.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Lakewood, OH

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, along with pressure that drops when two fixtures run at once. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up.
