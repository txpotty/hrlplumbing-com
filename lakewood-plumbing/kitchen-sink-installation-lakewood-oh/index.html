    Composite Sink Installation in Lakewood

    Granite and quartz composite sinks are tough on top but can chip at the drain opening if a strainer is overtightened. We tighten by hand first. Most composite brands want silicone rather than putty under the strainer because putty can stain them, so we go by the maker's instructions.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Lakewood

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for tile, laminate and butcher block counters. It is often the quickest swap. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in Lakewood, OH

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks carry separate health code requirements. Prep sinks usually need an indirect drain to a floor sink with an air gap, while the three-compartment sink often runs through the grease trap. The local rule gets checked first. We work before opening or after close so service is not interrupted.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Lakewood

    Drop-in sinks are common in Ohio homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim carries the weight. We check the cutout, run a bead of silicone under the lip and tighten the mounting clips evenly around the bowl so the rim seats flat. Next we connect and test the faucet and drain.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Lakewood

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Lakewood, OH

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and most are single deep bowls with an offset drain. The trap layout follows the offset drain, which lets the disposal fit and leaves storage room on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Lakewood

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. Heavy-wall fittings resist leaks.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Lakewood, OH

    Adding a disposal costs least when the sink is already out. The disposal mounts to the new flange, the trap is set to its outlet height and the dishwasher plug gets knocked out for the hose. You also need power. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.
