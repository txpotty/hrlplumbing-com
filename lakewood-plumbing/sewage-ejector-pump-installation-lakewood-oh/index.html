    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Lakewood

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Emergency Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Lakewood

    An ejector failure usually announces itself with the alarm, followed by slow drains and a smell near the pit. Stop all use of the basement fixtures and call, because once a plumber is on site most emergency replacements take just a few hours. We carry common pumps.
