    A one-piece toilet is cast as a single unit, so there is no tank-to-bowl joint to leak. Expect a higher price and more weight. A two-piece bolts the tank to the bowl with a rubber gasket, which makes it lighter to carry up stairs and cheaper to fix, since you can replace just the tank or just the bowl if one cracks.     Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the toilet drain. Twelve inches is the most common.
