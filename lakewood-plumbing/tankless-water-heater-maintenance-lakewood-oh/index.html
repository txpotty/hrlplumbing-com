    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Lakewood

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. Units with an air intake filter collect dust and lint, which can push the burner into a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Lakewood

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. A code 11 is a failed ignition, while code 12 is a flame that lit and then dropped out. We clean the Rinnai air intake filter and look over the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Emergency Tankless Repair in Lakewood, OH

    No hot water before work is a rough start. Nine times out of ten, a no-heat call is a dead igniter or a scaled exchanger tripping the high-limit. The truck carries igniters and descaling gear, so we can usually fix it that day.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in Lakewood, OH

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. We test gas pressure under full fire, not at idle.     Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Lakewood, OH

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. The recirc loop gets flushed with the exchanger. Next we confirm crossover valve or dedicated return, and set the pump schedule to fit.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Lakewood

    Private wells serve many rural homes, and iron, sulfur and hardness are frequent issues that call for filtration or softening. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. The flow returns. Temperatures stop swinging, and the unit quits shutting down on high-limit.

    Flush Now or Replace the Exchanger Later in Lakewood, OH

    A new heat exchanger can run $600 to $1,500 or more installed, and some manufacturers will not cover one that failed from scale. A descaling visit costs a fraction of that.     Service Valve Kit Installs in Lakewood, OH
