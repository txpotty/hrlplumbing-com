    Leaky Toilet Repair in Lakewood

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Lakewood, OH

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff.     Fill Valve Replacement in Lakewood

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports.
