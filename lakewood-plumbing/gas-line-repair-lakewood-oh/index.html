    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Lakewood

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Lakewood

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.
