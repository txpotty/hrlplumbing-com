    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Cuyahoga Falls

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. When it fails, the pump pumps the same water twice.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Cuyahoga Falls, OH
