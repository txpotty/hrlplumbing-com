    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Cuyahoga Falls, OH

    Basement Wall Sealing in Cuyahoga Falls, OH

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Cold winters and hot, humid summers give Ohio plumbing a wide range of seasonal stress from one year to the next. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Cuyahoga Falls

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Cuyahoga Falls, OH

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Newer homes around Cuyahoga Falls typically use copper or PEX supply lines with PVC drains, which are easier to maintain. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with interior drain tile. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Cuyahoga Falls

    Newer homes around Cuyahoga Falls typically use copper or PEX supply lines with PVC drains, which are easier to repair. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Not every basement needs one.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Cuyahoga Falls

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Heavy clay soils are common in Ohio, and they hold water and shift with the seasons, stressing foundations and sewer lines. We backfill after the drain is tested.
