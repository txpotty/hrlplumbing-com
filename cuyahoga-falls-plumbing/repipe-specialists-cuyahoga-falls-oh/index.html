    Type L Copper Repiping in Cuyahoga Falls

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time.     Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Weak flow and rusty water are common.     Galvanized Pipe Removal in Cuyahoga Falls

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Cuyahoga Falls, OH

    Private wells serve many rural homes, and iron, sulfur and hardness are frequent issues that call for filtration or softening. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Planning it now saves a second visit later.

    Drain Line Repiping in Cuyahoga Falls

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Slow drains in several fixtures, sewer odors and repeated clogs in the same line are common signs of a failing drain system. A camera shows the condition. New PVC or ABS, sloped and vented properly, replaces the bad sections and drains better than the old pipe.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Cuyahoga Falls

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. Our crews use Uponor or similar PEX-A, where the pipe end gets expanded and shrinks back over the fitting for a full-diameter connection. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Most homes are repiped with PEX-A in two to three days.
