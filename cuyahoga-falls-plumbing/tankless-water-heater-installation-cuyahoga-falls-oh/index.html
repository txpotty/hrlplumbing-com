    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Cuyahoga Falls, OH

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Much of Ohio has hard water, which leaves scale in water heaters, clogs aerators and cuts the life of appliances. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. A yearly flush prevents it. Service valves make it easy.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Cuyahoga Falls

    Sizing a home unit depends on two figures: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. With a shower at about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen tap a bit under, two showers and a sink together need around 5 or 6 gallons per minute at the needed rise. That narrows the model list fast.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Cuyahoga Falls

    Propane homes in Cuyahoga Falls are good candidates for tankless, using a model built or converted for propane. Since propane runs at different pressure from natural gas, the regulator, line sizing and gas valve must all match, and the supplier may need to swap the tank regulator. Tank size matters too.
