    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Cuyahoga Falls, OH

    Leak Detection and Repair in Cuyahoga Falls, OH

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Cuyahoga Falls

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Cuyahoga Falls

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and often have a separate control panel and solenoid water valve. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. Controls get checked first. A failed solenoid or a tripped overload in the control panel stops the unit just as completely as a jam, and it is a much faster fix.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Cuyahoga Falls, OH

    Jams and minor leaks usually cost far less than a new unit. Once a unit is eight to ten years old, leaking from the body or losing its motor, replacement usually wins, and we price both when it is a close call. The choice is yours.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Cuyahoga Falls

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes. We check the breaker, the reset button and the switch for silent units, and the flywheel and motor windings for humming ones, then decide if a repair makes sense.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Cuyahoga Falls

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Cuyahoga Falls

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Then the object comes out with pliers. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.

    Emergency Disposal Repair in Cuyahoga Falls, OH

    If water is reaching the floor, that comes first. Turn the disposal off, stop the dishwasher if it is running, and close the angle stops under the sink so the faucet cannot add more water to a backed up bowl. Then call us. Most times we stop the leak that day and either fix the disposal or price a new one on the spot.
