    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Cuyahoga Falls, OH

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Leach Field Restoration in Cuyahoga Falls, OH

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A tilted or crushed D-box can send everything to one trench and drown it. Leveling or replacing the box often brings the field back. Some fields are past saving. For those, we explain the design and permit steps.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Cuyahoga Falls
