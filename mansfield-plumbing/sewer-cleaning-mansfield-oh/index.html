    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Mansfield

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Mansfield, OH

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Lead service lines remain in some older neighborhoods, and replacing them is a common recommendation during larger projects. The bottom of the pipe wears first. Because a heavy cutter can punch through weak iron, we use chain descalers and a lower-pressure jet, then check the wall thickness on camera.
