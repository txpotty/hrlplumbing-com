    Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Mansfield, OH

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. So does an extra-green strip of lawn.     Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Mansfield

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The blockage sits downstream of every fixture. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, since a grease clog and a root mass need very different fixes. Older Ohio homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. We record what we find.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Mansfield

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. The full length gets recorded. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, so any excavation stays small and targeted. Older homes in Mansfield often hide two or three problems in one line, and the footage shows every one.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Mansfield

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Mansfield

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Mansfield, OH

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Per foot, lining runs higher. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often make it the cheaper total job. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. We price all three so you can compare.

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Then the sewer smell shows up.
