    Frequently Asked Questions About Residential Plumbing Service in Mansfield

    Pricing depends on the job. A leaky faucet or running toilet usually runs $150 to $350, a water heater installation is often $1,200 to $3,500, and slab leak repairs or full repipes can reach several thousand dollars. The exact number comes after the diagnosis.

    Hard water carries dissolved calcium and magnesium. Soft water has had most of those minerals removed, usually by an ion-exchange softener, and the difference shows up as scale in water heaters, white crust on faucets, spotty dishes and soap that will not lather well. Soft water is easier on fixtures.

    Start at the water meter. Shut off every tap and appliance, then watch the small leak dial, and if it still spins, look for warm spots on the floor, cracks in tile, mildew smell or a water bill that jumped without explanation. Then call for a leak test.

    Most repairs take one to three hours. A leaky pipe or clogged drain is often done in under two, while water heater installs take most of a day and full repipes can stretch over several days depending on the size of the house and access. We give you a timeline up front.

    Soft water and normal pressure prevent a lot of what we see. Grease out of the drains and a yearly pressure test help too, since anything over 80 psi wears out supply hoses and fixtures faster than people expect. An annual check finds small issues early.
