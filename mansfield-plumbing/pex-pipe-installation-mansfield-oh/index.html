    What Goes Into a PEX Job in Mansfield, OH

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Mansfield

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. No crimp tool to calibrate. Because the fittings are wide inside, each joint costs very little flow.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Mansfield

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Both last decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Solutions in Mansfield, OH

    Apartment buildings, small offices and churches in Mansfield, OH are good candidates for PEX as much as houses can. With a manifold and shutoff for each unit, a leak in one apartment only takes that apartment offline, not the whole riser. Fire-rated walls need care. Where PEX passes through a fire-rated assembly, we use listed firestop sleeves or sealant so the rating holds.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Mansfield

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Mansfield, OH

    Price on a repipe depends on a short list: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX almost always comes in below copper because the material is cheaper and a crew can run a house in two to four days instead of a week. Drywall patching may be separate. Ask and we will price all three, PEX-A, PEX-B and copper, side by side.
