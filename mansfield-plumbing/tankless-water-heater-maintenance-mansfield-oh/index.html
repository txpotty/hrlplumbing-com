    Service Valve Kit Installs in Mansfield, OH

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. A pair of isolation valves with purge ports goes on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. Future flushes then take minutes to set up.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Mansfield

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Mansfield

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. Spent media gets replaced. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Mansfield, OH

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. We flush the recirculation loop along with the exchanger. Next we confirm crossover valve or dedicated return, and set the pump schedule to fit.

    Flush Now or Replace the Exchanger Later in Mansfield, OH

    A new heat exchanger can run $600 to $1,500 or more installed, and some manufacturers will not cover one that failed from scale. Descaling costs far less. With yearly care, a tankless unit can last close to 20 years. We can also tell you whether a softener or scale inhibitor would pay off in your house.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Mansfield, OH

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Mansfield

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. The pump pulls solution from a bucket, through the cold port, out the hot port and back again. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Noritz Descaling in Mansfield, OH

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. We use a descaler that the manufacturer approves and keep the cycle inside the time limit in the manual, because too strong a mix can etch the metal. The fan motor and exhaust path get checked while the pump runs.
