    Pool Leak Detection in Mansfield

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation likely has a leak somewhere. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, use an underwater hydrophone to listen, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain to find the exact crack or fitting that is losing water.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Mansfield, OH

    The buried line from your meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. A green stripe in a dry yard is a hint. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, switching to tracer gas on plastic lines that do not carry sound.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Mansfield

    Our trucks carry acoustic correlators and ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Every tool covers a different case. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, while tracer gas catches silent leaks on PEX and PVC.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Mansfield, OH

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. We use a camera for these. Our tech runs a camera from the cleanout to the city tap, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, with a locator giving the exact depth and distance.

    Leak Testing for Multifamily and Offices in Mansfield

    Multifamily buildings and office parks have long supply systems where leaks hide for a long time. We isolate each unit or floor, test it and trace leaks with the same acoustic and thermal tools we use in single-family houses. Property managers get a report per unit.     Preventive Leak Inspections in Mansfield, OH
