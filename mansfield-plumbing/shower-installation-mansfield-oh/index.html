    Curbless Shower Installation in Mansfield

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. A linear drain often works best. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Mansfield, OH

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. On a slab, we cut concrete. The new shower is safer to step into.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Mansfield

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Commercial Shower Installation in Mansfield, OH

    Locker rooms need showers that take hundreds of uses a day. Commercial installs get tamper-resistant trim and heads, plus drains sized for heavy simultaneous use. Scheduling fits your hours. A master mixing valve can protect the whole room from scalding.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Mansfield, OH

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. We plan the layout first. Then we check whether the water heater can keep up.

    Shower Pan Installation in Mansfield

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. We level prefab pans carefully. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Mansfield

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. Valve, head, handheld and drain go in first, then the pan is sloped a quarter inch per foot to the drain. Then we test. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Mansfield, OH

    Budget showers can still be built right. A fiberglass or acrylic unit with a quality pressure-balance valve costs far less than a tiled shower, and when it is set on a mortar bed with a sealed drain, it lasts decades. Spend on the valve. The quote covers everything up front.
