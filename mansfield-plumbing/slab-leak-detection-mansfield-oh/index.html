    How We Find Slab Leaks in Mansfield, OH

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Mansfield, OH

    Supply leaks and drain leaks act differently. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. We test both kinds in your Mansfield, OH home.

    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Mansfield, OH

    Older homes in Mansfield, OH often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both wear out with time. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. We know those failure points. That knowledge points the search at the likeliest spots first.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Mansfield, OH

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. It only works on hot lines. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Mansfield, OH

    A second slab leak is often a sign of more. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, which ends the pattern of new leaks and new floor openings. Most repipes wrap up in two to four days.

    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Mansfield

    Plastic pipe muffles leak noise. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, often through grout joints and hairline cracks. It finds leaks acoustic gear misses in Mansfield homes.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Mansfield

    The hot side fails more than the cold. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, wearing against the gravel and concrete until a pinhole opens. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.

    Slab Leaks in Commercial Buildings in Mansfield

    Commercial slabs are thicker and carry larger pipe, so leaks can move more water and be harder to hear from the surface with a standard ground microphone. Our approach changes for those.
