    What Our Water Softener Maintenance Covers in Mansfield, OH

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Mansfield

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. Raw water gets tested from an unsoftened hose bib or with the valve on bypass. The softened sample comes from a kitchen faucet.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Mansfield, OH

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. The beads turn rusty brown and stop grabbing hardness. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.

    Commercial Twin-Tank Maintenance in Mansfield

    Salons, car washes, hotels and restaurants put far more gallons through a softener than any house does, and a hard-water day can mean spotted glassware or a scaled ice machine. On twin-tank and progressive-flow units, we spend extra time on the meter and the tank switching. We service them every one to three months, depending on volume.

    Repair or Replace a Softener in Mansfield, OH

    New softener systems often run $1,200 to $3,500 installed, while a resin rebed usually lands between $300 and $700. If the tank and valve are in good shape, keeping them makes sense. We recommend a new unit when the valve is obsolete and parts are gone, or when the tank itself is cracked.

    Softener Upkeep for Restaurants and Shops in Mansfield

    Ice machines, espresso machines, dish machines and boilers all scale up fast on hard water, and equipment warranties often require treated water. We test hardness on every visit and log it for your records. Brine tank cleanouts happen more often on commercial units.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Mansfield

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. If it hits a hard layer and then falls through, there is a bridge. We break the crust, pull out the mushy salt underneath with a wet-dry vac, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Mansfield

    Resin does the actual softening. Each bead trades sodium for calcium and magnesium until it runs out of capacity. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to see whether the bed still holds its rated capacity.

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Mansfield, OH

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We advance it manually through each cycle and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.
