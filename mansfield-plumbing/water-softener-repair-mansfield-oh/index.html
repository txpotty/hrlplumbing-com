    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Mansfield, OH

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. O-rings and fittings get replaced and pressure tested. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Mansfield

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Resin Bed Problems in Mansfield

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. New resin restores full capacity.

    Control Valve Repair in Mansfield

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. HRL Plumbing technicians rebuild these valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Timing problems get fixed, whether mechanical or electronic. Parts for the common valves are stocked, which keeps most repairs to one visit.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Mansfield

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. Each part gets tested. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.

    Urgent Water Softener Repair in Mansfield, OH

    Major leaks and total system failure call for a fast response. HRL Plumbing handles urgent softener calls in Mansfield, OH ahead of scheduled maintenance. We bypass the unit so the house keeps water, then repair the valve, the tank or the fitting that failed and caused the leak. Bypass first.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Mansfield, OH

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. We diagnose the valve on site. Seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol valves ride on the truck, so most rebuilds happen on the first visit.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Mansfield, OH

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. HRL Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. You get a clear breakdown.
