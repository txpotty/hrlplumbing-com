    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Springfield, OH

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Springfield, OH.     Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Springfield, OH

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life.     Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Springfield
