    Residential Urinal Installation in Springfield, OH

    Basement and garage bathrooms are where most home urinals go. We tie into the existing drain and vent with a 2 inch line, run a 3/4 or 1/2 inch supply depending on the flush valve, and frame in blocking or a compact carrier behind the wall. Ultra-low-flow models are popular. That barely touches the water bill.

    Home Gym and Garage Urinals in Springfield, OH

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. The drain ties to the nearest stack, a vent or AAV goes in where allowed, and a compact bowl fits the space. Unheated garages need freeze planning. Ask us about the layout.

    ADA Urinal Installation in Springfield

    Accessible urinals start at the carrier. We set the carrier so the finished rim lands at 17 inches or lower, keep the clear floor space free of partitions and door swings, and mount the flush handle or sensor where a seated user can reach it. Measurements get checked twice. Inspectors measure too.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Springfield

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Cleaning staff need a quick lesson. We show them how.

    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in Springfield

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most. Models like the Washbrook and the Bardon come in versions from 0.125 to 1.0 gallons per flush, and each one needs a flush valve matched to that rating or the bowl will either splash or not rinse clean. Matching is simple with the spec sheet. We order both together.

    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Springfield, OH

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. A low-flow bowl and matching valve on the existing carrier cut water use sharply. Ask your water utility about rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Springfield

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Rusted bolts and seized valves often have to be cut, so we protect the tile while working. New gaskets always go in. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Springfield

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. Manual valves go on a 3/4 inch line with the control stop tuned and the vacuum breaker at the correct height. Handle side follows the layout. ADA units need the handle within reach.
