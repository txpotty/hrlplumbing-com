    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Springfield

    Flow rating is the number that matters on a hydromechanical trap, and it has to match the fastest-draining fixture combination in your kitchen. Each sink compartment's volume, the drain size and a one or two minute drain time give us the GPM figure the code uses.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Springfield, OH

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. Skip it and a full sink drain overruns the rating, which sends grease right past the baffles. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.
