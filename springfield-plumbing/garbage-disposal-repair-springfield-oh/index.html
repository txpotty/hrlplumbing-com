    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Springfield, OH

    InSinkErator, Waste King and Moen each use different mounts, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. It means most repairs need only one trip.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Springfield, OH

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard cause most disposal smells.     Most jams and small leaks are much cheaper than a replacement. Once a unit is eight to ten years old, leaking from the body or losing its motor, replacement usually wins, and we price both when it is a close call. You make the call.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Springfield, OH

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed.
