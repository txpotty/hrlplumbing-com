    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Springfield

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. We replace the trap as one kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Springfield

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute.     Garbage Disposal Repair in Springfield

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. From below we turn the flywheel with a 1/4 inch hex key, hit the reset button and look for a bent impeller. A leaking unit is another matter. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Springfield, OH

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall.     Hair Clog Clearing in Springfield
