    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. Bursting or boring works. New HDPE or PEX goes in along the old path by bursting, or along a new one by directional boring, leaving lawns and drives alone. The line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Springfield

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Springfield yard.

    Residential Trenchless Pipe Repair in Springfield, OH

    Pipe Bursting in Springfield, OH

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Springfield

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.
