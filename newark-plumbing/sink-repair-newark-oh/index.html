    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Newark, OH

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A burst supply line can dump gallons a minute, and we answer at night and on weekends with a stocked truck. While you wait, shut off the valves under the sink. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Newark

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Newark, OH

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. We pull the drain and reseal it. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Newark

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Then lay towels down. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Newark, OH

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. In kitchens it is cold grease and food, and in bathrooms it is hair knotted on the stopper and glued with soap scum. We clear it with a hand auger. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Newark, OH

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Newark

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. That points to worn O-rings on the spout or a bad base gasket, and sometimes to a cracked faucet body that cannot be rebuilt. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Newark, OH

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. Bad traps get a new matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Local Sink Repair Near Me in Newark, OH

    Some sink problems look local but are not. A gurgling sink can mean a blocked roof vent, and a sink that backs up when the washer drains points to a clog in the shared line further down. Those get checked before we price anything.

    A local plumber knows the houses. The older parts of town have galvanized stubs and chrome traps, and the newer parts have PEX with plastic traps, so we bring parts for both. That cuts out a supply-house run.
