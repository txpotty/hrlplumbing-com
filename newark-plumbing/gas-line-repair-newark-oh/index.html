    CSST Repair in Newark

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Common damage comes from nails, rodents or lightning, and repairs require cutting out the bad section and using fittings from the same manufacturer. We check bonding on every CSST job. A bonding clamp and conductor tie the CSST to the home's grounding system, following the maker's instructions and code.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Newark

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. This is a quick fix. Ranges, dryers, water heaters and furnaces all need proper connections, and most also need a sediment trap ahead of the appliance gas valve.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Newark, OH

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Newark, OH. The inspector checks pipe sizing, material, support spacing, shutoff locations, sediment traps and the pressure test result before signing off. We handle the paperwork. Doing it this way keeps your insurance clean and gives you a record of the repair if you sell the house later.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Newark, OH

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. After the coating breaks down, corrosion starts, and constant moisture from irrigation or poor drainage makes it worse. We call 811 before digging. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Newark

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Tree roots and shifting clay often break into older clay or cast iron sewer laterals, leading to slow drains and backups. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Newark, OH

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Newark

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. Leaking joints get taken apart, cleaned, resealed with gas-rated compound and tightened, and damaged fittings or valves get replaced. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.
