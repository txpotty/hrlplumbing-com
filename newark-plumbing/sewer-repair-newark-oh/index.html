    Sewer Line Repair Jobs We Handle in Newark, OH

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Newark, OH

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line with a jetter first. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Newark

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. We still answer. An after-hours call gets a tech with a cable machine and a camera, which is enough to restore flow in most cases and to find out whether the trouble is a blockage or a structural break. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. Daylight digs are safer around buried utilities.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Newark

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Newark, OH

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Both save driveways and mature trees. We tell you which one your pipe can take.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Newark, OH

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Basements are standard in most homes, and many rely on sump pumps to handle groundwater away during wet periods. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Newark, OH

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. A closed kitchen loses revenue by the hour. For restaurants, strip centers and apartment buildings in Newark we schedule repairs after hours where we can, set up temporary bypass pumping when the line has to stay in service, and coordinate with property managers on tenant notices. Grease trap outlet lines are one of our most common commercial repairs.

    Emergency Sewer Repair in Newark

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. We answer at night and on weekends. The first goal on an emergency call is to get the line flowing, usually with a cable or jetter, and then camera it to find out whether you have a clog or a structural failure that will back up again tomorrow. Sometimes the permanent fix waits for daylight. In the meantime, we flag which drains to avoid.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Newark, OH

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. Cut roots regrow within a year or two. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and show you whether a liner or a spot repair would stop the cycle for good. Mature trees in Newark yards make this a common call.
