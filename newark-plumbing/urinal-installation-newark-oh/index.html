    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Newark, OH

    New urinals usually need a permit. Adding a fixture means new drain, vent and supply work that most cities inspect at rough-in and again at final, and commercial jobs often need plans that show fixture counts and accessible layouts. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Newark

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. ADA units need a rim at or below 17 inches, a 13-1/2 inch projection and 30 by 48 inches of clear floor. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in Newark

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most. Washbrook and Bardon models come in several flush ratings, and the valve has to match the bowl. Matching is simple with the spec sheet. We order both together.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Newark

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Newark

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Rusted bolts and seized valves often have to be cut, so we protect the tile while working. New gaskets always go in. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Urinal Brands We Install in Newark, OH

    Brand choice is mostly about parts support. We recommend brands whose valves and cartridges are easy to get locally, so repairs never wait. We install all of them. Spec sheets come first.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Newark, OH

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Newark

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. We remove the flush valve, cap the supply and set the waterless bowl on the same carrier. Some codes want the supply left in place. That allows a future change back.
