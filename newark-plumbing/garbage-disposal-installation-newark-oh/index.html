    We help you pick between the Badger and Evolution lines based on how you cook, how much noise bothers you and how much cabinet room you have.     Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Newark

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Newark, OH

No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. They are slower to use. Families with small kids often like the trade.
