    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Mentor, OH

    Much of Ohio has hard water, which leaves scale in water heaters, clogs aerators and cuts the life of appliances. Scale grows inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods until the linkage seizes. Forcing a scaled lift rod usually snaps the pivot and leaves the stopper stuck, with a narrowed tailpiece slowing the drain. The scaled parts all come out. Smooth PVC sheds buildup better.

    P-Trap Replacement in Mentor

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most. They rust inside and their nuts seize, so we cut them out and fit heavy-wall PVC or polished brass. Then it gets a full flow test. Total time is often under an hour.

    Emergency Sink Drain Service in Mentor

    A trap that falls apart sends a sink of water into the cabinet. Turn off the faucet, set a bucket under it, and stop using that sink, and if the drain is backed up into a second fixture, stop using water elsewhere in the house too. A plumber comes out with trap kits. Most get fixed for good on the first visit.

    Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Mentor

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. If your laundry or utility sink sits below the sewer line, its drain runs to a sink pump or a sewage ejector, and we check the check valve and the discharge line when we replace the trap. Pumps fail quietly. A gurgling pit is a clue.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Mentor

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. Mismatched drains are common. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Mentor

    Traps leak at the joints first. The beveled washers inside the slip nuts flatten and harden over the years, and a trap that has been taken apart several times to clear clogs often has stripped plastic threads or a cracked nut. Trap kits are cheap. Labor is most of the bill.

    Corroded Drain Pipe Replacement in Mentor, OH

    Galvanized drain arms rust from the inside. An old steel stub can crumble at the threads, so we cut it back and join new PVC with a shielded coupling. We handle cast iron the same way. Sometimes that means opening the wall.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Mentor, OH

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Caustic products sit in the bottom of a metal trap between uses and eat at the chrome plating and the thin brass underneath, which is why so many old traps we pull have pinholes right at the bottom of the bend. PVC does not have that problem. We recommend it for most cabinets.
