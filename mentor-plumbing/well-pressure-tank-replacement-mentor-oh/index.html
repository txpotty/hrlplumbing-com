    What Goes Into a Well Tank Swap in Mentor, OH

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Mentor

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Mentor, OH

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. A pitted switch can stick on or chatter. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, and we usually recommend replacing a switch older than the tank it served. Cheap part.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Mentor

    Rapid clicking at the switch is the classic sign. That fast on-off pattern means the tank has no usable air, and the pump is now taking every hit of demand directly, which wears the pump and the switch contacts. We swap the tank and the cycling stops.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Mentor, OH

    Well-Rite tanks use a replaceable diaphragm design in many models. We install them in the common residential sizes. Like any tank, the key is setting pre-charge before it fills, and checking that drawdown matches the pump. A good tank installed badly fails early.

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Mentor

    Old galvanized tanks have no bladder at all. Water sits right against an air pocket. Over time the water absorbs the air, the tank waterlogs, and someone has to drain and recharge it every few months. A sealed bladder tank ends that chore.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Mentor, OH

    Once a year, test the pre-charge. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, then add air with a small compressor if it has dropped. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Mentor

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. Fittings tend to line up. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, since many original tanks were undersized from the start. Check before swapping.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Mentor

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. Replacing just the tank can expose other weak spots. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, so you can choose a tank-only swap or a fuller refresh. Your call.
