    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Mentor, OH

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Mentor

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Mentor

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Mentor, OH

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HRL Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Water Softener Replacement in Mentor, OH
