    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Mentor

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Plan on a few days for a typical house.     PEX Pipe Installation in Mentor

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. It resists scale and corrosion. Private wells serve many rural homes, and iron, sulfur and hardness are common issues that call for filtration or softening. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.
