    What We Replace on Well Systems in Mentor, OH

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Mentor

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. Depending on depth and pump weight, it gets replaced with schedule 120 PVC or poly. Wire gets the same look. Nicked jackets and old tape splices go.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Mentor

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. Short cycling, where the pump starts every few seconds of water use, usually means the tank bladder has failed and the pump is paying for it. A tire gauge on the air valve tells us a lot. Precharge should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Mentor

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. Starters and overloads get replaced along with the pump. We schedule around watering times.

    What a Well Pump Job Costs in Mentor, OH

    A well pump job usually runs a few thousand dollars, and the pump itself is often less than half of that. Labor and hoist time, along with new wire and pipe, make up the rest. Every line is on the quote.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Mentor, OH

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. New pump, primed and tested. After that, we watch the pressure gauge cycle from cut-in to cut-out.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Mentor, OH

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. Air sneaks in through a pinhole in the suction line, the pump loses prime and the seal burns up running dry. The suction line gets a pressure test. If the well is too deep for a jet pump, we will say so and quote a submersible.

    No-Water Calls in Mentor

    Pump trouble rarely gives notice. One morning the faucet coughs air and then nothing, and by then the motor has often been struggling for weeks. Before we pull the pump, we test the switch and control box.

    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Mentor

    Pump and tank work as a pair. An undersized or waterlogged tank forces dozens of extra starts a day, and each start puts heat into the motor windings. We check it on every pump job.
