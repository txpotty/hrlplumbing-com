    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Mentor

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease and wipes cause most of it.     Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Mentor

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Mentor

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. A liner or new pipe at that joint is what actually stops regrowth.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Mentor

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.
