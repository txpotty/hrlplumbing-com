    Pipe Bursting Services in Mentor, OH

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. The new line takes its place. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, and fused HDPE follows right behind it, often at the same size or one size larger than the original. Collapsed sections are fine. Lining cannot do that.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Mentor

    Trenchless repair replaces or relines buried pipe without opening its full length. Two small pits, usually. We can pull new PEX or HDPE through the old pipe path or line a sewer lateral with resin, so concrete and established plantings stay put. Not every pipe qualifies. The route and condition decide it.

    Underground Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Mentor, OH
