    Why Choose HRL Plumbing for Toilet Installation in Mentor, OH?

    Our plumbers check the flange before they even unbox your new toilet. If the ring is cracked or rusted, it gets replaced right then. Setting a new toilet on a bad flange is the most common install mistake we see, and it shows up months later as a stained ceiling below or a soft spot in the floor around the base. We would rather fix it the first day.

    • Correct Rough-In, We measure 10, 12 or 14 inch rough-ins from the finished wall before the toilet is ordered.
    • Clean Work, Drop cloths down, old toilet bagged, and the bathroom cleaned before we leave.
    • Level Mounting, A shimmed, steady bowl keeps its seal and does not rock when you sit.

    Citywide Coverage

    Distance across Mentor does not change how we set a toilet. The flange gets checked every time. A condo near downtown and a house on the edge of town both get stainless closet bolts and a five-flush test before we pack up the truck.

    Our plumbers have set toilets on concrete slabs, wood subfloors, heated floors and thick mortar beds. Each one needs a different anchor. On a slab we use concrete screws, and on wood we use stainless screws driven into solid subfloor, not tile.

    Types of Toilet Installation in Mentor, OH

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Mentor

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. The tank sits behind an access plate. Because the carrier carries all the weight, we anchor its feet to the slab or framing first and check it for plumb before the drywall or tile goes up. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Mentor

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill. Manual and sensor valves go on both floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, with the control stop adjusted so the flush is full but does not splash. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Customer-Supplied Toilet Installation in Mentor, OH

    Bought the toilet yourself? Check the rough-in printed on the box first. Before the old toilet comes out, we inspect the new one for shipping cracks, because cracked china goes back to the store, not onto your flange. We bring the seal and stainless bolts.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Mentor

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Every brand has its quirks. Some one-piece TOTO models mount with a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler toilets use a tank-to-bowl gasket that most hardware stores do not carry, so we stock both. We keep those parts on hand.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Mentor, OH

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Check for the WaterSense label. A MaP rating of 800 grams or better is what we look for, since a weak low-flow toilet that needs double flushing saves no water at all. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    Commercial Restroom Toilets in Mentor, OH

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. Downtime costs a business money. Work gets scheduled before opening or after close, with fixtures staged on site, so each stall is out of service only briefly. We work with your GC on inspections.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Mentor, OH

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most require a GFCI outlet within reach of the cord. The water connection and mounting are ours, and when an outlet has to be added we schedule a licensed electrician instead of doing that wiring. Then we test every button.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Mentor, OH

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Consider who uses the bathroom first. Kids and shorter adults may not like their feet off the floor, but people with sore knees or a new hip usually love the extra height. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.
