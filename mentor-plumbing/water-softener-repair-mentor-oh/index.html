    Hard Water Symptoms and Diagnostics in Mentor

    Scale on faucets means trouble. HRL Plumbing checks hardness at the tap, looks at each part of the system and gives you a repair plan for your Mentor home. We test hardness before and after the repair, so you see the grains per gallon fall.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Mentor, OH

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. The valve gets tested in place. Our plumbers carry seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol heads, which covers a big share of the softeners installed in homes around here.

    Water Softener Leak Detection and Repair in Mentor, OH

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. Even a slow drip wastes water and soaks the floor. We find the source, whether it is the bypass, the valve-to-tank seal or the drain connection, and replace the seal or fitting that failed. We test it under pressure.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Mentor

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Mentor

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used. We retest hardness and reprogram the valve, or upgrade the head to a metered control that only regenerates when the resin is actually close to exhausted. Salt bills drop.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Mentor, OH

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. If scale comes back or pressure drops, the media or the prefilter is usually the problem. HRL Plumbing repairs these units in Mentor, OH. The media wears out in a few years.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Mentor, OH

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A hard crust has formed and is keeping the salt from dissolving. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. Pellet salt bridges less in warm or humid spaces, so we may suggest switching if your softener sits in a hot garage.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Mentor

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. Fresh resin brings capacity back.
