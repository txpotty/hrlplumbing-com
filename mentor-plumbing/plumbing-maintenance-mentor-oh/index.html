    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspection in Mentor, OH

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Mentor

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. A small pump circulates descaler. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Mentor

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Yearly tests are often required, and our certified testers check each valve and file results. We repair failures. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Late tests can mean fines.

    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Mentor, OH
