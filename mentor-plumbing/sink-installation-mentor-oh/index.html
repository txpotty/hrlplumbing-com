    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Mentor, OH

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Setting the faucet and drain while the bowl is on sawhorses is faster and seals better than working in the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Mentor

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Mentor, OH

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. Undermounts cost more for clips and cure time, and vessels add a special drain and a taller faucet. Moving the drain adds the most. We price each piece on the quote.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Mentor, OH

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Mentor, OH

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Mentor

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. We tie both drains into the wall with either two separate traps or a continuous waste to one trap, depending on the spacing, and add a new pair of stops for the second faucet. Vent sizing gets checked. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.
