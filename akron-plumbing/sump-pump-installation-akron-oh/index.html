    Sump Pump Replacement in Akron, OH

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. Undersized pumps get upsized. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Akron

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Akron

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Akron, OH

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Akron

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. Downtime costs money. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Akron, OH

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Akron

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Rentals need these. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Akron

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. The slab gets patched with new concrete. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.
