    How We Install Showers in Akron, OH

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in Akron

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. Commercial valves with metered or push-button controls keep run times in check, and the drains are sized for heavy use. Accessible stalls get handhelds on slide bars. Work is phased so part of the room stays open.

    Shower Stall Installation in Akron

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. Then the drain goes in. We check the flow at the head once the trim is on.

    Emergency Shower Replacement in Akron

    A cracked pan or a failed valve can force a replacement fast. When water is getting under the floor or into a ceiling below, we pull the damaged unit, dry what we can and install a standard stall so the bathroom is usable again within a day or two. Custom work can wait. The framing gets inspected first.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Akron, OH

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Handles and the head come later. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Akron

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. The circuit is an electrician's job. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

    Shower Pan Installation in Akron

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. We level prefab pans carefully. Either way, the slope runs to the drain.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Akron

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Then we test. We pressure-test the valve and flood the pan for a day before tile.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Akron

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Pair a rain head with a handheld and body sprays and a 40-gallon water heater may run cold halfway through. The water heater gets sized up. Thermostatic valves with volume controls run the outlets.
