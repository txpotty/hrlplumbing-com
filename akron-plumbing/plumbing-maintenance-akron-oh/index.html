    Hot Water Tank Service in Akron, OH

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Both get flushed. Mineral buildup on the bottom wastes fuel and can lead to early tank failure.

    Softener Upkeep in Akron, OH

    Softeners fail quietly. A unit that stopped regenerating or ran out of salt keeps sending water to the house, it just is not soft anymore, and you will not notice until scale shows up on the faucets and the water heater starts popping. We check the bypass and drain line. We measure hardness after.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Akron

    Tankless units need descaling. Hard water scales the heat exchanger until the unit runs lukewarm or shuts off mid-shower. We pump descaler through the service valves. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Repipe Assessments in Akron, OH

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more. We identify the pipe type, look for signs of failure and tell you whether repiping is urgent or can wait. We quote PEX and copper. Repiping can be done in stages.
