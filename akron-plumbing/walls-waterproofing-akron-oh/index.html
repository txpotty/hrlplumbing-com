    Waterproofing takes away the moisture mold needs to grow. Existing mold should be removed and the area dried before any sealer goes on, and for larger growth, generally more than about ten square feet, we recommend a remediation company. Small patches we cut out and replace ourselves.

    Done right with good materials, a waterproofing system lasts 20 to 50 years.     A shower wall job usually takes two to four days from demolition to finished membrane, counting the drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing often wraps up in a day. Bigger foundation jobs take several days, sometimes more if it rains.

    Look low on the wall first. Damp patches, a musty smell, mold on the bottom of the drywall, a white powder on block or brick called efflorescence, and puddles after rain all point to water coming through. Bowing walls and stair-step cracks need a closer look too.

    Check the basement after a heavy rain. Wet spots on the walls, mold or mildew, a musty smell in the crawl space, new foundation cracks, efflorescence on the concrete or standing water on the floor all mean water is getting in somewhere. Those signs rarely go away on their own.
