    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Akron, OH

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Sloan Flush Valve Installation in Akron, OH

    Sloan Royal valves are on most commercial urinals we see. We install them with the correct inside parts kit for the bowl's rated volume, set the control stop so the flush is strong without splashing, and keep rebuild kits on the truck for later service. Optima sensor valves fit the same spud. Battery models need no wiring.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in Akron

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. We set vitreous china units on floor-mounted carriers, connect them with new outlet gaskets and stainless bolts, and pair them with a Sloan or Zurn flush valve sized to the bowl. Partitions get spaced to code. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Akron

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. Carrier bolts get inspected first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    ADA Urinal Installation in Akron

    Accessible urinals start at the carrier. The carrier gets set for a 17 inch rim, the floor space stays clear, and the flush control stays reachable. We measure the rim twice. Inspectors measure too.

    Urinal Rough-In for New Restrooms in Akron

    Tenant improvements need rough-in before the walls close. We space urinals 30 inches apart and 15 inches from side walls, then set carriers and run all the lines. We stay in touch with the GC daily. Rough inspection comes first.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Akron, OH

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. The drain is usually 2 inch, the trap is built into the china, the fixture needs a vent within code distance, and the flush valve needs either a 3/4 inch supply or a lower-flow residential valve on 1/2 inch. Permit rules apply too. We check that first.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Akron

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. The flush handle has to be easy to reach. We mark it all during rough-in.
