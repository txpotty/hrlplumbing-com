    Leak Repair and Slab Leak Service in Akron

    Faucet and Fixture Repair in Akron

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet.     Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Akron

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Basements are standard in most homes, and many rely on sump pumps to handle groundwater away during wet periods. Our techs find them with ground microphones and thermal imaging, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. After that, you choose the repair. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute through the attic.
