    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Akron

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Akron

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, so any excavation stays small and targeted. Older homes in Akron often hide two or three problems in one line, and the video catches all of them.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Akron, OH

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. Cut roots regrow within a year or two. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and show you whether a liner or a spot repair would stop the cycle for good. Mature trees in Akron yards make this a common call.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Akron, OH

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Per foot, lining runs higher. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often make it the cheaper total job. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. You get each option in writing with a number attached.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Akron, OH

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Sewer gas follows soon after. Bird nests, leaves and ice can block a roof vent, and older galvanized or cast iron vent stacks crack inside the walls where nobody sees them, which is why we look at the vent whenever the lateral checks out fine. We clear it from the roof or replace the damaged section.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Akron

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease and wipes cause most of it. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, including after-hours digs and bypass pumping when the business has to stay open. A two-way cleanout usually goes in at the same time.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Akron, OH

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Akron

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they keep growing until the joint splits or the pipe cracks open. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. A liner or new pipe at that joint is what actually stops regrowth.
