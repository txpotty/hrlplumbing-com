    Each Kind of Sink We Install in Akron, OH

    Undermount Sink Installation in Akron

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Adhesive by itself will not hold it. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Utility Sink Installation in Akron

    Laundry and garage tubs take dirty water and a lot of it. The tub goes on a solid stand or bracket with a 1-1/2 inch trap, plus a faucet tall enough to fill a bucket. Homeowners around Akron often install a softener to protect water heaters and reduce spotting on fixtures. A cleanout plug goes on the trap.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Akron, OH

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. For an accessible bathroom we set the rim no higher than 34 inches, leave knee clearance underneath, and wrap or shield the hot water and drain pipes so a seated user cannot touch them. Offset drains help with knee space. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Akron

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. An even reveal around a polished cutout takes shims and patience before the clips are tightened. Uneven reveals look bad forever. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Akron

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. The basin itself hangs on a bracket lagged into 2x blocking behind the drywall, and if the old wall has no blocking we open it, add a board between the studs, and patch it back. Drywall anchors do not cut it. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Restaurant and Office Sink Installation in Akron

    Breakroom and prep sinks get used hard. Stainless bowls with commercial faucets go in strong enough for a full bowl and a stack of dishes. We schedule around when you are open. Night installs are possible.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Akron, OH

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Mounting the faucet and the pop-up on a sink that is still on sawhorses takes a fraction of the time and gets a tighter seal than working upside down inside the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Akron, OH

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. Undermounts cost more for clips and cure time, and vessels add a special drain and a taller faucet. Relocating the drain is the biggest add. We price each piece on the quote.
