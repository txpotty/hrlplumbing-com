    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Akron, OH

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements.     Ejector Pump Replacement in Akron

    Ejector pumps tend to run 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Signs of trouble include a pump that runs constantly, a grinding or humming noise, an alarm that keeps tripping and sewage odor near the pit, and any of those means it is time to call before the basement floor tells you. The float usually goes first.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Akron, OH

    Businesses in Akron, OH with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. A duplex system with an alternating panel is common, so if one pump fails the other keeps things running. Downtime costs money. We size both pumps to carry the full load.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Akron

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. Never mix the two.

    Emergency Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Akron

    An ejector failure usually announces itself with the alarm, followed by slow drains and a smell near the pit. Shut off water to the basement fixtures, keep everyone from flushing below grade, and call us, since most emergency pump replacements are done in a few hours once the plumber is on site. Trucks stock popular models.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Akron

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Akron, OH

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Matched packages speed up the install, and Liberty's float design is less likely to snag on the basin wall, a common reason older pumps fail to start. Switch failures cause many calls.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Akron

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. Grinders run pricier. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.
