    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Akron

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Akron

    A gravity interceptor works on time, not machinery. The tank holds water for roughly half an hour, which gives grease time to rise and food solids time to drop. We size by fixture count and retention time, then match the tank to what the local pretreatment office for your building will accept. No moving parts to fail.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Akron

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. A trap that sits out of level loses capacity, so we shim it flat before connecting anything.
