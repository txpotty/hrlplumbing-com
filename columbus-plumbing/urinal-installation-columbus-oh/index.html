    Waterless Urinal Installation in Columbus

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Cleaning staff need a quick lesson. We show them how.

    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in Columbus

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most. Washbrook and Bardon models come in several flush ratings, and the valve has to match the bowl. The spec sheet makes matching easy. We order both together.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Columbus, OH

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. A replacement on an existing carrier with good supply and drain lines costs far less than a new install that needs a carrier, a new 2 inch drain and a 3/4 inch supply run through finished walls. Sensor valves add a few hundred. We itemize each piece.

    Urinal Brands We Install in Columbus, OH

    Brand choice is mostly about parts support. American Standard, Kohler, Zurn and Sloan all make reliable urinals, and we steer customers toward models whose flush valves and cartridges are stocked at local supply houses so a repair never waits on shipping. We install all of them. Spec sheets come first.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in Columbus

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. We set vitreous china units on floor-mounted carriers, connect them with new outlet gaskets and stainless bolts, and pair them with a Sloan or Zurn flush valve sized to the bowl. Partitions get spaced to code. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    Home Gym and Garage Urinals in Columbus, OH

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. The drain ties to the nearest stack, a vent or AAV goes in where allowed, and a compact bowl fits the space. Freeze protection matters in garages. Ask us about the layout.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Columbus, OH

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. We install battery or hardwired units, set the sensing range so the valve does not fire when someone walks past, and turn on a sanitary flush cycle if the restroom sits empty for long stretches. Expect years between battery changes. Hardwired units need an electrician first.

    Sloan Flush Valve Installation in Columbus, OH

    Sloan Royal valves are on most commercial urinals we see. Each Sloan Royal gets the right inside parts for the bowl, a tuned control stop and a rebuild kit on hand. Optima sensor valves fit the same spud. Battery models need no wiring.
