    Picking a Tankless for Your Columbus, OH House

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Columbus, OH

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Navien A models and Rinnai units with internal pumps can recirculate through a return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. The pump runs on a timer. Usually just the morning and evening peaks.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Columbus, OH

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. We handle that conversion. Most of the plumbing lines up already.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Columbus

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. We check meter capacity and pipe length against the manufacturer's sizing chart, then confirm pressure with the unit running. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Columbus, OH

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. One bad unit can come out while the others keep running. Health code sets the hot water temperature at the hand sinks and dish areas.

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Columbus

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We still check sizing against the new model. Old service valves often get replaced too.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Columbus, OH

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. The catch is gas, since most tankless units need a 3/4-inch line or bigger plus new venting. Before quoting, we check the meter and pipe run, and afterward we cap the old flue and seal the roof. Plan on a full day.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Columbus, OH

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Columbus, OH

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. Expect a bigger gas line, a new vent run and a wall-mounted spot for the heater. The old flue gets capped.
