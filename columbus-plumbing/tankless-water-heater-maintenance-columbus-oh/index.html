    What Our On-Demand Maintenance Covers in Columbus, OH

    Home Tankless Flushing in Columbus

    Much of Ohio has hard water, which leaves scale in water heaters, clogs aerators and shortens the life of appliances. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. The flow returns. Temperatures stop swinging, and the unit quits shutting down on high-limit.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Columbus, OH

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in Columbus, OH

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. Gas pressure gets checked with every burner lit, not at idle. We pull and clean the inlet screens and air filters. Downtime is the real cost. We can book it before you open or after you close.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Columbus

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Columbus

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent pipe, pitched back toward the heater so condensate drains. Every joint gets checked, and we test for carbon monoxide by the heater. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Columbus, OH

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around Columbus, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. The manufacturer's steps come first, not a generic checklist. Warranty claims later can hinge on it.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Columbus

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. We soap-test the gas connections and try the pressure relief valve. We pull the air filter and check the igniter. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Columbus

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is your heat exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.
