    Sewer Repair Methods for Columbus, OH Homes

    Emergency Sewer Repair in Columbus

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. Our phone is answered after hours. The first goal on an emergency call is to get the line flowing, usually with a cable or jetter, and then camera it to find out whether you have a clog or a structural failure that will back up again tomorrow. Sometimes the permanent fix waits for daylight. Until then, we tell you which fixtures are safe to use.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Columbus, OH

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. Every drain backs up. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and the camera tells us exactly where the pipe gave way and how deep it sits. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Columbus, OH

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Columbus, OH

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Heavy clay soils are common in Ohio, and they hold water and shift with the seasons, stressing foundations and sewer lines. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and reset the slope to the quarter inch per foot most codes call for on residential laterals. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Columbus

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. We still answer. An after-hours call gets a tech with a cable machine and a camera, which is enough to restore flow in most cases and to find out whether the trouble is a blockage or a structural break. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. We avoid digging blind at night near gas and electric lines.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Columbus

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Columbus

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. The full length gets recorded. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, which keeps the dig to one small pit. Older homes in Columbus often hide two or three problems in one line, and the video catches all of them.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection & Repair in Columbus, OH

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. So does an extra-green strip of lawn. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and mark the location so the repair stays small. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, with no digging at all. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.
