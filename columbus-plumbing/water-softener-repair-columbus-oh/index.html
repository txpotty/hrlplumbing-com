    Resin Testing and Replacement in Columbus

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Columbus, OH

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Columbus

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. We test each part. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.
