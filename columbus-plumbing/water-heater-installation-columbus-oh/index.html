    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Columbus, OH

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. Upfront cost is higher. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Columbus

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with.     Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Columbus, OH

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Columbus

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. That protects your coverage.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Columbus

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Licensing and permit rules can differ by city and county, so it pays to hire a plumber who knows local requirements. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Columbus, OH

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Our crews set the gas line, the drip leg and the vent connector, then do a draft test at the hood to prove the exhaust is leaving the house. Leak checks follow. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Columbus, OH

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. Shut the cold supply valve on top of the tank and turn the gas knob to pilot or flip the breaker, then call us. We carry common sizes. Same-day replacement is normal.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Columbus, OH

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. No venting needed. That makes placement easier.
