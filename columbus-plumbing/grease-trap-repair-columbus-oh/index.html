    Grease Trap Repairs We Handle in Columbus, OH

    Flow Control Device Repair in Columbus

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Without it, a full sink dump pushes grease straight through. Inspectors look for it. The air intake gets cleared too, because the fitting needs air to work.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Columbus, OH

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling. Older steel bodies rust out along the bottom seams, too. We dry the area, fill the trap and watch where the water shows up. Sometimes it is a $15 gasket. If the seam itself has opened, patching rarely holds and we will tell you so before you pay for it.

    Residential Grease Trap Repair in Columbus, OH

    Homes rarely need a grease trap, but some have one, usually a small plastic unit under the kitchen sink on a house with a septic system or a home bakery. These leak at the lid or the threaded inlet. We replace the gasket, reset the fittings with fresh thread sealant, and clear any grease plug in the outlet. Small job, usually under an hour.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Columbus, OH

    Outdoor risers take a beating from garbage trucks and the occasional forklift. Once a riser cracks, rainwater and grit wash straight into the tank. We replace damaged risers and set traffic-rated covers, H-20 rated where vehicles cross, and seal the riser joints with butyl rope so the tank stays tight. Pedestrian lids in a drive lane are a liability.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Columbus, OH

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. On the inlet side, the tee directs incoming water down under the floating grease. The outlet tee draws water from low in the tank, well under the grease cap, so if it breaks off or someone removes it during pumping, floating grease walks right out to the sewer. New tees go in as Schedule 40 PVC, cut to the drop length the manufacturer or local code specifies.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Columbus, OH

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. Once cracked, it leaks smell out and lets mop water into the trap. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. While the lid is off, we look at the baffles too.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Columbus, OH

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. Once a steel unit has rust holes in the body, it is finished. A plastic or fiberglass trap with a broken baffle is usually worth fixing, and a concrete interceptor with sound walls and a bad riser almost always is, since replacing a buried tank means excavation and several days closed. We put both options in writing with a price on each.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Columbus, OH

    Lines downstream of the trap should stay clean if the trap is working. When we find grease past the outlet, the trap is not doing its job. We jet the downstream run to the property line or the sewer tap, then camera it to confirm the pipe is clear and to document any cracked or sagging sections for a repair quote. Clearing the pipe without fixing the trap just resets the clock.
