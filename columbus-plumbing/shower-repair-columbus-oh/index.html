    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Columbus, OH

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Moen Posi-Temp showers take the 1222, and Delta Monitor 13 and 14 showers take the RP46074, and both wear out on a fairly regular clock. We stock both. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Columbus

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. We replace the cartridge. If the valve is too old for parts, we talk through a replacement valve and what it means for the tile.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Columbus

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. We swap in a new one. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Columbus

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. When that happens, water goes under the shower instead of down the pipe, and the first sign is often a soft spot in the floor next to the shower or a stain in the ceiling below. The drain gets cleared and tested. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Columbus, OH

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. We start with the head. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Columbus, OH

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. Each part gets tested. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Columbus

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. The trim comes off first. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Columbus, OH

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Shut the main, or use the shower's integral stops if your valve has them behind the trim or an access panel. Next, call us. HRL Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.
