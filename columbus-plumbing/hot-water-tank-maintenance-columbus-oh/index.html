    What a Tank Tune-Up Covers in Columbus, OH

    Burner and Pilot Service in Columbus, OH

    On a gas tank, a dirty burner throws a lazy yellow flame and leaves soot on the flue baffle. We pull the burner assembly and brush it clean. A tired thermocouple drops the pilot, so we test it too. The goal is a steady blue flame.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Columbus, OH

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not. Much of Ohio has hard water, which leaves scale in water heaters, clogs aerators and cuts the life of appliances. We break the hex head loose, pull the rod and check how much bare wire shows. Six inches of bare wire means replace it.

    Relief Valve Testing in Columbus

    Temperature and pressure relief valves on residential tanks are typically rated to open at 150 psi or 210 degrees, and they can stick shut after years of mineral buildup. Our tech lifts the lever, lets water flow, then watches it close. A valve that drips afterward gets replaced. We also confirm the discharge pipe runs full size to within six inches of the floor or to an approved drain, which is what plumbing codes ask for.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Columbus

    Street pressure over 80 psi is hard on every fixture in the house, and plumbing codes generally call for a pressure reducing valve above that number. We check static pressure at a hose bib. The relief valve lever gets lifted next. If the valve opens and reseats cleanly and the expansion tank holds its charge, the tank is safe from pressure spikes.

    Standard Tank Tune-Up in Columbus

    A standard tune-up starts with the fuel off and the cold supply closed, then a full drain through the bottom valve until the water runs clear. Figure on half an hour or a bit more for the drain. We check the heating elements or burner, read the thermostat setting and look at every fitting on top of the tank for green crust or drips. Any weeping at the dielectric nipples goes on the report.

    Gas Venting Tune-Up in Columbus, OH

    Atmospheric gas tanks vent by draft alone, so the vent connector needs upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot and tight joints with three screws each. Draft at the hood gets checked first. Lint on the flame arrestor of an FVIR tank can starve the burner and kill the pilot. We vacuum it clean.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Columbus

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. The harder the water, the sooner it matters. We drain, partly refill and drain again until the hose runs clear, sometimes four or five rounds. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

Each plan is a fixed yearly rate for one or two visits, priority booking and a parts discount. Homes on well water or very hard water usually do better with the twice-a-year option.
