    Each Kind of Sink We Install in Columbus, OH

    Vessel Sink Installation in Columbus, OH

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Columbus

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Columbus

    A cracked bowl cannot be patched for long. When a bowl cracks or a drain rusts through, we cap the lines first so the house keeps water, then set a new sink. Common sizes can go in the same day. Odd cutouts take longer.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Columbus, OH

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. The bowl gets filled and drained while we watch every nut under the sink with a light and a dry towel. A towel shows a weep fast. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Utility Sink Installation in Columbus

    Laundry and garage tubs take dirty water and a lot of it. The tub goes on a solid stand or bracket with a 1-1/2 inch trap, plus a faucet tall enough to fill a bucket. Much of Ohio has hard water, which leaves scale in water heaters, clogs aerators and cuts the life of appliances. We add a cleanout plug.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Columbus

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. Uneven reveals look bad forever. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Columbus, OH

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. Since the trap and stops are right behind the column, we center the drain and use chrome parts that look finished. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.
