    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Columbus

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open the pipe end and let it shrink back over the fitting for a full-bore joint. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Most homes are repiped with PEX-A in two to three days.

    PEX Repiping in Columbus, OH

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. Our PEX-A installs use expansion fittings that do not shrink the flow path. Flow stays strong at the last shower.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Columbus, OH

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe.     Slab Leak Repiping in Columbus

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Tree roots and shifting clay often crack older clay or cast iron sewer laterals, leading to slow drains and backups. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Columbus, OH

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.
