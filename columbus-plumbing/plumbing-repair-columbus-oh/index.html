    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Columbus, OH

    A warm spot on tile, a meter that spins with every tap off, or a bill that doubles for no reason. Those are slab leak signs.     Sewer Line Repair in Columbus, OH

    Roots are the usual cause of a sewer line that backs up on a regular schedule. They find a cracked joint, grow inside and catch paper and grease. We camera the line to see the damage and mark the depth on the surface, then choose between an open dig and a trenchless method. Clay pipe usually goes first.

    Slab Leak Repair for Columbus, OH Homes

    Tree roots and shifting clay often crack older clay or cast iron sewer laterals, leading to slow drains and backups. Lines under a slab take the brunt of that movement, and Ohio homeowners often find out from a warm floor or a spinning meter. HRL Plumbing finds the leak with electronic equipment before any concrete is cut. Then the choice is a spot repair through the floor or a reroute that abandons the old line. Reroutes skip the jackhammer.
