    How We Find Slab Leaks in Columbus, OH

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Columbus, OH

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Columbus, OH home.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Columbus, OH

    Drain lines under the slab can leak too. A broken cast iron or PVC drain will not move the water meter, but it can cause odors, soggy floors and slow drains, which is why we push a camera through to look for cracks, bellies and pulled joints. You see the footage yourself before any repair talk starts in your Columbus home.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Columbus

    Rerouting leaves the failed pipe in the slab. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, which avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than opening a finished floor. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.

    Urgent Slab Leak Calls in Columbus, OH

    A slab leak that is soaking carpet or pushing water up through tile needs attention the same day. Shut off the main. HRL Plumbing sends a tech to Columbus, OH homes quickly, isolates the failed side and gets the rest of the house back on water while the repair gets planned.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Columbus

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or recommend a reroute that never touches the concrete.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Columbus

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time. That tells us where to look. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Columbus, OH home.

    Direct Access Slab Leak Repair in Columbus, OH

    A single leak on sound pipe can be fixed in place. We cut a small opening at the marked spot, dig down to the pipe, repair the section with approved fittings and pressure test it. Then we backfill and patch the concrete level, ready for flooring. A precise mark keeps the opening tight.
