    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Columbus

    Electric water heaters in Columbus usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. Tank leaks are the end, so we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to your existing circuit. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Columbus, OH

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Columbus, OH homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Columbus, OH

    Rheem and AO Smith cover nearly every size and fuel, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. Both are solid, and the pick usually depends on what fits the space, the warranty length you want and what the supply house has in stock. We lay out the options.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Columbus, OH

    Ruud and Rheem come out of the same company and share many parts. We install both, and because parts overlap, a thermostat or element is easy to find when repairing a newer tank beats replacing it. We will say when repair works.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Columbus

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Columbus

    Ohio has a lot of older housing, and many homes still have galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains that are wearing out.
