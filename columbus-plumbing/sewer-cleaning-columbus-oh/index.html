    What Our Columbus, OH Sewer Techs Handle

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Columbus

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Columbus

    No cleanout means pulling a toilet. That adds time. A two-way cleanout by the foundation gives us access toward the house and toward the street, so future service is quicker, and the work is done to local code with any required permit.

    Video Inspection Before Cleaning in Columbus, OH

    Some lines need a look first. Fragile pipe gets the camera before the cutter. Seeing the pipe first lets us pick the cheapest method that will work, and avoid damaging a line that is already failing.

    Apartment and Strip Center Sewers in Columbus, OH

    Shared laterals carry heavy loads. Several units, one pipe. We set up jetting on a schedule that fits your pump-out cycle and do the work when the building is quiet, with a written report and a video file on request.

    Jetting a Sewer Line in Columbus, OH

    Grease and scale are jetting work. A cable only scratches them. A jetter nozzle with rear-facing jets pulls itself down the line and blasts the buildup off the pipe wall, restoring close to the full diameter of the pipe and slowing the next clog.

    Tree Root Removal in Columbus, OH Sewers

    Roots get in through tiny gaps. Then they fill the pipe. A root saw on a sectional cable cuts the mass loose so we can pull it out through the cleanout, and a jetter can follow to flush out the fine root hairs left on the pipe wall, and a camera shows where they are getting in.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Columbus, OH

    History predicts the next backup. Repeat lines get a schedule. Homes with past root problems, older cast iron or a lot of kitchen grease do well on a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the growth or buildup closes the line.
