    What Our Gas Water Heater Repairs Cover in Canton, OH

    Pilot Light Repair in Canton

    The thermocouple is the first thing we test when a pilot will not stay lit. A millivolt meter tells us in about a minute whether it is producing enough current. If it reads fine, the next suspects are a clogged pilot orifice, a cracked piezo wire or a thermal cutoff that tripped because the combustion chamber overheated from blocked air. Figure on roughly an hour for most pilot repairs.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Canton

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. The usual failures are the blower motor, the pressure switch and the hot surface igniter. Condensate pooling in a vent with the wrong slope can also trip the pressure switch, so we check the pipe run all the way to the termination before we sign off on a part. We read the error code first. Every suspect part then gets tested with a meter.

    Repairing Older Gas Water Heaters in Canton

    Older tanks with standing pilots and no vapor sensor are still out there in Canton, mostly in basements and utility closets. Parts for them are simpler, and a universal thermocouple fits most. We can keep a pre-2003 heater running when the tank is sound, but we also check the draft hood, the flue connector and the gas cock, since those fittings on an old install are often original and corroded. Some old setups lack a sediment trap. We add one during the repair.

    Repair or Replace: Straight Answers in Canton, OH

    Before any wrench turns, you get a written price for the repair and an honest read on the tank in your Canton, OH home. A bad thermocouple on a six-year-old tank is an easy repair to justify. We will tell you which side of that line you are on. You make the call.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Canton

    Rumbling or popping from the bottom of the tank is water boiling under a layer of sediment. Hard water speeds that up, and so does a tank that has never been flushed. We drain the tank through the bottom valve, stir up the settled scale with short bursts of cold water and keep going until it runs clear, which on a neglected tank can take a while. A high whistle is different. A relief valve or a half-closed supply valve is the usual cause.

    Burner Assembly and Venting Repair in Canton

    Rust scale on the burner tray is a sign the flue baffle or the tank top is shedding. We pull the burner, clear the ports and look at the flame arrestor screen underneath. Then the vent gets checked from the draft hood to the roof, because a flue that is loose, sagging or capped by a bird nest can push carbon monoxide back into your Canton home. We hold a smoke pencil at the draft hood to see which way the air moves.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Canton, OH

    Yellow flame tips, soot on the burner door or a roaring sound at startup all mean the burner is not getting clean air. The burner comes out, the ports get brushed and the orifice gets cleared. We also confirm the orifice is drilled for the fuel you have, because a natural gas orifice on a propane heater, or the reverse, will soot up the flue and push carbon monoxide levels well past normal. You want a steady blue flame. Rust flakes on the burner send us to check the flue baffle next.
