    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Canton

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat. We carry the common parts. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Canton, OH

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. Cleaning often works. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Commercial Shower Repair in Canton, OH

    Gyms and apartment buildings run showers dozens of times a day. Commercial valves get heavy-duty cartridges, and we schedule around your busy hours so the showers stay open when people need them. We stock tamper-resistant heads. Every repair is set to scald-limit code.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Canton, OH

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Moen Posi-Temp showers take the 1222, and Delta Monitor 13 and 14 showers take the RP46074, and both wear out on a fairly regular clock. We stock both. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Canton, OH

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Canton

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. We fix the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in Canton

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action. Shut the main valve, catch the drip in a bucket and keep people out from under any sagging drywall, since a soaked ceiling can let go without warning. Then give us a call.     Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Canton, OH

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.
