    What Goes Into an Ejector System in Canton, OH

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Canton

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. Older cities with combined sewers can back up during big storms, so a backwater valve can protect a finished basement. Battery backup is an option.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Canton

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Canton, OH

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Redundancy matters here.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Canton, OH

    Businesses in Canton, OH with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. Commercial setups often run duplex pumps with an alternating panel, so one pump can fail and the other keeps the restrooms open. Downtime costs money. We size both pumps to carry the full load.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Canton, OH

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin costs the least, often done in a few hours. A full new system with basin, floor excavation, roof vent, electrical and alarm costs more, but it is usually the only way to add a below-sewer bath besides a macerator. We price both options.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Canton, OH

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Matched packages speed up the install, and Liberty's float design is less likely to snag on the basin wall, a common reason older pumps fail to start. Stuck floats are common.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Canton

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Ejector Pump Replacement in Canton

    Ejector pumps usually last 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Watch for constant running, a hum or grind, repeated alarms and odor at the pit, since any of them means trouble is close. Old switches fail first.
