    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Canton, OH

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Canton

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Then the object comes out with pliers. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Canton

    If a disposal is completely dead, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. We check the reset button, breaker, wall switch and wiring, and we also test air switches and batch-feed lids that use a magnet to start the motor. Most are simple fixes.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Canton

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Bad bearings mean replacement.

    Leaking Disposal Fix in Canton, OH

    When the drip starts at the top, the putty under the sink flange is usually dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. Most top leaks stop there. Side leaks are usually the discharge gasket or the dishwasher hose clamp.

    After-Hours Disposal Service in Canton

    A dead disposal during dinner prep is a real problem, especially if the sink is full of water. Skip the drain chemicals. Chemical openers sitting in a disposal can damage the seals and splash back when the plumber opens the trap, so it is better to leave the water and call us.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Canton, OH

    Good grinding but slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We pull the trap, clear the tube and the arm with the right size cable, and check that the arm has at least a quarter inch of fall per foot toward the wall.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Canton

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Canton, OH

    InSinkErator, Waste King and Moen each use different mounts, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. Common kits ride on our trucks. It means most repairs need only one trip.
