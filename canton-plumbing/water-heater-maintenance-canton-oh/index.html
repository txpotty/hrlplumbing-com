    Descaling Tankless Units in Canton

    Error codes often mean scale. Tankless heaters are especially sensitive to hard water, and once scale coats the heat exchanger, flow drops, the unit overheats and the control board shuts it down to protect itself. We descale through the service valves. Yearly descaling is standard advice for hard water.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Canton, OH

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. We flush it with descaler. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.

    Thermostat, Element and Burner Checks in Canton

    Wrong temperature means a part is off. Electric units get thermostat and element tests, and gas units get burner, pilot and control checks. A long heating season keeps water heaters and boilers working hard, so yearly maintenance helps avoid midwinter breakdowns. Faulty parts waste energy. They also cut your hot water short.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Canton, OH

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. We check for gas leaks at the valve and connector, watch the flame, clean the burner and confirm the flue pulls exhaust up and out instead of spilling it into the room. Carbon monoxide is the concern. The thermocouple or flame sensor gets checked as well.
