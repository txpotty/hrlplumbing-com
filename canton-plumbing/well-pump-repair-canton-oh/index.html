    Jet Pump System Repair in Canton, OH

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. HRL Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Control Box & Electrical Repair in Canton, OH

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. Breaker trips usually come first. With the power locked out, we test and replace the failed parts and tighten every terminal. Wiring meets Ohio code when the job is done.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Canton, OH

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. We carry the common Franklin parts. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Canton, OH

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. Fast cycling kills pumps young. The air charge gets checked and the bladder inspected, then the tank is recharged or replaced so the pump runs a full minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.
