    Why Choose HRL Plumbing for Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Canton, OH?

    Digging a sewer the old way means a trench from the house to the street, and every foot of it gets backfilled, compacted and resodded afterward. Trenchless skips most of that work. We dig one or two pits about three feet square, or none if the cleanout is usable. Less to fix later.

    • Jointless Pipe, A cured liner has no joints, so roots and ground water have nowhere to get in.
    • Long Service Life, Liners and HDPE pipe carry a 50-year design life when installed right.

    Pipe Bursting in Canton, OH

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. The new HDPE pipe is butt-fused into one length first. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. A 60-foot pull often takes under an hour.

    Sectional Point Repair in Canton

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. It rides in on an inflatable packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. It fixes a single crack or root entry, not a whole failing line. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.
