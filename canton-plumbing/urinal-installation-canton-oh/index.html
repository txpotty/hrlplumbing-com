    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Canton

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Each brand uses its own cartridge, and in busy restrooms it may need changing every few months. We set up a schedule with you. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Canton

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Canton
