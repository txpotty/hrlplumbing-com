    Sewer Line Replacement Methods in Canton, OH

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Canton

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. The inspector looks at fall, cleanouts and material before backfill. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and only bury pipe rated for it. Paperwork stays on file.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Canton, OH

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture. A hairline crack is enough. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease, and a root cutter clears them for a season at most, because the opening is still there. New PVC with glued joints gives them no way in.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Canton, OH

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. Lining patches the inside of the old one. We use bursting when the old line is broken or undersized, since it puts a new pipe in place instead of reinforcing the old, and lining when the pipe is still round and mostly intact. Each has its place.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Canton

    Sewer pipe under a slab is the hardest kind to reach. There are two options. We can cut and remove a strip of floor inside the house and patch it after, or dig a tunnel beneath the foundation from the yard, and the right choice depends on the length of the run, the flooring, and what sits above the pipe.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Canton, OH

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. One sits at the house, the other at the far end of the run. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, so the new line takes the same path without a trench. Driveways stay put.

    Residential Sewer Replacement in Canton, OH

    Most house laterals run 30 to 100 feet. Depth varies a lot. We measure both before quoting, check whether the run passes under a driveway, porch or tree, then pick trench or trenchless based on what is in the way, so the estimate you sign is the price you pay.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Canton, OH

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Every clog then means pulling a toilet. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, bringing a capped riser up to grade where you can reach it, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Canton

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, but not forever. Over decades it scales up inside and the bottom wears through. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.
