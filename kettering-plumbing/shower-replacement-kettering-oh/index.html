    Walk-In Shower Conversion in Kettering, OH

    Shower Pan Replacement in Kettering, OH

    Cracked pans damage subfloors, walls and nearby rooms. On slab homes, water can travel under the flooring and show up as staining or odors in a hallway, so we trace it before we start tearing anything out. Then we remove the pan. The new pan gets proper slope, waterproofing and a flood test.

    Old Shower Valve Replacement in Kettering

    Two- and three-handle valves from decades back often leak and lack scald protection. We open the wall from the back side when we can, remove the old valve and install a pressure-balance or thermostatic valve, then cover the old opening with an oversized trim plate. Usually no new tile. Safety improves a lot.

    Custom Shower Replacement in Kettering, OH

    Built-in benches and niches change the plumbing plan. Benches move the ideal valve and handheld spots, and niches must avoid the supply lines, so we plan together before framing. More outlets need larger supplies. We size those to your water pressure.

    Shower Unit Replacement in Kettering

    Cracked fiberglass units leak at the base first. The crack usually starts where the base was never fully supported, flexing a little every shower until the gel coat splits and water gets under the unit into the subfloor. We pull the old unit. The subfloor gets checked and the new base goes on mortar.

    Tub-to-Shower Replacement in Kettering

    Old bathtubs that nobody uses take up the best spot in the bathroom. We remove the tub, reroute the drain to the new pan location and install the valve at shower height, then set walls and a door or glass panel. No more tub wall. It is safer for everyone.

    Custom Tile Shower Replacement in Kettering, OH

    Custom tile replacement starts with careful demolition. We take the old tile and pan down to the studs and subfloor, replace any damaged wood and install a new waterproofed pan and walls, then set the valve and drain to the finished tile depth. Slope matters most. A flood test comes before tile.
