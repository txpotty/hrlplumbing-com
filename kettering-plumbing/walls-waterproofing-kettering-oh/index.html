    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Kettering, OH

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall.     Basement Wall Sealing in Kettering, OH

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Cold winters and hot, humid summers give Ohio plumbing a wide range of seasonal stress from one year to the next. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Kettering, OH

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. We flood test the new pan before any tile goes down.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Kettering, OH

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst.     Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Kettering
