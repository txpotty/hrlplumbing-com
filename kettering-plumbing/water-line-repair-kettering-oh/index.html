    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Kettering, OH

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. By the time one leaks, the rest of the pipe is close behind. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. That fixes the pressure too.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Kettering

    Underground leaks hide well. Many people find out from the bill before they see anything in the yard. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or a spot near the walk that stays muddy days after rain. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. We dig where the noise is loudest.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Kettering, OH

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patching that kind of pipe tends to buy months, not years. We run new Type K copper or PEX from the meter to the house, usually in one day with a trench or a trenchless pull. We pull the permit, set up the inspection and pressure test the new line before it is covered.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Kettering, OH

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Repeated leaks on galvanized pipe call for a new line. Age matters too, since most service lines past 40 years old are living on borrowed time. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. You see the cut-out section yourself.
