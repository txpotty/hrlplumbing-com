    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Replacement in Kettering

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. Over time it softens, goes oval and blisters on the inside, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Vitrified clay that still holds shape lines well.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Kettering, OH

    Depth changes the math. A shallow line in open lawn is cheap to dig, but a lateral eight feet deep that runs under the driveway and a porch costs far more to excavate than to burst or line from two pits. You get both prices on paper. Then you pick.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Lining Systems in Kettering

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both are built to ASTM standards for cured-in-place pipe. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. We clean to bare pipe first.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Kettering, OH

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. The crew saturates a felt tube with two-part epoxy on a roller table at the truck, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Ambient cure takes a few hours, and hot water or steam shortens it. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Upgrades in Kettering

    Bursting can upsize the line. We can pull in 6-inch HDPE behind a head that bursts an old 4-inch clay pipe, which helps on lines that clog because they were undersized from the start or have been added onto with extra bathrooms. Each end only needs a pit around three by four feet. No trench in between.

    Sectional Point Repair in Kettering

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. We push it in on a packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. It fixes a single crack or root entry, not a whole failing line. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Lining in Kettering, OH

    Office and retail buildings often have a cast iron building drain under the slab that has rotted along the bottom. Lining it avoids cutting the floor. Work gets scheduled around tenant hours, nights included. A typical commercial lining job runs one to three days, depending on footage and how many branch connections need to be reinstated with a cutter.

    Pipe bursting uses high-density polyethylene, HDPE, fused into one continuous length with no joints. For lining, it is a felt or fiberglass tube saturated with epoxy resin.
