    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Kettering

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings.     Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house.
