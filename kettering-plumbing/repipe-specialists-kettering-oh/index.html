    PEX Pipe Replacement in Kettering, OH

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Kettering

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Kettering

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it.     PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Kettering, OH

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. Copper commonly costs 20 to 40 percent more than PEX, and some owners prefer it anyway for its long history. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Kettering

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. Our crews use Uponor or similar PEX-A, where the pipe end gets expanded and shrinks back over the fitting for a full-diameter connection. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.
