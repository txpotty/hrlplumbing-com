    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Kettering

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Kettering, OH

    Upgrading an old ejector can often keep the existing pit. With a sound basin, a new pump, fresh check valve, shutoff and alarm give you most of a new system for far less. We tell you if the basin is done.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Kettering, OH

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin is the least expensive option, often done in a few hours. A full new system with basin, floor excavation, roof vent, electrical and alarm costs more, but it is usually the only way to add a below-sewer bath besides a macerator. Ask for both numbers.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Kettering, OH

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements.     Grinder Pump Installation in Kettering

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. Grinders run pricier. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.

    Emergency Ejector Pump Replacement in Kettering, OH

    A dead ejector pump takes every below-grade fixture out of service until it is fixed. Stop using those fixtures, including the washer if it drains to the pit, then call (740) 449-7505 and we aim to have a plumber and pump at your door the same day. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Kettering, OH

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Two pumps, one alarm.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Kettering

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.
