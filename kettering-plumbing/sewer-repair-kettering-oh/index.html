    What Sewer Repair Covers in Kettering, OH

    Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in Kettering, OH

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. The whole house loses its drains. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and we locate the break and its depth before we start digging. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Kettering, OH

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. The joint is the real problem. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Kettering, OH

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. Downtime costs money. For restaurants, strip centers and apartment buildings in Kettering we schedule repairs after hours where we can, set up temporary bypass pumping when the line has to stay in service, and coordinate with property managers on tenant notices. We repair a lot of grease-clogged outlet lines.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Kettering

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Kettering, OH

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. A lush stripe over the line is another clue. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and locate it on the surface to keep the excavation tight. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, so nothing in the yard gets disturbed. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Kettering

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Kettering

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Kettering, OH

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. Not every line qualifies, and we will say so.
