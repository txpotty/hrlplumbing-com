    What Our Kettering, OH Sewer Techs Handle

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Kettering

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Kettering

    Household sewers clog slowly. Grease, hair and wipes build up over months. Debris catches at a rough joint or a sag, and every load of laundry adds to it until the line closes, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Kettering, OH

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. A crack stays a crack. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.

    Video Inspection Before Cleaning in Kettering, OH

    Some lines need a look first. Fragile pipe gets the camera before the cutter. Seeing the pipe first lets us pick the cheapest method that will work, and avoid damaging a line that is already failing.
