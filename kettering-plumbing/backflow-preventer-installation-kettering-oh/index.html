    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Kettering, OH

    Commercial kitchens and chemical injection systems need an RPZ.     Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Kettering

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. When pressure between the checks gets too close to supply pressure, the relief opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Kettering. Brands include Watts and Febco.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Kettering, OH

    High-hazard connections in Kettering, OH call for a reduced pressure zone assembly. That means chemical feed systems and medical equipment, where a backflow event could put something toxic in the supply, so the RPZ adds a relief valve between two checks that dumps water if either check leaks. It has to drain somewhere.
