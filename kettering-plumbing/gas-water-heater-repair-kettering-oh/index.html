    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Kettering, OH

    Lukewarm showers and a tank that never catches up often trace back to the gas control. We check the thermostat setting, the immersion sensor and the valve's outlet pressure with a manometer. On most residential tanks the thermostat lives inside the gas valve body, so a bad sensor or a stuck magnet usually means replacing the whole control rather than one small part. Plan on roughly an hour for the swap. Every joint on the new valve gets leak tested.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Kettering, OH

    Atmospheric tanks rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue with an upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot. Sections that pull apart, rust through or sag let combustion gas spill into the room. The vent gets repaired. Each joint gets three screws, the connector gets proper clearance from wood and drywall, and we run the heater for several minutes with a smoke test at the draft hood to watch the exhaust go up. Spillage gets fixed before we leave.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Kettering

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. Air enters through a flame arrestor screen at the base that also keeps flame inside. When lint, dust or pet hair coats that screen, the burner starves, the chamber overheats and a thermal switch shuts the whole heater down, sometimes several times a week. The screen and the chamber both get cleaned. We replace any tripped thermal switch.

    Pilot Light Repair in Kettering

    The thermocouple is the first thing we test when a pilot will not stay lit. A millivolt meter tells us in about a minute whether it is producing enough current. If it reads fine, the next suspects are a clogged pilot orifice, a cracked piezo wire or a thermal cutoff that tripped because the combustion chamber overheated from blocked air. Figure on roughly an hour for most pilot repairs.

    Repair or Replace: Straight Answers in Kettering, OH

    Before any wrench turns, you get a written price for the repair and an honest read on the tank in your Kettering, OH home. A six-year-old heater with a bad thermocouple is worth fixing. A fourteen-year-old tank with a rusty flue, a leaking bottom and a failed gas valve is a different story, and putting a $500 control on it rarely makes sense. You will hear plainly which side of that line your heater falls on. The decision is yours.

    Gas Water Heater Code Checks in Kettering, OH

    Fuel gas code covers more of a water heater repair than most people expect. The tech looks for a drip leg, a listed flex connector and an accessible shutoff valve. Garage installs in Kettering, OH need the burner raised 18 inches off the floor unless the heater is listed as vapor resistant, and strapping goes on wherever the local code asks for it. Clearances get measured too.

    Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Kettering

    A pilot that lights, holds for ten seconds and then goes out is almost always a thermocouple or a weak magnet in the gas valve. No flame at all usually means a plugged pilot orifice or a bad piezo. Main burner problems show up another way. The pilot stays lit but the main flame never comes on, which points to the thermostat side of the valve, a tripped thermal cutoff or a flame arrestor so clogged with lint that the burner starves for air. We test each piece in that order.
