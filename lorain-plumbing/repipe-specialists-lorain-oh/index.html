    Repiping Services We Handle in Lorain, OH

    PEX Repiping in Lorain, OH

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. Our PEX-A installs use expansion fittings that do not shrink the flow path. That keeps pressure up at the far fixtures.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Lorain

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. We count fixtures, check attic and crawlspace access, look at where the main comes in and ask about plans for future remodels before we draw a route. Then we size the lines. A three-bath house with a tankless heater may need 1-inch main trunk lines to keep showers steady when the washer kicks on.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Lorain, OH

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Weak flow and rusty water are common. Old galvanized gets removed or abandoned and replaced with PEX or copper, and most homes see better pressure and clearer water right away.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Lorain

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved. Our office works with your HOA on approvals and shutoff times, and we take steps to protect the units next door. Building-wide jobs go in phases. Big buildings get repiped by stack or wing, which lets residents stay home.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Lorain

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. Rerouting one branch overhead and leaving the bad pipe in the slab is often cheaper than both a repipe and a jackhammer repair. It suits a first-time leak. If the house has had several leaks, we will walk through why a full repipe usually costs less over time.

    Commercial Repipe Services in Lorain

    Older commercial buildings in Lorain often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code. We replace supply risers, branch lines and drains while keeping offices, shops or apartments running as much as possible. Tenants get notice of every shutoff. We plan the work by zone and handle keys, access and quiet hours with your manager.

    Drain Line Repiping in Lorain

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. We camera the lines first. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Lorain

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. New PEX or copper stops the rust, restores pressure and ends leaks at the threads, which is where galvanized fails first. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Lorain

    Most PEX repipes for Ohio homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, based on size, bathroom count and access. Copper typically adds 20 to 40 percent.

    PEX resists scale and pinhole corrosion and handles expansion well, which makes it a strong choice for hard water. Copper lasts but is pricier and can pinhole in corrosive water.

    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A with ProPEX fittings and Type L copper from domestic mills. Copper jobs often get Viega ProPress fittings.

    Most whole-home repipes take three to five days, including patching. Bigger homes take longer. Water goes off during the work day and comes back on each evening, so you can still shower and cook while the job is underway.

    Both work well, and the best choice depends on your house and your water. PEX is cheaper. PEX bends, resists scale and handles freezes better, and copper is rigid, UV-tolerant and proven but pricier and vulnerable to aggressive water.

    Copper is rigid metal pipe with a long track record. PEX bends instead. PEX costs less, installs faster, handles freezing better and runs quietly, while copper tolerates sunlight and heat better and appeals to owners who want metal pipe, so the right pick depends on your house.

    We provide a written workmanship warranty, and PEX makers such as Uponor offer long-term warranties when certified installers use their system. You get the details before we start.

    Having the repipe documented with a permit can make your home easier to sell and insure, especially when replacing polybutylene or Kitec. Buyers and inspectors notice it.
