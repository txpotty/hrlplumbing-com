    Manufacturer rules for PEX are specific: horizontal supports roughly every 32 inches, a minimum bend radius, and no long sun exposure. We follow them on every run. Skipping them leads to kinks, sagging lines and fittings that strain, and it can void the pipe warranty the manufacturer would otherwise honor.

    What Goes Into a PEX Install in Lorain, OH

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Lorain

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Lorain

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures. In an older house that usually means rust-choked galvanized or polybutylene with tired fittings, and a new faucet cartridge will not touch it. A whole-home repipe will. Every line from the main valve to each angle stop gets replaced, and the system is tested before anything gets closed up.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Lorain, OH

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to swap out the old pipe. While the studs are exposed we can run PEX back to a manifold or a sound trunk, set fresh stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a fridge line. Additions get their own sizing. A far-end bathroom may need a three-quarter inch feed to hold pressure.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Lorain, OH

    Think of a manifold as a breaker panel for water. Each fixture gets its own line from a central header, and each line gets its own valve, so you can shut off one toilet without killing water to the kitchen. It uses more pipe. What you get back is very few hidden fittings and steadier pressure when two people shower at the same time.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Lorain, OH

    A single bad section does not always mean a repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two. That works for one leak. By the third leak, though, we will talk to you honestly about whether the rest of that copper is close behind.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Lorain, OH

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Lorain, OH

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. We let joints set before testing.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Lorain

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. After 40 or 50 years the bore is so choked with scale that a half-inch line delivers a trickle, and the water comes out tinted brown after it sits overnight. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. Pressure comes back at the fixtures and the rust stops.
