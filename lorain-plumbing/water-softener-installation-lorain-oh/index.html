    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Lorain, OH

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HRL Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Water Softener Replacement in Lorain, OH

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Metered valves save salt. Performance comes back right away.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Lorain, OH

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. If you bought a Pelican system for your home, we install it with secure connections, a proper bypass and the startup steps the manual lists for flushing the media. Skipping the flush causes trouble. We follow it step by step.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Lorain

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron adds to the setting. You get a walkthrough of the settings.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Lorain

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. That pays back over time. We install several models.
