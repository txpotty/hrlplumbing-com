    What Our Commercial Plumbers Do in Lorain, OH

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Lorain, OH

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. We check draft and combustion, test water temperature at the far end of the building and flush the tank, then tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense. Tankless units get a descaling flush. If the building has a recirc loop, we test the pump and check valve too.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Lorain

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. Our certified testers check reduced pressure zone assemblies, double check valves and pressure vacuum breakers with calibrated differential gauges, then file the report with your water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly. That is why the truck carries Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits.

    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Lorain, OH

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property. We camera the line, locate the problem depth with a sonde and then decide between a spot repair, pipe lining or pipe bursting. Trenchless saves parking lots. Lining a 6-inch lateral can often be done in a day without tearing up asphalt or closing an entrance.

    Commercial Fixture and Faucet Repair in Lorain, OH

    Commercial faucets and toilets are built heavy, but they still wear. Cartridges leak. Sensor and metering faucets, wall-hung toilets, mop sinks and compartment sinks are routine for us, with Sloan, Chicago Faucets, Zurn and T and S Brass parts on hand. Low-flow aerators cut water use without slowing hand washing. A typical fixture repair takes an hour or two.

    Grease Trap and Interceptor Repair in Lorain, OH

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. We repair broken baffles, replace cracked lids and outlet tees, jet the inlet lines and confirm the unit is sized for your fixture count and flow rate. Slow sinks and overflow usually point to a full or damaged trap. Keeping the pumping log current helps at inspection time.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Lorain, OH

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. Our crew checks the baffles and tees, replaces what is broken and jets the feed lines to strip hardened grease a cable cannot touch. Regular jetting means fewer backups during the dinner rush.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Lorain, OH

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. Running flush valves, stuck urinals and dripping sensor faucets usually need a diaphragm, a solenoid or fresh batteries. We stock those. Clogged wall-hung toilets sometimes need the carrier checked, because a loose bowl on a carrier can leak behind the wall. Low pressure on one fixture usually points to a clogged supply stop.

    Grease Trap Installation in Lorain
