    Anode Rod Checks in Lorain

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. We pull it and look. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Lorain, OH

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. Leak checks, flame inspection, burner cleaning and a draft test are part of every gas visit. Carbon monoxide is the concern. We also test the thermocouple.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Lorain

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment buries the lower element until it overheats and fails, leaving you with a short supply of hot water. Both elements get tested. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Electric Element Service in Lorain, OH

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. Power gets shut off first. Good parts stay in.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Lorain, OH

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. We swap bad ones. We check the expansion tank too.
