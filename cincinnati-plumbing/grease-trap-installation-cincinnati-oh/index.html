    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Cincinnati, OH

    Sizing starts with fixture drain rates and ends with where the trap can physically go. Long runs to the trap let grease cool and coat the line, so we keep the trap as close to the fixtures as we can. Closer is better.     Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Cincinnati, OH

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. You can put one under a sink, beside a dish station or in a pit in the slab. The flow control is not optional. Skip it and a full sink drain overruns the rating, which sends grease right past the baffles. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Cincinnati

    Outdoor gravity interceptors run from 750 gallons to well past 2,000, and setting one means an excavator on site and a traffic-rated lid if it sits in the drive lane. The inlet invert has to sit a couple of inches above the outlet, and we shoot both with a laser level before backfill.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Cincinnati

We size by fixture count and retention time, then match the tank to what the local pretreatment office for your building will accept. No moving parts to fail.
