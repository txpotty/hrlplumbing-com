    Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Cincinnati

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. It finds rebar and cables. Combined with a listening device, radar finds leaks in any pipe type, even with traffic noise, since radar maps the route and the mic confirms the spot. We use both in Cincinnati.

    Thermal and Acoustic Tools in Cincinnati

    A thermal camera shows the warm streak a hot water leak leaves on the floor. Cold leaks need the mic. On cold lines we rely on an acoustic sensor that listens through the slab, marking the point where the sound is loudest. Both tools ride on every Cincinnati truck.

    Residential Slab Leak Repair in Cincinnati
