    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Cincinnati

    Sizing a home unit depends on two figures: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. With a shower at about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen tap a bit under, two showers and a sink together need around 5 or 6 gallons per minute at the needed rise. That narrows the model list fast.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Cincinnati

    Propane homes in Cincinnati are good candidates for tankless, using a model built or converted for propane. Propane runs at a higher pressure than natural gas, so the regulator, gas line sizing and the unit's gas valve all have to match, and the propane supplier may need to change the regulator at the tank. Tank size matters too.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Cincinnati, OH

    Businesses in Cincinnati, OH with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. If one unit goes down, the others keep running. Peak demand sets the rack size. Expect a bigger gas line and a mechanical permit on commercial jobs.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Cincinnati

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job since the tank's location may not suit the new unit.     Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Cincinnati
