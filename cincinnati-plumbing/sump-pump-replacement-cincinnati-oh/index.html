    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Cincinnati

    Horsepower matters less than people think. For most houses a 1/3 HP pump is enough, provided the water only has to climb 10 to 15 feet to get out. Before we choose, we read the pump curve at your real discharge height. A pump that is too big empties the pit too fast and wears itself out.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Cincinnati, OH

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They suit pits too tight for two separate pumps. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.

    Wayne Sump Pump Swaps in Cincinnati

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. The usual failure is a burned-out motor or a float that hangs up on the cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Takes about two hours.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Cincinnati, OH

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We swap in a corrosion-resistant pump and clear out leaves and silt. Discharge goes away from the foundation. Test them each spring.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Cincinnati

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. Pumps and oil-sensing panels get replaced to code. Two alternating pumps on one controller mean one can fail without flooding the pit. Some insurance carriers ask for test records.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Cincinnati

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. Out comes the old pump, out comes the muck at the bottom of the basin, and the new one goes in on a brick to stay clear of silt. Then a new check valve goes on. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Cincinnati

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Most swaps take one to two hours. We also check that the float has room to travel without touching the basin wall.
