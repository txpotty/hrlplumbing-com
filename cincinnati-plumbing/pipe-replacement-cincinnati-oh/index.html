    What Goes Into a Repipe in Cincinnati, OH

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Cincinnati, OH

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Pinholes start showing up. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, and fixing awkward runs from past remodels. Better flow.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Cincinnati, OH

    Pinhole leaks start small. First a damp spot, then drywall damage. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, and when one shows up, more are often on the way along the same lines. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Cincinnati

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. Homeowners around Cincinnati often install a softener to protect water heaters and reduce spotting on fixtures. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Cincinnati, OH

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.

    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Cincinnati, OH

    A single bad spot can usually be fixed. Several leaks in different rooms, rusty water throughout, or pipe that crumbles when we cut it means the rest is close behind. We show you what we find.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in Cincinnati, OH

    A burst pipe needs fast action. Close the main shutoff right away. Then call, because water spreading through walls and floors does damage every minute, and our crews aim to stop it fast. Drying comes next.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Cincinnati, OH

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Water chemistry plays a role. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, including fittings near the heater. Look at the joints.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Cincinnati

    Water under a slab shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water, or a meter that spins with everything off. Finding the exact spot comes first. Then you have a choice between breaking the slab to fix that spot or rerouting the line through walls or the attic, and rerouting often costs less and avoids future slab leaks on that line. We explain both.
