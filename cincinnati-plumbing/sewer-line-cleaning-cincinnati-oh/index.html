    Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Compared to Replacement in Cincinnati, OH

    Cleaning costs a few hundred dollars. Replacement costs thousands. If the pipe is structurally sound, regular cleaning is often the better value, but if the camera shows a collapse or a long belly, cleaning only buys time, and you see both prices.

    Sewer Line Cleaning for Rentals and HOAs in Cincinnati

    Rentals see more clogs from wipes and grease. Turnover adds to it. Landlords and HOAs can set a cleaning schedule, with records for each building to help with planning, coordinating access with tenants.

    Pre-Purchase Sewer Line Cleaning and Inspection in Cincinnati, OH

    Most home inspections skip the sewer line. It can be costly. We clean the line first so the camera can see the pipe walls clearly, then record the full length and provide a written report that buyers can use to negotiate repairs or credits.

    Tree Root Removal from Sewer Lines in Cincinnati

    Roots enter through small cracks and loose joints. Tree roots and shifting clay often break into older clay or cast iron sewer laterals, leading to slow drains and backups. They grow fast inside. Root saws on a sectional cable cut the mass loose, and a jetter follows to remove fine root hairs and bring the pipe back to its full diameter, and we explain options to keep them out.
