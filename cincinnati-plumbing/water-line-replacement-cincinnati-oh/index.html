    Copper Water Line Installation in Cincinnati

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. It handles high street pressure without complaint. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years eventually wear a hole.     Emergency Water Line Replacement in Cincinnati

    Water bubbling up through the lawn or the street side of the sidewalk means a break, not a drip. Close the main shutoff at the house first.     PEX Water Line Installation in Cincinnati, OH

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. During deep cold snaps, homeowners around Cincinnati can let faucets drip and open sink cabinets to keep warm air on the pipes. PEX can swell a little in a freeze and go back to shape, where copper splits. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and we keep it out of direct sun at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Cincinnati, OH

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Plumbing code caps house pressure at 80 psi. If your new line comes in above that, we add a pressure reducing valve right after the main shutoff, set it around 55 to 65 psi, and swap the old gate valve for a full-port ball valve. A quarter turn and the water is off.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Cincinnati, OH
