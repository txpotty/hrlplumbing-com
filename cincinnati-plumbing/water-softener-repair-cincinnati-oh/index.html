    Resin Bed Replacement in Cincinnati

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. HRL Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Cincinnati, OH

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Keep the salt about half full.

    Commercial Water Softener Repair in Cincinnati, OH

    Laundromats and restaurants in Cincinnati, OH run through soft water fast, and a dishwasher or boiler starts scaling the moment the softener stops working. Downtime costs money.     Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Cincinnati

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. We retest hardness and reprogram the valve, or upgrade the head to a metered control that only regenerates when the resin is actually close to exhausted. Salt bills drop.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Cincinnati, OH

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.
