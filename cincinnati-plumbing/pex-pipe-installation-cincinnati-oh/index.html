    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Cincinnati

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house.     PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Cincinnati, OH

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. Every one is listed for potable water.
