    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first.     Clogged Drain Repair in Cincinnati

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Repeat clogs are a warning. We clear the line with a drum auger or a jetter, then run a camera if the clog returns within a few weeks or sits more than a few feet past the trap, which shows whether you have buildup, a sag or a broken fitting.     Preventive Drain Maintenance in Cincinnati

    Scheduled cleaning keeps buildup from turning into a repair. Most homes need it yearly.     Main Line Drain Repair in Cincinnati

    Restaurant and Office Drain Repair in Cincinnati

    Restaurants, salons, offices and medical suites all need working drains during business hours. Kitchens clog the most.     Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Cincinnati, OH

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Sometimes it is a bad glue joint. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then replace that section with new pipe joined by shielded couplings that fit the old and new outside diameters. The repair gets a flow test.

    Recurring Clog Repair in Cincinnati

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Cleaning will not stop it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and each one shows up clearly on a camera or when we pull the trap. We repair the part that catches debris.
