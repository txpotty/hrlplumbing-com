    Sump Pits, Pumps and Backups in Cincinnati, OH

    Same-Day Sump Pump Installation in Cincinnati

    Same-day emergency installs cover pumps that quit in the middle of a storm. We remove standing water from the pit first, then set a new pump on a new check valve, plug it into a tested GFCI outlet and watch it run through several cycles before leaving your Cincinnati basement. A backup is the next conversation.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Cincinnati, OH

    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor. That makes them quieter than a pedestal, and in Cincinnati, OH we set them on a couple of bricks so the intake stays above the silt that settles on the bottom of every liner. The cord seal matters.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Cincinnati

    Duplex systems with two pumps and an alternating panel are standard for parking garages and multi-unit buildings with below-grade levels in Cincinnati. Each cycle, the panel switches which pump leads, so wear stays even. Staff get a high water alarm. We coordinate the wiring with your electrician.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Systems in Cincinnati, OH

    Some lots sit in a high water table and fill a standard pit every few minutes during spring. For those, we step up to a 1/2 or 3/4 horsepower unit in a bigger basin, all sized after we time how fast your pit refills. We use a stopwatch.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Cincinnati, OH

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Older cities with combined sewers can back up during big storms, so a backwater valve can safeguard a finished basement. We add a second pump in the same pit with its own float, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. Your primary pump still handles the everyday load.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Cincinnati

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. A battery backup keeps a second pump running without house power, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We test it under load.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Cincinnati

    Older homes often have narrow pits that will not fit a submersible. A pedestal pump fits where nothing else will, and we install it with rigid discharge, a quality check valve, and a float rod set to your pit depth so it starts before water reaches the top of the liner. They are noisy but durable.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Cincinnati, OH

    Pricing starts with whether you already have a pit. A pump swap in an existing basin costs the least, while a new pit adds saw cutting, digging and a concrete patch, and a battery backup adds the second pump and battery on top of that. We itemize every quote.
