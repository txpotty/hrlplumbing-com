    Fixing Showers in Cincinnati, OH

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Cincinnati

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. We swap in a new one. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Cincinnati

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat. We carry the common parts. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Commercial Shower Service in Cincinnati

    Hotels and dorms cannot wait days on a shower. We carry commercial cartridges and thermostatic parts, and we can stage repairs room by room so only one unit is down at a time. We can work nights. We set and test the limit stop on each valve before the next room.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Cincinnati, OH

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not. For a sudden wall leak or a valve that will not close, the main valve near the meter is the first move. Then call us. Our plumber stops the water, fixes it or makes it safe and checks for wet areas.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Cincinnati, OH

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Cincinnati

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. Replacing the cartridge solves it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Cincinnati

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. A new cartridge usually fixes it. If the valve is too old for parts, we talk through a replacement valve and what it means for the tile.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Cincinnati, OH

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. Both ride on our trucks. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.
