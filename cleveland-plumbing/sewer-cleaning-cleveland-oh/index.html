    Video Inspection Before Cleaning in Cleveland, OH

    Some lines need a look first. Old pipe especially. A camera run before cleaning shows whether the line has roots, a broken section or a grease plug, which tells us whether to cable, jet or stop and talk about repair.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Cleveland

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    After-Hours Sewer Backups in Cleveland

    Backups happen at bad times. We answer anyway. The after-hours rate is quoted on the phone before we dispatch, and the tech arrives ready to clear the main, set up a wet vac and photograph the damage for your insurance company.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Cleveland, OH

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.
