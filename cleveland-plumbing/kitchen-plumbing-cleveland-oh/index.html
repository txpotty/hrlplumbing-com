    Kitchen Drain Clearing in Cleveland, OH

    Water rising in the second bowl when the first drains tells us the clog is past the trap, in the branch line inside the wall.     Recurring Kitchen Clogs in Cleveland, OH

    Grease, rice, pasta and coffee grounds stack up in the trap and the drain arm over months, not days.     Kitchen Faucet Repair and Replacement in Cleveland

    InSinkErator Installation in Cleveland, OH

    Dishwasher and Ice Maker Lines in Cleveland

    A dishwasher needs a hot supply with its own shutoff, a drain to the disposal or a wye tailpiece, and either an air gap at the sink or a high loop under the counter, depending on local code.
