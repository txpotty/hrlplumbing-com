    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Cleveland

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Cleveland

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. Our jetters push water at 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle that scours the full pipe wall, which clears 4-inch and 6-inch lines back to bare pipe. Jetting keeps the line open for months instead of days. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.

    Tenant Improvement and Build-Out Plumbing in Cleveland, OH

    New tenants usually need restrooms moved, a break room added or a kitchen built in a shell space. Our crew installs from the plans, works around the framers and electricians and books inspections early so the opening date holds. Old lines get capped. Abandoned drains and supplies are cut back to the main and capped properly, which prevents dead legs and sewer gas later.

    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Cleveland, OH

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property. We camera the line, locate the problem depth with a sonde and then decide between a spot repair, pipe lining or pipe bursting. Trenchless saves parking lots. Lining a 6-inch lateral can often be done in a day without tearing up asphalt or closing an entrance.

    Commercial Fixture and Faucet Repair in Cleveland, OH

    Commercial faucets and toilets are built heavy, but they still wear. Cartridges leak. We repair and replace sensor faucets, metering faucets, wall-hung toilets, mop sinks and three-compartment sinks, and we stock parts for Sloan, Chicago Faucets, Zurn and T and S Brass. Low-flow aerators cut water use without slowing hand washing. A typical fixture repair takes an hour or two.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Cleveland

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We test RPZ, double check and PVB assemblies with calibrated gauges and send the results straight to the water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly. That is why the truck carries Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits.
