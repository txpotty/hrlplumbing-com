    Foundation, Shower and Block Wall Sealing in Cleveland, OH

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Cleveland

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Basements are standard in most homes, and many rely on sump pumps to handle groundwater away during wet periods. Backfill goes in last.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Cleveland, OH

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Ohio has a lot of older housing, and many homes still have galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains that are wearing out. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with interior drain tile. We repoint failed mortar joints before sealing.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Cleveland

    Ohio has a lot of older housing, and many homes still have galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains that are wearing out. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with real hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Not every basement needs one.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Cleveland

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with membranes rated for constant water exposure, and we repair the supply and drain piping behind them if demolition turns up a leak. After-hours scheduling is available.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Cleveland, OH

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and makes sense when a patio or driveway sits against the foundation.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Cleveland, OH

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while replacing drywall, cabinets and flooring again and again costs far more, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill. The price for exterior block sealing depends on how much wall there is. Your quote lists each option with its price.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Cleveland, OH

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, a drop of about six inches in the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. You get the options in writing, side by side.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Cleveland, OH

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan.
